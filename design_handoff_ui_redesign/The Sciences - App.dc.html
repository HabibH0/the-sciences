عَلِمَ وعَرَفَ
عَلِمَ: اليقين بصفة أو حكم

علم as a فعل قلب means to know a quality, judgement, or fact about something. It connects the مبتدأ to its خبر with certainty.

Example
عَلِمْتُ زَيدًا كَرِيمًا
"I knew Zayd to be noble" — زيدًا is مفعول به أول, كريمًا is مفعول به ثان.
عَلِمْتُ زَيدًا كَرِيمًا asserts a certain fact about زيد. What does علم do here, grammatically?
It connects زيدًا (مفعول به أول) to كريمًا (مفعول به ثان), the way مبتدأ connects to خبر
It takes زيدًا as its only object, with كريمًا as حال
عَرَفَ: معرفة الذات لا وصفها

عرف, by contrast, is normally used for recognising the ذات of something — identifying a person or thing distinctly, not predicating a quality about them.

Verb
Typical use
Two objects?
عَلِمَ
knowing a صفة about something
Yes
عَرَفَ
recognising the ذات
Typically one
عَرَفْتُ زيدًا takes only one object, unlike عَلِمْتُ زيدًا كريمًا. Why does عرف typically stay single-object؟
عرف is normally about recognising a ذات, not predicating a صفة, so it doesn't need a second object
دَرَى: العلم بعد جهل

درى means to know, with the added sense of coming to know after not knowing — knowledge acquired through being informed, discovering, or figuring something out.

Careful

درى is never used of Allah's knowledge — درى implies a prior cause or state of not-knowing, and Allah's knowledge has neither.

درى is never used to describe Allah's knowledge, while علم is. What distinction explains that؟
درى implies knowledge acquired after a prior state of not knowing, through some cause; Allah's knowledge has no such prior ignorance
تَعَلَّمْ: بمعنى اعْلَمْ

تعلَّم in certain usages carries the meaning of knowing with certainty, and may take two objects — rare in Quranic Arabic, but found in classical literature.

In تَعَلَّمْ شِفَاءَ النَّفْسِ قَهْرَ عَدُوِّهَا, تعلَّم carries the meaning of اعلم. What are the two objects, respectively؟
مفعول به أول, then مفعول به ثان

What is the main grammatical difference between علم and عرف؟
