# Types of Indirect Inference

**Deduction** applies a universal rule to a particular case. **Induction** moves from observed particulars toward a universal judgement. **Analogy** transfers a judgement from one particular case to another because of a relevant similarity.

## Worked example

Using the rule that every triangle has three sides to judge a new triangle is deduction. Generalising about water from observations is induction. Comparing cocaine with alcohol by their intoxicating effect is analogy.

## Guided check

Identify the direction of each argument: universal→particular, particular→universal, or particular→particular.

## Takeaway

The direction of movement distinguishes the three inference families.
