# Obversion

The **complement** of a term contains everything outside that class: the complement of humans is non-humans. To obvert, keep the subject and quantity, change the quality, and replace the predicate with its complement.

## Worked example

“All birds are animals” becomes “No birds are non-animals.” The mapping is A↔E and I↔O, and all four forms obvert validly.

## Guided check

Obvert “Some Muslims are not Arabs.” Check separately that the quality changed and that the new predicate is the exact complement.

## Takeaway

Obversion changes two things together: quality and predicate complement.
