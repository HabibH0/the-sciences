# Eight Rules of Categorical Syllogisms

Test a syllogism in four groups:

1. **Number:** exactly three unambiguous terms and three propositions.
2. **Distribution:** M is distributed at least once; anything distributed in the conclusion was distributed in its premise.
3. **Negatives:** not two negative premises; one negative premise and a negative conclusion go together.
4. **Particulars:** not two particular premises; a particular premise requires a particular conclusion.

Violations include four terms, undistributed middle, illicit major or minor, and exclusive premises.

## Worked example

If M is undistributed in both premises, the two outer terms have not been securely connected. The conclusion fails even if it happens to be true.

## Guided check

Apply rules 1–8 in order and stop at the first failure, while recording any additional failures for feedback.

## Takeaway

Validity belongs to the argument's structure, not to the accidental truth of its conclusion.
