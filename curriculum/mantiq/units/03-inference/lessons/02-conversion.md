# Conversion

Conversion swaps subject and predicate while retaining the quantifier. E and I convert simply: **No S are P** becomes **No P are S**, and **Some S are P** becomes **Some P are S**.

An A-form converts only by limitation: **All S are P** supports **Some P are S** in the textbook's treatment, not **All P are S**. Simply converting A or O commits illicit conversion. An accidentally true converse does not validate the inference pattern.

## Worked example

“No Muslims are polytheists” validly converts to “No polytheists are Muslims.” “All Muslims are monotheists” does not support “All monotheists are Muslims.”

## Guided check

Identify the original form before swapping terms, then consult the conversion rule for that form.

## Takeaway

E and I convert simply; A converts by limitation; O does not validly convert.
