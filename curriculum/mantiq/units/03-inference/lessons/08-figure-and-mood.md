# Figure and Mood

The **figure** records where M appears in the major and minor premises:

| Figure | Major premise | Minor premise |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | M–P | S–M |
| 2 | P–M | S–M |
| 3 | M–P | M–S |
| 4 | P–M | M–S |

The **mood** records the A/E/I/O form of the major premise, minor premise, and conclusion in that order.

## Worked example

Two A premises and an A conclusion produce mood AAA. If M is subject in the major premise and predicate in the minor, the figure is 1.

## Guided check

Label S, P, and M before naming figure; classify all three propositions before naming mood.

## Takeaway

Figure is term position; mood is proposition form.
