# Subalternation, Contrariety, and Subcontrariety

Under **subalternation**, truth descends A→I and E→O. A and E are **contraries**: they cannot both be true but may both be false. I and O are **subcontraries**: they cannot both be false but may both be true.

## Worked example

If “All Muslims are monotheists” is true, “Some Muslims are monotheists” follows. The word “some” does not imply “some but not all.”

## Guided check

For each square relationship, state what is forbidden: both true, both false, or unequal truth values.

## Takeaway

Do not transfer truth or falsity in a direction the relationship does not license.
