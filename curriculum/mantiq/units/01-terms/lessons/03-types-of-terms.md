# Types of Terms

The textbook divides terms by their scope into **universal** and
**particular**.

## Universal — كُلِّي (*kullī*)

A universal is a word that applies to **all things within a category**.

### Worked example

> all pens

The expression ranges over the whole category of pens, so its scope is
universal.

## Particular — جُزْئِي (*juzʾī*)

A particular is a word that does **not** apply to all things within a category.

### Worked examples

> a pen · some pens · most pens

Each expression selects less than the complete category. Even “most” remains
particular because some members may be left out.

## Guided comparison

Do not decide from the noun alone. “Pens” can occur in either kind of term. The
scope marker decides:

- **all pens** → universal
- **some pens** → particular
