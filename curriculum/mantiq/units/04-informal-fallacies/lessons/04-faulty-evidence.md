# Faulty Evidential Support

A **circular argument** restates or assumes its conclusion in the premise. An **appeal to ignorance** treats lack of disproof as proof, or lack of proof as disproof. An **irrelevant authority** substitutes fame or unrelated expertise for evidence.

## Worked examples

“Drugs are illegal because they are against the law” supplies no independent reason. “Aliens exist because you cannot prove otherwise” shifts the burden of proof. A celebrity endorsement does not establish a technical claim unless the celebrity has relevant expertise and evidence.

## Guided check

Remove the conclusion from the premises. Is independent support left? If a source is cited, identify their exact field and its relevance.

## Takeaway

Good support must be independent, positive, and relevant to the claim.
