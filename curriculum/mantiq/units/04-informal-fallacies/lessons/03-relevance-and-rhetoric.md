# Relevance and Rhetoric

An **ad hominem** attacks the person instead of the position. **Loaded language** uses emotion in place of evidence. A **straw man** distorts the claim and attacks the weaker substitute. A **red herring** diverts attention to a different issue.

## Worked comparison

Replying to a fair-trial claim with “You support terrorists” distorts what was asserted, so it is a straw man. Replying to a speeding ticket by pointing to dangerous criminals distracts from the speeding question, so it is a red herring.

## Guided check

Write the original claim in neutral words. Then ask whether the reply addresses that exact claim with relevant evidence.

## Takeaway

Separate emotional force, personal criticism, distortion, and distraction; they are related but diagnostically distinct.
