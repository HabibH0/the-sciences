# Part, Whole, and Meaning

**Composition** moves a property from part to whole. **Division** moves a property from whole to part. Neither transfer is automatically valid. **Equivocation** keeps the same word while silently changing its meaning.

## Worked examples

Water being healthy does not establish that every drink containing water is healthy: that is composition. A wealthy country does not make every resident wealthy: that is division. Treating “nothing” as both silence and the number zero is equivocation.

## Guided check

For scope errors, draw an arrow showing the direction of transfer. For equivocation, replace each occurrence with its intended definition and see whether the argument still connects.

## Takeaway

Track both the scope of a property and the meaning of every repeated word.
