# Quality and Quantity

**Quality** asks whether the predicate is affirmed or denied: **affirmative** (*mūjibah*) or **negative** (*sālibah*). **Quantity** asks how much of the subject class is covered: **universal** (*kulliyyah*) or **particular** (*juzʾiyyah*).

## Worked example

> Some students are not writers.

“Some” makes the proposition particular. “Are not” denies the predicate, so its quality is negative.

## Guided check

Classify “No books are boring things” twice: once by quantity and once by quality.

## Takeaway

Quantity measures scope; quality records affirmation or denial.
