# Mode and Its Basis

**Mode** states how the subject and predicate can be connected. A connection may be **necessary**, **contingent**, or **impossible**.

The judgement may rest on physical experience, rational or metaphysical necessity, or moral and religious authority. The basis is evidence for the judgement; it is not another name for the mode.

## Worked examples

“A whole is necessarily greater than one of its proper parts” rests on rational necessity. “A healthy person can become ill” is contingent and known through experience. A contradiction is impossible because its terms cannot be true together.

## Guided check

For each claim, ask: What mode is asserted? What kind of evidence warrants it?

## Takeaway

Classify the force of the connection and the basis of the judgement separately.
