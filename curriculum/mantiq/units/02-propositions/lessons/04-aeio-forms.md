# The A, E, I, and O Forms

Quantity and quality combine into four standard forms:

| Form | Quantity | Quality | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | universal | affirmative | All S are P |
| E | universal | negative | No S are P |
| I | particular | affirmative | Some S are P |
| O | particular | negative | Some S are not P |

## Worked example

“No books are boring things” is universal and negative, so it is an E-form.

## Guided check

Classify “Some vegetables are tasty things.” State quantity and quality before naming its letter.

## Takeaway

The letter is shorthand for a precise quantity–quality combination.
