# Venn Representation

Place the subject circle on the left and predicate circle on the right. **Shading** marks an empty region; an **x** marks at least one member.

| Form | Mark |
|---|---|
| A | shade the subject-only region |
| E | shade the overlap |
| I | place x in the overlap |
| O | place x in the subject-only region |

## Worked example

“Some students are not writers” is O. Its x belongs inside the students circle but outside the writers circle: the subject-only region.

## Guided check

Represent “No books are boring things.” Decide first whether it asserts existence or emptiness, then choose the region.

## Takeaway

Universal forms shade; particular forms place an x. The form determines the region.
