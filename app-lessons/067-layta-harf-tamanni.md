# Lesson 67: ليت — حرف التمنّي

The sixth of the seven حروف تنصب الاسم وترفع الخبر is لَيْتَ.

> **Definition Box**
>
> لَيْتَ حَرْفٌ لِلتَّمَنِّي، وَهُوَ طَلَبُ مَا لَا طَمَعَ فِيهِ

التمنّي is a particular kind of wanting — not just any request, but one aimed at something entirely outside the realm of the possible, something you have no real hope of attaining.

> **Example Box**
>
> لَيْتَ الشَّبَابَ يَعُودُ يَوْمًا فَأُخْبِرَهُ بِمَا فَعَلَ المَشِيبُ
>
> If only youth would return one day, that I might tell it what old age has done.

Youth cannot return — that is exactly why wishing for it counts as تمنٍّ rather than an ordinary request. The poet personifies الشباب, imagining a day it might come back just so he could describe to it, in detail, everything old age has since done to him: the aches, the failing digestion, the disturbed sleep — but also the psychological relief of having outgrown youthful excess and bad habits.

## الغالب أن يسبقها يا

> **Rule Box**
>
> الغَالِبُ فِي التَّنْزِيلِ أَنْ يَسْبِقَهَا يَا

لَيْتَ never actually appears alone in the Qur'an — it is regularly preceded by يَا, most often stretched into لَيْتَنِي.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿يَا لَيْتَنِي لَمْ أُوتَ كِتَابِيَهْ﴾
>
> Oh, I wish I had not been given my record!

This is the cry of the wrongdoer on the Day of Judgment upon receiving their book of deeds. The يَا and the elongated مَدّ both intensify the وَجْد, the emotional weight of the wish — they make the تمنّي felt more sharply than لَيْتَنِي alone would.

> **Careful Box**
>
> في إعرابه وجهان — grammarians differ on what this يَا is actually doing here, since there is no ordinary مُنادى (someone being called) in sight. One view: يَا هُنَا حَرْفُ تَنْبِيهٍ, a mere attention-particle, not a real vocative يَا at all. The other: المُنَادَى مَحْذُوفٌ — a vocative is intended, but its object has been dropped, understood but unspoken. Either analysis agrees on the same point: grammatically, this يَا is neither a true نداء nor a true تنبيه in the fullest technical sense — its entire purpose is to intensify لَيْتَ itself, giving the wish more emotional weight.
