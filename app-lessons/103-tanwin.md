# Lesson 103: التنوين

التَّنْوين — "nunation" — is the eighteenth and final قسم of الحرف غير العامل. Though written as a diacritic rather than a separate letter, it is treated grammatically as a حرف, because it is fundamentally a سكون-carrying نون folded into the end of a word.

> **Definition Box**
>
> القِسْمُ الثّامِنُ عَشَرَ: التَّنْوينُ. أَصْلُ التَّنْوينِ نونٌ ساكِنَةٌ، أَيْ أَحَدُنْ.

## أصل التنوين

> **Rule Box**
>
> أَصْلُ التَّنْوينِ نونٌ ساكِنَةٌ.

Why treat a diacritic mark as if it were a حرف? Because historically, التنوين IS a نون ساكنة (a silent نون) attached to the end of the word — it just isn't written out as a full letter. In وَصْل (continuous recitation), a تنوين is pronounced exactly as a نون ساكنة would be: ﴿قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ﴾ ﴿اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ﴾ is read أَحَدٙنِ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ — the تنوين surfaces as a نون sound connecting into the next word, shifting to a كسر before a following hamzat-waṣl exactly as an ordinary نون ساكنة would. The name تَنْوين itself comes from the root ن-و-ن ("to noon-ify"), because its function is to produce the sound of a نون at the end of the word.

## أقسام التنوين

> **Rule Box**
>
> التَّنْوينُ أَرْبَعَةُ أَقْسامٍ: تَمْكينٌ، تَنْكيرٌ، مُقابَلَةٌ، تَعْويضٌ.

### الأول: تنوين التمكين

Attaches to fully مُعْرَب, مُنْصَرِف nouns — nouns with complete إعراب and no restriction on their case-endings. Also called تَنْوين الصَّرْف and تَنْوين الْأَمْكَنِيَّة.

> **Example Box**
>
> زَيْدٌ وَرَجُلٌ — ordinary, fully-declinable nouns. Note: a noun can be نَكِرَة (indefinite) and still carry تَنْوين التَّمْكين rather than تَنْوين التَّنْكير — بَيْتٌ is indefinite, but because it is a normal مُنْصَرِف noun, its تنوين is تَمْكين, not تَنْكير. The category is defined by what the noun IS grammatically, not merely by whether it happens to be indefinite.

### الثاني: تنوين التنكير

Attaches to أَسْماء الْأَفْعال (verb-nouns) and to certain مَبْنِي proper nouns — specifically those ending in وَيْه.

> **Example Box**
>
> صَهٍ — "quiet!" Without تنوين, صَهْ means "stop talking about the specific thing we're discussing"; WITH تنوين, صَهٍ means "be quiet altogether, about everything" — the تنوين marks a shift from a definite, specific reference to a fully indefinite/general one.
>
> سيبَوَيْهٍ — without تنوين, سيبَوَيْه refers to the specific, famous grammarian (معرفة, one particular person). WITH تنوين, سيبَوَيْهٍ becomes a common noun meaning "a Sībawayh" — any grammarian of that caliber, a generic member of the class (نكرة) — as in هٰذا سيبَوَيْهٍ جَديدٌ, "this is a new Sībawayh [a new great grammarian]."

### الثالث: تنوين المقابلة

Attaches specifically to جَمْع الْمُؤَنَّث السّالِم (the sound feminine plural).

> **Example Box**
>
> مُسْلِماتٌ — the تنوين here corresponds directly (مُقابَلَة) to the نون that appears in the sound MASCULINE plural (مُسْلِمونَ). Both signal that the word is تامّ (complete, nothing further attached — no مُضاف إِلَيْه) — masculine plurals use a نون, feminine plurals use a تنوين, for the same job. Compare: كِتابٌ (تنوين present → تامّ) versus كِتابُ (no تنوين → إضافة expected, e.g. كِتابُ زَيْدٍ).

### الرابع: تنوين التعويض

Stands in as a substitute (عِوَض) for something dropped — a letter, a word, or an entire sentence.

> **Example Box**
>
> **عوض عن حرف:** ﴿غَواشٍ﴾ — originally غَواشِيٌ (the plural pattern فَواعِل of فاعِلَة); the final ي was dropped and the تنوين compensates for its loss.
>
> **عوض عن كلمة:** ﴿كُلٌّ آمَنَ﴾ — "each one believed," meaning كُلُّ أَحَدٍ آمَنَ ("each PERSON believed"); the تنوين on كُلٌّ stands in for the dropped word أَحَدٍ.
>
> **عوض عن جملة:** ﴿وَيَوْمَئِذٍ يَفْرَحُ الْمُؤْمِنونَ﴾ — "and on that day the believers will rejoice." The تنوين on يَوْمَئِذٍ stands in for an entire dropped clause: the full original sense is يَوْمَ إِذْ كانَ كَذا, "the day WHEN [such-and-such] occurs" — referring to the day Allah grants the Romans victory over the fire-worshippers. The تنوين compresses that whole understood clause into itself.

## تنوين الترنم (إضافة من جماعة من النحاة)

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَزادَ جَماعَةٌ تَنْوينَ التَّرَنُّمِ، وهو اللّاحِقُ لِلْقَوافي الْمُطْلَقَةِ.

Beyond the four standard types, a group of grammarians add a fifth: تَنْوين التَّرَنُّم, purely for melodic effect at the end of a line of poetry, with no grammatical function at all. It attaches to the القافِيَة الْمُطْلَقَة (an "open" rhyme, whose final letter is preceded by a حَرْف مَدّ), and unlike the other three types, it can attach to any part of speech: اسم, فعل, or حرف.

> **Example Box**
>
> أَقِلّي اللَّوْمَ عاذِلُ والْعِتابَنْ ... وَقولي إِنْ أَصَبْتُ لَقَدْ أَصابَنْ — "Reduce your blame, O critic, and your reproach... and say, if I got it right, 'he got it right.'" Without تنوين ترنم, the sentence is simply: يا عاذِلَةُ! أَقِلّي اللَّوْمَ والْعِتابَ، وَقولي إِنْ أَصَبْتُ لَقَدْ أَصابَ. Notice العِتابَنْ (from العِتابَ, a noun) and أَصابَنْ (from أَصابَ, a PAST-TENSE VERB) both take the same تنوين — proving this تنوين attaches for sound alone, since ordinary تنوين never attaches to a فعل ماضٍ.

> **Careful Box**
>
> This closes out حَرْف غَيْر عامِل entirely. Three أقسام in between — القِسْم الخامِس عَشَر (الرّابِطَة لِلْجَواب: الفاء واللّام), القِسْم السّادِس عَشَر (تاء التَّأْنيث السّاكِنَة), and القِسْم السّابِع عَشَر (هاء السَّكْت) — are straightforward enough that they are left for direct reading from the matn rather than a separate lesson.
