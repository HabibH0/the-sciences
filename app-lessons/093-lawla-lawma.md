# Lesson 93: لَوْلا ولَوْما

The second and third of the four أحرف الشرط are covered together in this short lesson: لَوْلا and لَوْما. Both share the exact same meaning and grammatical behavior — لَوْما is simply a less common variant of لَوْلا, used identically.

## الثاني: لولا

> **Definition Box**
>
> لَوْلا لِامْتِناعِ الجَوابِ لِوُجودِ الشَّرْطِ.

The meaning here is the OPPOSITE polarity from لَوْ. Where لَوْ signals "الجواب failed to happen because الشرط failed to happen" (شرط negative → جواب negative), لَوْلا signals "الجواب failed to happen BECAUSE الشرط DID happen" (شرط positive → جواب negative). لَوْلا is often translated "were it not for..." or "had it not been for..." — it introduces something that DID occur, and that occurrence is precisely what PREVENTED the جواب.

> **Rule Box**
>
> وهي تَدْخُلُ على جُمْلَتَيْنِ: أَوَّلُهُما اِسْمِيَّةٌ خَبَرُها مَحْذوفٌ تَقْديرُهُ مَوْجودٌ، وَثانيهِما فِعْلِيَّةٌ.

لَوْلا governs two clauses. The FIRST clause is always اِسْمِيَّة (nominal) — it names the thing that "existed"/"was present" and thereby blocked الجواب — and its خبر is always OMITTED, with the implied meaning simply مَوْجود ("exists/is present"). The SECOND clause is فِعْلِيَّة (verbal), and functions as جواب الشرط.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَلَوْلا فَضْلُ اللهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُ ما زَكٰى مِنْكُمْ مِنْ أَحَدٍ أَبَدًا﴾ — "And were it not for the favor of Allah upon you and His mercy, not one of you would ever have been purified." فَضْلُ اللهِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَتُهُ is the first, اِسْمِيَّة clause — فَضْلُ is the مُبْتَدَأ, and its خَبَر is the omitted مَوْجود (i.e., "Allah's favor upon you and His mercy [exists/is present]"). ما زَكٰى مِنْكُمْ مِنْ أَحَدٍ أَبَدًا is the second, فِعْلِيَّة clause, serving as جَواب الشَّرْط: because Allah's favor and mercy DID exist, purification did NOT fail to happen for anyone — grammatically, لولا frames it as: had that favor not existed, purification would never have occurred at all.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Do not confuse لَوْلا's polarity with لَوْ's (covered in the previous lesson). With لَوْ, both شرط and جواب are negative (neither happened). With لَوْلا, الشرط is POSITIVE (it did happen — that is exactly what "blocks" الجواب), while الجواب remains negative (it did not happen, precisely because الشرط did). Always check which of the two particles you are looking at before assigning polarity to شرط and جواب.

## الثالث: لوما

> **Rule Box**
>
> لَوْما لِامْتِناعِ الجَوابِ لِوُجودِ الشَّرْطِ — same meaning and function as لَوْلا.

لَوْما behaves identically to لَوْلا in every respect: same meaning (الجواب blocked because الشرط occurred), and the same two-clause structure (اسمية بخبر محذوف تقديره موجود، ثم فعلية كجواب الشرط) applies here as well.

> **Example Box**
>
> لَوْما التَّوْفيقُ لَما عَمِلْنا الصّالِحاتِ — "Were it not for [Allah's granting of] success, we would not have done righteous deeds." التَّوْفيقُ existed (that is الشرط), and precisely because it existed, "we did not do righteous deeds" is negated — meaning we DID do them, thanks to that success being granted.
