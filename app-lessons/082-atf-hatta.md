# Lesson 82: أحرف العطف — حَتّى

The fourth of the nine أحرف العطف is حَتّى. It carries two ideas at once: غايَة (an ending point) and تَدْريج (a gradual, step-by-step approach toward that ending point).

> **Definition Box**
>
> الرّابِعُ حَتّى، وهي لِلْغايَةِ والتَّدْريج.
>
> الغايَةُ آخِرُ الشَّيْءِ، وَمَعْنَى التَّدْريج أَنَّ ما قَبْلَها يَنْقَضي شَيْئًا فَشَيْئًا إِلَى أَنْ يَبْلُغَ إِلَى الغايَةِ.

غايَة simply means "the end of something." تَدْريج means what comes before حَتّى does not arrive at that end all at once — it unfolds bit by bit, stage by stage, until it finally reaches the غاية. For example, قَرَأْتُ القُرْآنَ حَتّى سورَةَ النّاسِ ("I read the Qur'an until Surat an-Nas") pictures the reading proceeding gradually, sūrah after sūrah, until reaching the very last one — not the whole Qur'an consumed in one instant.

## غايتها تكون: زمانًا، مكانًا، أو رتبةً

> **Rule Box**
>
> غايَتُها يَكونُ زَمانًا، أَوْ مَكانًا، أَوْ رُتْبَةً.

> **Example Box**
>
> **زَمانًا** (a point in TIME): ﴿سَلٰمٌ هِيَ حَتّٰى مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ﴾ — "Peace it is until the rising of dawn" [Laylat al-Qadr]. The peace and tranquility sent down by the angels continues, stage after stage through the night, until it reaches its end-point: the break of dawn.
>
> **مَكانًا** (a point in SPACE): مَشَيْتُ حَتّى بابِ المَسْجِدِ — "I walked until the door of the masjid." The walking proceeds step by step until reaching that spot.
>
> **رُتْبَةً** (a point on a scale of RANK): ماتَ النّاسُ حَتَّى الْأَنْبِياءُ — "People died, even the Prophets." Here حَتّى's غاية is not a place or a time — it is a rung on a ladder of rank. Death works its way, degree by degree, all the way up to the highest possible rank of people: the Prophets.

> **Careful Box**
>
> When حَتّى's غاية is رُتْبَة, do NOT assume it is also telling you a chronological order. حَتّى هٰذِهِ لا تُفيدُ التَّرْتيبَ الزَّمانِيَّ، بَلْ هِيَ كالْواوِ — "This kind of حتى does not indicate chronological order at all; it is [in that respect] just like واو." In ماتَ النّاسُ حَتَّى الْأَنْبِياءُ, the Prophets did not necessarily die LAST in time — history shows many ordinary people died after many Prophets. حَتّى here only ranks them at the top of the scale of رتبة (greatness), saying nothing about when, chronologically, their deaths occurred. Any apparent time-order in a رتبة example is coincidental, not something حتى itself asserts.

## الشرط الأول: أن يكون المعطوف داخلًا فيما قبله

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَيُشْتَرَطُ أَنْ يَكونَ مَعْطوفُها داخِلًا في المَعْطوفِ عَلَيْهِ.

Whatever comes after حَتّى must genuinely be a PART of whatever came before it — not merely something associated with it.

> **Example Box**
>
> ✓ أَكَلْتُ السَّمَكَةَ حَتّى رَأْسَها — "I ate the fish, even its head." Correct: the head (رأسها) is a part of the fish itself.
>
> ✗ أَكَلْتُ السَّمَكَةَ حَتّى الفاكِهَةَ — INCORRECT as a way to say "I ate the fish and even the fruit [because I was so hungry]." الفاكِهَة (fruit) is not a part of السَّمَكَة (fish) at all, so حَتّى cannot join them this way — واو or ثُمَّ would be needed instead (أَكَلْتُ السَّمَكَةَ والفاكِهَةَ / أَكَلْتُ السَّمَكَةَ ثُمَّ الفاكِهَةَ).

## الشرط الثاني: أن يكون المعطوف آخر جزء مما قبله

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَيُشْتَرَطُ أَنْ يَكونَ مَعْطوفُها آخِرَ جُزْءٍ مِمّا قَبْلَها، حَقيقَةً أَوْ حُكْمًا.

Being merely "a part" is not enough — it must be the LAST part, since حَتّى signals a غاية (an end-point), and an end-point necessarily comes at the end. This "lastness" can be either حَقيقَة (literally, factually the final part) or حُكْمًا (only in a certain respect/ranking, not literally the final item).

> **Example Box**
>
> **آخر جزء حقيقةً:** قَرَأْتُ القُرْآنَ حَتّى سورَةَ النّاسِ — correct, because Surat an-Nas genuinely is the very last sūrah of the Qur'an. بِخِلافِ حَتّى سورَةَ الكَهْفِ — you could NOT say "...until Surat al-Kahf," because al-Kahf is somewhere in the middle of the Qur'an, not its literal last part.
>
> **آخر جزء حُكْمًا — إمّا في القوة أو التعظيم** (last in RANK, on the high end — strength/greatness): ماتَ النّاسُ حَتّى الْأَنْبِياءُ — the Prophets are not literally the last people to die in time, but they occupy the top-most rank of greatness, so حَتّى can treat them as the "last part" of the scale in that sense.
>
> **آخر جزء حُكْمًا — إمّا في الضعف أو التحقير** (last in RANK, on the low end — weakness/lowliness): قَدِمَ الحُجّاجُ حَتّى المُشاةُ — "The pilgrims arrived, even those on foot." Those walking are the slowest and arrive last in a practical sense — the bottom of the rank-scale of speed/means of travel, which حَتّى treats as its "last part" from the opposite direction.

## حَتّى العاطفة وحَتّى الجارّة

حَتّى does not always function as a حرف عطف. The very same word can also work as a حرف جرّ (a preposition). Since the underlying meaning of غاية + تدريج is baked into حَتّى either way, learning to tell the two apart matters.

> **Rule Box**
>
> فالفَرْقُ بَيْنَهُما مِنْ وَجْهَيْنِ:
>
> الأول: العَطْف يَشْتَرِطُ أَنَّ المَعْطوفَ جُزْءٌ مِمّا قَبْلَهُ؛ الجَرُّ لا يَشْتَرِطُ ذٰلِكَ — قَدْ يَكونُ المَجْرورُ مُتَّصِلًا بِما قَبْلَهُ فَقَطْ، لا جُزْءًا مِنْهُ.
>
> الثّاني: المَعْطوفُ بِحَتّى يَكونُ في حُكْمِ ما قَبْلَهُ أَلْبَتَّةَ (always); المَجْرورُ بِحَتّى يَكونُ في حُكْمِ ما قَبْلَها فقط إِذا دَلَّ عَلَيْهِ دَليلٌ — وَإِلّا فَلا.

> **Example Box**
>
> **حَتّى عاطِفَة** (the following word IS part of what precedes, and automatically shares its حكم): صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى آخِرَ يَوْمِها — "I fasted Ramadan, [up] to its last day." آخِرَ يَوْمِها is a genuine part of رمضان, and — because حَتّى here is عاطِفَة — it is automatically included in the ruling: yes, the last day of Ramadan was fasted too.
>
> **حَتّى جارَّة** (the following word is merely CONNECTED, not a part, and is not automatically included in the حكم): صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى يَوْمَ الفِطْرِ — "I fasted Ramadan, until the Day of Fitr." يَوْمَ الفِطْرِ is NOT part of Ramadan at all (Eid begins precisely because Ramadan has ended) — so this حَتّى must be جارَّة (a preposition), never عاطِفَة, and nothing here tells us Eid day itself was fasted (indeed it could not be, since fasting on Eid is forbidden). حَتّى في هٰذا المِثالِ جارَّةٌ حَتْمًا — "حَتّى in this example is necessarily جارَّة" — precisely because Eid day is excluded from Ramadan; had it been عاطِفَة, Eid day would have had to be included in the fast.

> **Careful Box**
>
> A middle case shows why the SECOND rule (حكم ما قبلها إلا إذا دلّ عليه دليل) matters even for حَتّى الجارّة: سِرْتُ النَّهارَ حَتّى العَصْرِ — "I travelled through the day, until 'Asr." العصر here is not literally a part of النهار in the same tight sense as رأسها is part of السمكة, yet nothing in the sentence excludes 'Asr from the travelling — so, absent a دليل (contextual indicator) to the contrary, المجرور here is understood to share the same حكم as what came before it (the travelling extended through 'Asr). Compare this with يَوْمَ الفِطْرِ, where a clear دليل — the fact that fasting on Eid is impermissible — positively excludes it from the حكم. The rule is not "المجرور is never included" — it is "المجرور is included only when nothing indicates otherwise," which is a weaker guarantee than المعطوف's unconditional inclusion.

## ملخّص: ما بعد حتّى

Putting the two rules together, "what follows حَتّى" falls into three situations, not two:

| ما بعد حتّى | جُزْءٌ مُتَّصِلٌ بِما قَبْلَها فَقَط | جُزْءٌ مِمّا قَبْلَها | جُزْءٌ مِمّا قَبْلَها |
|---|---|---|---|
| نوع حتّى | جارَّةٌ | جارَّةٌ | عاطِفَةٌ |
| في حكم ما قبلها؟ | لَيْسَ في حُكْمِ ما قَبْلَها | في حُكْمِ ما قَبْلَها | في حُكْمِ ما قَبْلَها |
| مثال | صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى يَوْمَ الفِطْرِ | صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى آخِرَ أَيّامِها | صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى آخِرَ أَيّامِها |

Notice the middle and right columns share the exact same sentence: صُمْتُ رَمَضانَ حَتّى آخِرَ أَيّامِها is genuinely ambiguous between the two readings, because آخِرَ أَيّامِها really is a جزء of رمضان either way — the sentence works whether you parse حَتّى there as جارّة or as عاطِفَة, since both readings land in the same حكم (the last day was fasted). Only when what follows is NOT a جزء (like يَوْمَ الفِطْرِ) does the ambiguity collapse — there, حَتّى can only be جارّة.
