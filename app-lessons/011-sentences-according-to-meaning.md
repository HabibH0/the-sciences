# Lesson 11: أقسام الجملة باعتبار معناها

## خبر and إنشاء

A جملة may be classified by meaning into two types:

| Category | Definition | General meaning |
|---|---|---|
| الجملة الخبرية | speech whose speaker can be truthful or false | informing: إخبار |
| الجملة الإنشائية | speech whose speaker is not described as truthful or false | performing the meaning: إنشاء |

`خبرية` does not mean the جملة is صادقة. It means the جملة is the kind of statement that can be judged صدقًا or كذبًا.

> **Example Box**
>
> `زَيْدٌ قَائِمٌ` is خبرية.  
> It may be صادقة or كاذبة depending on whether Zayd is actually standing.

The same applies even when the content is certainly صادق or كاذب. `الله واحد` is خبرية and صادقة. A false mathematical statement is still خبرية because it gives إخبارًا that can be judged.

## خبرية example

> **Example Box**
>
> `إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ`
>
> This gives information: worship is for Allah alone and help is sought from Allah alone. It is خبرية.

## Visual tarkeeb: إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ

| إِيَّاكَ | نَعْبُدُ |
|---|---|
| مفعول به مقدم | فعل + فاعل مستتر |

The جملة is خبرية in meaning even though its arrangement gives strong emphasis.

## Types of إنشاء

إنشاء includes expressions such as:

| Type | Example | Translation | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| استفهام | أَإِلَهٌ مَعَ اللهِ | Is there a god with Allah? | question |
| تمني | يَا لَيْتَ قَوْمِي يَعْلَمُونَ | If only my people knew | wishing |
| نداء | يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّسُولُ | O Messenger | calling |
| تحذير | نَاقَةَ اللهِ وَسُقْيَاهَا | the she-camel of Allah and her drink | warning |
| إغراء | العِلْمَ العِلْمَ | knowledge, knowledge | urging |
| أمر | أَقِيمُوا الصَّلاةَ | establish the prayer | command |
| نهي | لا تَقْرَبُوا الفَوَاحِشَ | do not approach immoralities | prohibition |
| دعاء | سَلامٌ عَلَيْكُمْ | peace be upon you | supplication |
| مدح | وَلَنِعْمَ دَارُ المُتَّقِينَ | excellent indeed is the home of the pious | praise |
| ذم | بِئْسَ الاسمُ الفُسُوقُ | wretched is the name of wickedness | blame |
| توجع / تفجع | يَا حَسْرَتَى عَلَى مَا فَرَّطْتُ فِي جَنْبِ اللهِ | O my regret over what I neglected towards Allah | pain or grief |
| قسم | وَالعَصْرِ | by time | oath |

These are not judged as صدق or كذب in the ordinary way. They perform إنشاء: استفهام, نداء, أمر, تحذير, مدح, ذم, توجع, or قسم.

> **Careful Box**
>
> The difference is in what the speaker is doing, not in the subject matter.  
> `أَإِلَهٌ مَعَ اللهِ` is إنشاء because it asks. Drop the همزة and say `إِلَهٌ مَعَ اللهِ` and it becomes خبرية, because it now reports something — a report whose value happens to be كاذب.

## اللفظ والمعنى قد يختلفان

Sometimes the wording points one way, while the meaning functions another way.

| Example | Translation | Wording | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| الحَمْدُ للهِ | all praise belongs to Allah | خبر | may function as إنشاء by expressing praise |
| فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ | let him take his seat in the Fire | إنشاء | means خبر: he will take his place in the Fire |

> **Careful Box**
>
> The classification may consider wording, meaning, or both.  
> `الحمد لله` is a statement in wording, but it can also perform praise.

In the second case, `فليتبوأ` looks like أمر because of لام الأمر. Its meaning, however, is not an invitation to choose Hellfire. It is خبر شديد: whoever lies deliberately against the Messenger will take his place in the Fire.

The أمر wording is chosen for a reason. Moving a meaning away from its ordinary صيغة adds تأكيد: it holds the listener's attention and makes the warning land harder than a plain report would.
