# Lesson 16: العامل and غير العامل

## عامل, معمول, and إعراب

Three terms belong together:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| العامل | what causes a grammatical state |
| المعمول | what receives that grammatical state |
| الإعراب | the grammatical state or change |

## What is an عامل?

> **Definition Box**
>
> العَامِلُ مَا يُوجِبُ حَالَةً

An `عامل` brings about a grammatical state in another word.

> **Example Box**
>
> `وَلَنْ نُشْرِكَ بِرَبِّنَا أَحَدًا`
>
> and we will never associate anyone with our Lord
>
> `لن` affects the فعل after it and makes it منصوب.

## Visual tarkeeb: وَلَنْ نُشْرِكَ

| وَ | لَنْ | نُشْرِكَ |
|---|---|---|
| حرف عطف | عامل ناصب | فعل مضارع منصوب |

## What can be عامل?

| Category | Status |
|---|---|
| الأفعال | all of them are عوامل |
| الحروف | some are عوامل, some are not |
| الأسماء | some are عوامل, but most are not |
| الجملة | never an عامل |
| شبه الجملة | never an عامل |

All أفعال govern something connected to them. For example, a فعل may govern a فاعل, نائب فاعل, مفعول به, or another element depending on the structure. When you see a فعل, expect a معمول with it.

Some حروف govern, such as `لن`. Other حروف carry meaning but do not cause a grammatical state. The two groups are roughly balanced: a large share of the حروف govern, and a large share do not.

Some أسماء also govern when they have the right form and conditions, such as certain مشتقات and مصدر constructions. Governing أسماء are the small minority, though; the great majority of أسماء perform no عمل.

## جملة and شبه الجملة

A `جملة` never performs عمل. This holds for the جملة كبرى and the جملة صغرى alike: neither of them governs another word.

`شبه الجملة` is the same. There is an عامل working upon the شبه الجملة, and that عامل is what produces the جار ومجرور or the ظرف, but the شبه الجملة itself governs nothing. It is always معمول, never عامل.

## What is غير العامل?

> **Definition Box**
>
> غَيْرُ العَامِلِ مَا لا يُوجِبُ حَالَةً

`غير العامل` does not cause a grammatical state, even if it still carries meaning.

> **Example Box**
>
> In `مَا أَشْرَكْنَا` (we did not associate partners), `ما` gives نفي but does not change the grammatical state of `أشركنا`.

## عاطل and مهمل

A حرف غير عامل may be called:

| Term | Sense of the word | Intended meaning |
|---|---|---|
| عاطل | idle, out of work, doing nothing | it performs no grammatical عمل |
| مهمل | null and void, left with no effect | it performs no grammatical عمل |

These terms do not mean the حرف has no meaning. They mean it does not govern another word.

This distinction matters for حروف. A حرف always has meaning, but it may be عامل or غير عامل. Calling it `مهمل` or `عاطل` only speaks about grammatical عمل, not about whether it contributes meaning.
