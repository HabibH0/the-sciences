# Lesson 6: أضرب الاسم باعتبار معناه

## الضربان الرئيسيان

An `اسم` may be classified by what it points to in meaning. From this angle, أسماء divide into:

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الموصوف / اسم الذات | something that can be described |
| اسم الصفة | a description attached to something described |

> **Definition Box**
>
> المَوْصُوفُ: مَا دَلَّ عَلَى ذَاتٍ تَحْتَمِلُ الصِّفَةَ

`الموصوف` means the thing being described. It is also called `اسم الذات`.

## اسم العين and اسم المعنى

`اسم الذات` has two types:

| Type | Definition | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| اسم عين | what points to something that exists in itself | رَجُلٌ | a man |
| اسم معنى | what points to something that does not exist by itself, but exists in another | عِلْمٌ | knowledge |

`اسم عين` points to something that exists in itself, like a man, pen, or book.

`اسم معنى` points to a meaning that does not stand by itself. Knowledge, for example, exists in someone who has knowledge.

> **Careful Box**
>
> `علم` is not a physical object standing by itself.  
> It is a meaning found in someone or something, so it is `اسم معنى`.

## اسم الصفة

> **Definition Box**
>
> الصِّفَةُ: مَا دَلَّ عَلَى وَصْفِ مَوْصُوفٍ

`اسم الصفة` gives a description of a `موصوف`.

> **Example Box**
>
> `رَجُلٌ صَالِحٌ`
>
> `رجل` is the thing being described.  
> `صالح` is the description.

## Visual tarkeeb: رَجُلٌ صَالِحٌ

| رَجُلٌ | صَالِحٌ |
|---|---|
| موصوف / اسم ذات | صفة |

Another useful set is:

| Word | Type | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| رَجُلٌ | اسم عين | exists in itself |
| عِلْمٌ | اسم معنى | exists in another |
| عَالِمٌ | اسم صفة | describes one who has علم |

The distinction is based on meaning, not on إعراب. `رجل`, `علم`, and `عالم` may all behave as أسماء in a جملة, but they point to different kinds of meanings.

`رجل` gives a thing that can be described. `علم` gives a meaning that exists in someone. `عالم` gives a description of the person who possesses that meaning.
