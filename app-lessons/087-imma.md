# Lesson 87: إمّا — هل هي من أحرف العطف؟

Having covered all nine أحرف العطف (واو، فاء، ثمّ، حتّى، أو، أمّ، لا، بل، لكن), a related word deserves a note precisely because it is easy to mistake for a tenth: إِمّا. Some books do count it among the أحرف العطف — but this text follows the more correct opinion (الأصحّ) that it is NOT one of them at all.

> **Definition Box**
>
> وَكَلِمَةُ إِمّا لَيْسَ بِحَرْفِ عَطْفٍ على الْأَصَحِّ، وهي مِثْلُ أَوْ وَأَمْ في المَعْنى.

إمّا behaves, in MEANING, much like أَوْ and أَمْ — presenting a choice between options — which is exactly why some grammarians were tempted to list it as a tenth حرف عطف. But the stronger position treats it as something else structurally (its precise classification is a longer discussion this text sets aside), even while acknowledging it does real work of the same general kind.

## لا تأتي إلا مكررة (أو منوبا عنها بأو)

> **Rule Box**
>
> لا يَأْتي إِمّا إِلّا مُكَرَّرًا، أَوْ مَنوبًا عَنْها بِأَوْ.

Unlike أَوْ or أَمْ, which can appear just once between two items, إمّا has a strict structural requirement: it must appear TWICE (once before each option) — or, if it appears only once, the second occurrence must be replaced by أَوْ instead.

> **Example Box**
>
> **مكررة (repeated):** ﴿إِنّا هَدَيْناهُ السَّبيلَ إِمّا شاكِرًا وَإِمّا كَفورًا﴾ — "Indeed We guided him to the way, be he grateful or be he ungrateful." إمّا appears TWICE: إِمّا شاكِرًا ... وَإِمّا كَفورًا. Neither occurrence can be dropped.
>
> **منوبة عنها بأو (the second replaced by أَوْ):** الِاسْمُ إِمّا مُذَكَّرٌ أَوْ مُؤَنَّثٌ — "The noun is either masculine or feminine." Here only the FIRST إمّا appears; the second slot is filled by أَوْ instead of a second إمّا.

> **Careful Box**
>
> What you may NOT do is use إمّا only once with a plain واو standing in for the second occurrence (الِاسْمُ إِمّا مُذَكَّرٌ وَمُؤَنَّثٌ is not correct in this pattern) — the second slot must be filled either by a second إمّا, or specifically by أَوْ, never by a bare واو.

## إعراب إمّا الثانية وما يجاورها من واو

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِمّا الْأولٰى حَرْفُ تَفْصيل. وَفي إِعْرابِ إِمّا الثّانِيَةِ (حين تُسبَق بِواو) وَجْهانِ.

The FIRST إمّا is always straightforwardly حَرْفُ تَفْصيل ("a particle of detailing/itemizing" — it introduces the first of the options being spelled out). But when a واو precedes the SECOND إمّا (as in وَإِمّا كَفورًا above), grammarians offer two different ways to parse that واو + إمّا combination:

> **Example Box**
>
> **الوجه الأول:** الواوُ زائِدَةٌ (an extra/non-functional واو) وَإِمّا حَرْفُ عَطْفٍ (and إمّا itself is doing the joining).
>
> **الوجه الثاني:** الواوُ حَرْفُ عَطْفٍ (the واو is what does the joining) وَإِمّا حَرْفُ تَفْصيلٍ زائِدَةٌ في الْإِعْرابِ (and إمّا is just a detailing-particle, extra as far as الإعراب/the case-analysis is concerned).

> **Careful Box**
>
> This exact two-sided analysis — "either the واو is زائدة and the other word does the عطف, or the واو does the عطف and the other word is زائدة in الإعراب" — is the SAME pattern grammarians use for وَلٰكِنْ (studied separately): in both وَإِمّا and وَلٰكِنْ, there are two valid ways to assign which word is "doing the grammatical work" and which is along for the ride. Recognizing this recurring two-wajh pattern helps when you meet it again elsewhere.
