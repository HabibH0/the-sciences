# Lesson 65: لكنّ — حرف الاستدراك

The fourth of the seven حروف تنصب الاسم وترفع الخبر is لَكِنَّ.

> **Definition Box**
>
> لَكِنَّ حَرْفٌ لِلِاسْتِدْرَاكِ

## معنى الاستدراك

> **Rule Box**
>
> الِاسْتِدْرَاكُ تَعْقِيبُ الكَلَامِ الأَوَّلِ بِكَلَامٍ يَرْفَعُ مَا يُتَوَهَّمُ ثُبُوتُهُ أَوْ نَفْيُهُ مِنَ الكَلَامِ الأَوَّلِ

الاستدراك means following up an initial statement with a second one that clears away a false impression the first statement might have left — whether that impression was one of ثبوت (that something is the case) or نفي (that something is not the case). تَعَقُّبَ literally carries the sense of "coming on the heel of" — أَقِب is the heel itself — so الكلام الثاني trails right behind الكلام الأول, correcting what it might have implied.

> **Example Box**
>
> مَا زَيْدٌ قَوِيًّا، لَكِنَّهُ شُجَاعٌ
>
> Zayd is not strong — but he is brave.

مَا زَيْدٌ قَوِيًّا, the first statement, might leave a وَهْم — a false impression — that Zayd is also not شُجاع, since weakness and cowardice often travel together in the mind of the listener. لَكِنَّهُ شُجَاعٌ steps in immediately to remove exactly that impression: whatever Zayd's strength, his bravery is not in question.

## الغالب أن يسبقها الواو

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَالغَالِبُ فِي التَّنْزِيلِ أَنْ يَسْبِقَهَا الوَاوُ

In the Qur'an specifically, لَكِنَّ is most often preceded by a واو.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَمَا كَفَرَ سُلَيْمَانُ وَلَٰكِنَّ الشَّيَاطِينَ كَفَرُوا﴾
>
> And Solomon did not disbelieve, but the devils disbelieved.

وَمَا كَفَرَ سُلَيْمَانُ might leave a وَهْم that كفر happened nowhere in the story at all — that Sulaymān's kingdom, mentioned so favorably, was untouched by disbelief altogether. وَلَٰكِنَّ الشَّيَاطِينَ كَفَرُوا removes exactly that false impression: Sulaymān himself was innocent of it, but الشياطين were not.
