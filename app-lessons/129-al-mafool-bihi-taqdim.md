# Lesson 129: Al-Maf'ul Bihi: Taqdim wa Ta'khir (المفعول به: تقديمه وتأخيره)

المفعول به ("the direct object") is the second of المنصوبات, coming right after المفعول المطلق. It's one of the most common of all منصوبات — so common that it has already been invoked repeatedly, as an illustrative example, in other chapters (such as الفاعل and نائبه). So the focus here is on a single main question not yet addressed: the rules governing when المفعول به fronts ahead of الفعل and الفاعل.

> **Definition Box**
>
> المَفْعولُ بِهِ: ما وَقَعَ عَلَيْهِ فِعْلُ الفاعِلِ — that [noun] upon which the doer's action falls. Example: ﴿اُعْبُدوا رَبَّكُمْ﴾ — "Worship your Lord" — رَبَّكُمْ is المفعول به here, since the action of عبادة (worship) falls upon it.

## ترتيب المفعول به

> **Rule Box**
>
> الأَصْلُ في المَفْعولِ بِهِ أَنْ يَكونَ بَعْدَ الفِعْلِ وَالفاعِلِ — the default word order is فعل + فاعل + مفعول به. المفعول به may — and in some situations must — front ahead of الفعل (and so ahead of الفاعل as well).

## تقديم المفعول به جوازًا

المفعول به may front ahead of الفعل and الفاعل **جوازًا** (optionally) for rhetorical purposes — such as تَوكيد (emphasis) and اِخْتِصاص (exclusivity/restriction) — without anything in the sentence's own structure requiring it.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ﴾ — "You [alone] we worship." Here المفعول به إِيَّاكَ fronts ahead of the فعل نَعْبُدُ and its hidden فاعل, نحن ("we"), to convey اختصاص and توكيد — meaning: we worship You, and You alone, no one else.

## تقديم المفعول به وجوبًا

Fronting المفعول به becomes obligatory (وجوبًا) whenever it has **صَدارَةُ الكَلامِ** ("primacy of speech") — that is, whenever it belongs to a class of words that have the inherent right to open the sentence, and can never be pushed back away from that opening position. This happens in two situations:

> **Rule Box**
>
> **١) اسم الشرط** — a conditional noun. Example: ﴿أَيًّا مَّا تَدْعُوا﴾ — "Whichever [name] you call upon..." أَيّا here is مفعول به مقدَّم for the فعل تَدْعُوا, fronted precisely because, as اسم شرط, it carries صدارة الكلام.
>
> **٢) اسم الاستفهام** — an interrogative noun. Example: ﴿فَأَيَّ آيَاتِ اللَّهِ تُنكِرُونَ﴾ — "Then which of the signs of Allah do you deny?" أَيَّ is مفعول به مقدَّم for the فعل تُنْكِرونَ, fronted because, as استفهام, it too carries صدارة الكلام — it would not be valid to say instead تُنكِرون أَيَّ آياتِ اللهِ, leaving أَيَّ in the "normal" post-verbal position.

> **Careful Box**
>
> **كَمِ الخَبَرِيَّةِ** (كم used to mean "how many!" — expressing abundance, not a real question) is attached to الاستفهام for this same obligatory-fronting rule. Example: ﴿وَكَمْ أَرْسَلْنَا مِنْ نَبِيٍّ فِي الْأَوَّلِينَ﴾ — "And how many a prophet did We send among the former peoples!" Even though this كم is not a genuine question here — it is خَبَرِيَّة, meaning roughly "and We sent many a prophet..." — because it originates as one of أدوات الاستفهام, it still retains صدارة الكلام. So whatever depends on it (its مفعول and the like) must still front, exactly as it fronts with a true استفهام.
