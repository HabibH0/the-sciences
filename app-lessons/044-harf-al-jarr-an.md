# Lesson 44: حرف الجر الرابع — عَنْ

عَنْ is the fourth of the thirteen حروف الجر. Its root meaning is الْمُجَاوَزَة.

> **Definition Box**
>
> **مَعْنَى عَنِ الْمُجَاوَزَةُ**

> **Careful Box**
>
> Literally, مُجَاوَزَة means تَعَدِّي الطَّرِيق — traversing a path, crossing it, going past it. Picture a path: crossing it means starting at one edge, moving away from that starting point, and continuing toward the far side. Distancing from the start is صُدُور; continuing on beyond it is مُجَاوَزَة proper. That single picture — a starting point left behind, movement continuing past it — runs underneath nearly everything عَنْ does.

## ثلاث صور للمجاوزة

### صدور الفعل من المجرور

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَمَا يَنْطِقُ عَنِ الْهَوَى﴾
>
> Nor does he speak from [his own] desire.

The verb نُطْق itself is pictured starting from الهوى and passing beyond it. مِنِ الْهَوَى would say only that the speech started there, leaving open whether it is still shaped by هوى. عَنِ الْهَوَى goes further — the speech moves past هوى entirely, coming instead from وَحْي: ﴿إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحْيٌ يُوحَى﴾. المفسرون put it as أَيْ لَا يَصْدُرُ نُطْقُ الرَّسُولِ بِالْقُرْآنِ عَنِ الْهَوَى — the Prophet's ﷺ speech of the Qur'an does not issue from هوى at all.

### ابتعاد الفاعل عن المجرور بسبب الفعل

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿يَصُدُّونَ عَنْ سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ﴾
>
> They turn away from the path of Allah.

Here الفاعل himself moves away from المجرور — he was on the path and, because of his own turning away, leaves it: يَبْتَعِدُ عَنْ سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ بِسَبَبِ ضَلَالِهِ.

### ابتعاد المفعول به عن المجرور بسبب الفعل

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿حَتَّى يَرُدُّوكُمْ عَنْ دِينِكُمْ﴾
>
> Until they turn you back from your religion.

Here it is المفعول به, "you," that is moved away from المجرور by another's action — الفاعل drives المفعول به away from دِينِكُمْ because of their own رَدّ.

## إذا لم تصلح هذه المعاني: تقدير محذوف

> **Rule Box**
>
> **إِذَا لَمْ يَصْلُحْ هَذَا الْمَعْنَى بِمَا ذُكِرَ مِنَ الْفِعْلِ أَوِ الْمَفْعُولِ بِهِ وَالْمَجْرُورِ، يُقَدَّرُ مَحْذُوفًا**

﴿عَفَا اللَّهُ عَنْكَ﴾ fits none of the three patterns as it stands: عَفَا cannot itself صدر from you, Allah does not move away from you because of عفو, and there is no explicit مفعول به present to be displaced. A dropped مفعول is supplied by تقدير instead: أَبْعَدَ اللَّهُ أَثَرَ الْخَطَأِ عَنْكَ بِسَبَبِ الْعَفْوِ — "Allah removed the trace of the fault from you, because of His pardon." عَفَا carries at root the sense of wiping away; what Allah removes, أَبْعَدَ, is a معطوف left unspoken but understood.

## بخل: تتعدى بالباء أو على أو عن

> **Careful Box**
>
> بَخِلَ, "to be stingy," is not مُتَعَدٍّ on its own — a حرف جر makes it so, and each one shifts what المجرور represents.
>
> With الباء: المجرور is مَا أَمْسَكَهُ الْفَاعِلُ, the very thing withheld — لَا تَبْخَلْ بِالسَّلَامِ, "do not withhold even the greeting of peace." الباء keeps its usual sense of إِلْصَاق, the withheld thing staying attached to the one withholding it.
>
> With عَلَى: المجرور is غَالِبًا مَا يَعُودُ عَلَيْهِ ضَرَرُ الْبُخْلِ, whatever bears the harm of the stinginess — بَخِلَ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ, "stingy against himself."
>
> With عَنْ: المجرور is غَالِبًا مَا ابْتَعَدَ عَنْهُ نَفْعُ الْإِنْفَاقِ, whatever the benefit of spending has moved away from. ﴿وَمَنْ يَبْخَلْ فَإِنَّمَا يَبْخَلُ عَنْ نَفْسِهِ﴾: whoever withholds only withholds from his own soul — the benefit of spending is what his own بُخْل has driven away from his نفس. Here عَنْ is doing exactly the work مجاوزة always does — something displaced away from المجرور because of الفعل.

## العوض، البدل، المكان في عن

> **Example Box**
>
> «حُجَّ عَنْ أَبِيكَ»
>
> Perform ḥajj on behalf of your father.

> **Careful Box**
>
> **قَدْ يَكُونُ عَنْ بِمَعْنَى مَكَانَ، وَهُوَ قَرِيبٌ مِنَ الْعِوَضِ، وَيُسَمَّى بَدَلًا – وَهُوَ غَيْرُ التَّابِعِ**
>
> عَنْ أَبِيكَ here does not carry the ordinary sense of مجاوزة — it carries مَكَانَ أَبِيكَ, "in the place of your father," performing the ḥajj standing in for him. This بدل sense sits close to عِوَض, already met with الباء and مِنْ: something done in place of another. The name بدل here only borrows the word — it has nothing to do with بَدَل التَّابِع, the grammatical appositive that follows and re-identifies an earlier noun. It names a substitution relationship, not that construction.

## عن الاسمية

> **Rule Box**
>
> **تَكُونُ كَلِمَةُ عَنْ اسْمًا بِمَعْنَى جَانِبٍ إِذَا سُبِقَتْ بِمِنْ، نَحْوُ: جِئْتُ مِنْ عَنْ يَمِينِهِ**

Ordinarily عَنْ is purely a particle, always followed by a noun. But preceded by مِنْ, another حرف جر, عَنْ itself shifts into the اسم مِنْ governs — no longer a particle but a noun meaning جَانِب, "side." جِئْتُ مِنْ عَنْ يَمِينِهِ: "I came from the direction of his right side" — عَنْ يَمِينِهِ together forming one اسم, "the region of his right."

## عن قليل مقابل بعد قليل

> **Careful Box**
>
> ﴿عَمَّا قَلِيلٍ لَيُصْبِحُنَّ نَادِمِينَ﴾ — "in a little while they will certainly become regretful." عَنْ marks الزَّمَان here, with مَا as زَائِدَة لِلتَّوْكِيدِ, an emphatic addition that leaves عَنْ's عمل untouched.
>
> Why عَنْ قَلِيلٍ rather than the more familiar بَعْدَ قَلِيلٍ? بَعْدَ places the short span entirely before the regret and treats the regret as arriving only once that span has fully elapsed. عَنْ, keeping its usual sense of مجاوزة, instead pictures the regret issuing directly out of the قليل itself — emerging from within that short interval rather than waiting for it to end. The practical effect is a tighter, closer sense of "soon" than بَعْدَ قَلِيلٍ carries on its own. والله أعلم.
