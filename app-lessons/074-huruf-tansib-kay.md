# Lesson 74: حروف تنصب الفعل المضارع — كَيْ

The second of the four particles that put the فعل المضارع into نصب is كَيْ. Like أنْ, it is a حرف مصدر — but where أنْ simply converts a clause into the equivalent of a مصدر, كَيْ specifically carries the meaning of تعليل, "giving a reason."

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّاني كَيْ، وهي حَرْفُ مَصْدَرٍ لِلتَّعْليلِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿فَرَجَعْناكَ إلى أُمِّكَ كَيْ تَقَرَّ عَيْنُها﴾ — "So We returned you to your mother that her eyes might be comforted." (Moses عليه السلام, restored as an infant to his mother's care.) تَقَرَّ is منصوب, and the whole كَيْ-clause explains WHY he was returned to her.

## لام الجرّ قبل كَيْ

كَيْ often appears with a لام الجرّ directly in front of it.

> **Rule Box**
>
> كَيْ يَسْبِقُها لامُ الجَرِّ، نحو: ﴿لِكَيْ لا يَعْلَمَ بَعْدَ عِلْمٍ شَيْئًا﴾.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Compare ﴿فَرَجَعْناكَ إلى أُمِّكَ كَيْ تَقَرَّ عَيْنُها﴾ (no لام) with an equivalent phrasing like فَرَدَدْناهُ إلى أُمِّهِ كَيْ تَقَرَّ عَيْنُها — even without لام written out, the meaning أَيْ لِكَيْ (i.e., "in order to") is still understood; the لام is simply left implicit (تُقَدَّر) rather than omitted from the meaning.

## لماذا يُقدَّر اللام قبل كَيْ إذا كانت ناصبة؟

كَيْ is not always a ناصب — it can also function as a حرف جرّ (comparable to لِمَ / لِمَاذا-type questions, كَيْمَهْ — "for what?"). Because the same word كَيْ covers both jobs, grammarians rely on a small structural test to keep the two uses apart.

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَذَلِكَ لِأَنَّ كَيْ تَكونُ جارَّةً وَناصِبَةً، وَإِذا كانَتْ ناصِبَةً تُقَدَّرُ اللّامُ.

When كَيْ is functioning as ناصبة (governing a فعل مضارع, as in the examples above), grammarians treat a لام as مُقَدَّرة — understood before it, whether or not it is actually written. When كَيْ is instead جارّة (a حرف جر), no لام is supplied at all, whether تقديرًا (understood) or مذكورًا (written). The presence or absence of that implied لام is effectively the signal that distinguishes which of the two jobs كَيْ is doing in a given sentence.

> **Careful Box**
>
> الغالِبُ في الاِسْتِعْمالِ القُرْآنِيِّ أَنَّ كَيْ لا تُسْتَعْمَلُ إِلّا لِلتَّعْليلِ الحَقيقِيِّ — in the Qur'an, كَيْ is used almost exclusively for real, direct تعليل (a straightforward "in order that"). اللّام, by contrast, has a wider range in Qur'anic usage: لِلتَّعْليلِ الحَقيقِيِّ, but also لِلسَّبَبِ (indicating a cause) and لِلْعاقِبَةِ (indicating an eventual outcome, even one not originally intended). This is worth keeping in mind when comparing a لِ-phrase to a كَيْ-phrase: they overlap in meaning but are not perfectly interchangeable in how the Qur'an actually uses them.
