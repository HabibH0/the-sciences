# Lesson 28: الأفعال الناقصة — كان

## Where كان sits

The عامل verbs split into قياسي and سماعي. The قياسي side is finished: the verb with one مفعول, the verb with two, the verb with three, and أفعال القلوب among them. كان belongs to the سماعي side.

> **Definition Box**
>
> السَّمَاعِيُّ: مَا لَيْسَ لَهُ قَاعِدَةٌ مُطَّرِدَةٌ، بَلْ يَتَوَقَّفُ عَلَى السَّمَاعِ
>
> A سماعي verb has no fixed قاعدة you can apply to produce more of its kind. These are words heard from the Arabs, used a certain way, and that usage is continued. Knowing them depends on سماع: hearing the Arabs use them. For a later reader, on finding them in writing.

السماعي has three أقسام.

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| الأفعال الناقصة | Verbs entering on a مبتدأ and خبر, of which كان is the first |
| أفعال المدح والذم | Verbs of praise and blame |
| صيغ التعجب | The forms of wonder |

## القسم الأول: الأفعال الناقصة

> **Definition Box**
>
> وَهِيَ أَفْعَالٌ تَدْخُلُ عَلَى المُبْتَدَأِ وَالخَبَرِ، وَتَرْفَعُ الأَوَّلَ وَيُسَمَّى اسْمَهَا، وَتَنْصِبُ الثَّانِيَ وَيُسَمَّى خَبَرَهَا

The verb enters on a جملة اسمية that is already built out of a مبتدأ and a خبر. The first stays مرفوع and is now called اسمها. The second is made منصوب and is now called خبرها.

Why the name ناقص?

> **Rule Box**
>
> يُسَمَّى الفِعْلُ النَّاقِصُ نَاقِصًا لِنُقْصَانِهِ فِي أَدَاءِ مَعْنَاهُ بِغَيْرِ خَبَرِهِ، بِخِلَافِ الفِعْلِ التَّامِّ الَّذِي يُؤَدِّي مَعْنَاهُ بِفَاعِلِهِ

A فعل تام delivers its meaning as soon as it has a فاعل. A فعل ناقص is deficient in delivering its meaning without its خبر, so the خبر is not optional decoration; the sentence is unfinished without it.

الأفعال الناقصة come in four أضرب. The first is كان وأخواتها. أفعال المقاربة and أفعال الشروع are also أفعال ناقصة.

## الضرب الأول: كان وأخواتها

> **Core Text**
>
> وَهِيَ ثَلَاثَةَ عَشَرَ: الأَوَّلُ كَانَ

## معاني كان

كان carries a ماضي meaning by default. Inside that default there are several distinct shades, and the قرآن uses them constantly.

| Meaning | What كان is saying | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| الماضي المنقطع على وجه الثبوت | A settled attribute that has since ended | ﴿وَكَانُوا عَلَيْهِ شُهَدَاءَ﴾ | And they were witnesses over it |
| الماضي المنقطع على وجه الحدوث | An event that occurred and finished | ﴿وَلَقَدْ كَانُوا عَاهَدُوا اللهَ مِنْ قَبْلُ﴾ | They had made a covenant with Allah beforehand |
| الماضي المتجدد على وجه الاستمرار | One event running over a stretch of time | كُنْتُ أَذْهَبُ يَوْمًا | I was going one day |
| الماضي المتجدد على وجه العادة | An event that recurred; their habit | ﴿كَانَا يَأْكُلَانِ الطَّعَامَ﴾ | They both used to eat food |
| الحال بمعنى ما زال | A trait that began long ago and still holds | ﴿وَكَانَ الإِنْسَانُ عَجُولًا﴾ | And man was ever hasty |
| الاستقبال | A future event stated in the ماضي to mark its certainty | ﴿كَانَتْ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتُ الفِرْدَوْسِ نُزُلًا﴾ | The gardens of Firdaws will be their welcome |

### الماضي المنقطع

منقطع means it came to an end, it stopped. It was, and no longer is. What ended may have been a settled quality or a single occurrence.

**على وجه الثبوت:** the thing that came to an end was a standing, long-term attribute.

> **Example Box**
>
> كَانُوا مُعْتَمِرِينَ
>
> They were in the state of عمرة, performing عمرة.
>
> كَانُوا فُقَرَاءَ
>
> They were poor.
>
> A person is not a فقير for one minute and then not a فقير. There is ثبوت in فقر: it is a settled condition, as بلاغة establishes. So what is منقطع here is something ثابت. They were فقراء, and now they are not.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَكَانُوا عَلَيْهِ شُهَدَاءَ﴾
>
> And they were witnesses over it.

| وَكَانُوا | عَلَيْهِ | شُهَدَاءَ |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص + واو الجماعة اسم كان | جار ومجرور | خبر كان منصوب |

Being a witness was a long-term quality of theirs. Then they passed away and no longer exist in the world, and the quality came to an end.

**على وجه الحدوث:** the thing that came to an end was an event that occurred.

> **Example Box**
>
> كَانَ قَدْ صَدَقَ
>
> He had spoken the truth.
>
> He spoke the truth and then stopped speaking. This does not say he is now speaking lies. It says the act of truthful speech occurred and finished. English carries it with *had*.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَلَقَدْ كَانُوا عَاهَدُوا اللهَ مِنْ قَبْلُ﴾
>
> They had made a covenant with Allah beforehand.

The making of the covenant happened, occurred, and stopped occurring. Whether its effects remain is a separate matter and does not bear on the وجه الحدوث.

### الماضي المتجدد

The action is not presented as a single finished point in the past. It either ran on, or it recurred.

**على وجه الاستمرار:** one event stretched over a period of time.

> **Core Text**
>
> كُنْتُ أَذْهَبُ يَوْمًا
>
> I was going one day.

> **Example Box**
>
> كَانَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ يُصَلِّي فَدَخَلَتْ عَائِشَةُ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا
>
> The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was performing صلاة, and Aisha entered.
>
> One event held over a stretch of time. He was in his صلاة, and while it was in progress she came in.

This use normally arrives with a second sentence attached: something was under way, and another act interrupted it.

**على وجه العادة:** the event happened repeatedly; it was their habit.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿كَانَا يَأْكُلَانِ الطَّعَامَ﴾
>
> They both used to eat food.

Multiple events in the past: on Monday, on Tuesday. It was their habit to eat. This use stands on its own as a piece of information, with nothing needing to follow it. Context decides which of the two shades is meant.

> **Careful Box**
>
> قَدْ لَا يَدُلُّ صِيغَةُ كَانَ يَفْعَلُ عَلَى التَّكْرَارِ
>
> كان يفعل does not always show repetition. It can point instead at something being a person's nature, at the sort of thing they did. English behaves the same way: *when we were kids we used to blow things up doing chemical experiments* may describe a single afternoon, yet it reports what used to happen, what was our way.
>
> This matters in حديث, where كان يفعل appears about حج even though the Prophet ﷺ performed حج once. The example usually given for the point is كَانَ يُصَلِّي وَهُوَ حَامِلٌ أُمَامَةَ بِنْتَ زَيْنَبَ بِنْتِ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ, meaning he ﷺ prayed while carrying Umāmah, the daughter of Zaynab, the daughter of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

### الحال بمعنى ما زال

مَا زَالَ means to remain, to continue.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَكَانَ الإِنْسَانُ عَجُولًا﴾
>
> And man was ever hasty.

| وَكَانَ | الإِنْسَانُ | عَجُولًا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص | اسم كان مرفوع | خبر كان منصوب |

عَجِلَ means to be hasty, and عَجُول is one who is hasty.

The كان here is not ماضي منقطع in the sense of *he was and no longer is*. It reaches backwards to say that this is old in him, from his beginning, from his طبيعة, from the way he was created, and that he still is so. Something long-standing is something native. He was hasty, he remains hasty.

The trait shows everywhere. Duʿā is made for an oppression to be lifted and the answer is wanted straight away, though Allah knows best when it will come, why, and how. Money is invested and the returns are wanted immediately. Sickness comes and health is wanted the same day.

### الاستقبال

كان can carry a future meaning. This is the prophetic past: a future event expressed with a ماضي form to indicate and highlight certainty.

The Arabs use it constantly in دعاء. جَزَاكَ اللهُ is ماضي in form, *Allah has rewarded you*, and future in sense: Allah will most certainly reward you. The ماضي is certain, and the future being spoken of is placed on the same footing. ﴿أَتَى أَمْرُ اللهِ﴾ says the command of Allah has come, when the decree of Allah is what will come; the ماضي form is there because it is certain.

An ordinary verb carries that certainty in its own ماضي صيغة. A جملة اسمية has no verb to do it, so كان is inserted, and that كان means *certainly*. The sense of تقدير sits inside it too.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ كَانَتْ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتُ الفِرْدَوْسِ نُزُلًا﴾
>
> Those who believe and do righteous deeds will have the gardens of Firdaws as their welcome.

نُزُل is what is laid out for an arriving guest: the welcome, the entertainment, the banquet. جنات الفردوس will certainly be the نزل Allah has prepared for them.

## مسألة: two further meanings of كان

### معنى الانبغاء

الانبغاء is being suitable, appropriate, befitting, permissible.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿مَا كَانَ لَنَا أَنْ نُشْرِكَ بِاللهِ مِنْ شَيْءٍ﴾
>
> It was not for us to associate anything with Allah.

Not suitable for us, not befitting us, not permissible for us. كان stands here in place of يَنْبَغِي. In some verses ينبغي is stated outright, in others it is dropped and كان alone carries the sense.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَمَا يَنْبَغِي لِلرَّحْمَنِ أَنْ يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدًا﴾
>
> It does not befit ar-Rahmān that He take a child.

Verses of this second kind are what establish the انبغاء reading where كان stands alone. The word itself is built on this sense: انْبَغَى is the مطاوع of بَغَى.

### معنى القدرة

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَمَا كَانَ لِنَفْسٍ أَنْ تَمُوتَ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ اللهِ﴾
>
> No soul can die except by the permission of Allah.

No نفس has the ability; it is not possible, not permissible, for any soul to die otherwise. It dies only when Allah has written that for it.

Allah normally runs this through ordinary أسباب. Put a gun in front of yourself and shoot, and you will die, but only because Allah allowed that to take effect. Were Allah to will otherwise, it would not work. Death comes only when Allah has decreed it, and adopting those أسباب at that moment is itself a sign that the death was decreed for that time.

القدرة sits close to الانبغاء. A proper study of انبغاء turns up القدرة as one of its senses.

## حذف نون كان

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجُوزُ حَذْفُ نُونِ كَلِمَةِ يَكُونُ وَتَكُونُ وَأَكُونُ وَنَكُونُ إِذَا كَانَتْ مَجْزُومَةً، وَلَيْسَ بَعْدَهَا سَاكِنٌ وَلَا ضَمِيرُ نَصْبٍ مُتَّصِلٌ

Four صيغ are named: يكون، تكون، أكون، نكون. تكون covers two forms, so five in practice. Three conditions have to hold together.

| Case | Example | Translation | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| The verb is مجزوم and nothing blocks it | ﴿لَمْ نَكُ مِنَ المُصَلِّينَ﴾ | We were not of those who prayed | The نون drops: لَمْ نَكُنْ becomes لَمْ نَكُ |
| A ساكن follows | ﴿لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا﴾ | Those who disbelieved were not … | The نون stays; the لام of الَّذِينَ is ساكن |
| A ضمير نصب متصل follows | وَلَمْ أَكُنْهُ | And I was not it | The نون stays |

> **Careful Box**
>
> What blocks the حذف in وَلَمْ أَكُنْهُ is the attached ضمير. Had the خبر been an اسم ظاهر instead of a ضمير, the حذف would have been available.

## مسألة: what the dropped نون adds to the meaning

The نحو gives the licence. There is a reason on the معنى side too, and it belongs to بلاغة.

> **Rule Box**
>
> كَثْرَةُ المَبْنَى تَدُلُّ عَلَى زِيَادَةِ المَعْنَى

A longer صيغة, more letters, more word: more meaning. Fewer letters, less meaning. قَتَلَ against قَتَّلَ is the standing illustration, since باب التفعيل shows تكثير on the strength of its extra letters.

Run the principle in reverse. Dropping the نون shortens the form, and the shortened form points at تقليل in the معنى. Where there is a possibility that Allah wishes to highlight تقليل, لم يكُ and لم أكُ are what appear.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿أَوَلَا يَذْكُرُ الإِنْسَانُ أَنَّا خَلَقْنَاهُ مِنْ قَبْلُ وَلَمْ يَكُ شَيْئًا﴾
>
> Does man not remember that We created him before, when he was nothing?

وَلَمْ يَكُنْ شَيْئًا would also say *nothing*, but more كَوْن would be left standing in the word. لَمْ يَكُ says he was nothing at all, because even the نون has gone from it.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَلَمْ أَكُ بَغِيًّا﴾
>
> And I was not unchaste.
>
> The dropped نون is what carries the *not in the least*.

## حذف كان مع اسمها

كان is sometimes dropped together with its اسم, leaving only the خبر منصوب standing.

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ يُحْذَفُ كَانَ مَعَ اسْمِهَا بَعْدَ إِنْ وَلَوِ الشَّرْطِيَّتَيْنِ

> **Core Text**
>
> «الْتَمِسْ وَلَوْ خَاتَمًا مِنْ حَدِيدٍ»
>
> Seek something, even a ring of iron.

> **Core Text**
>
> النَّاسُ مَجْزِيُّونَ بِأَعْمَالِهِمْ إِنْ خَيْرًا فَخَيْرٌ وَإِنْ شَرًّا فَشَرٌّ
>
> People are recompensed for their deeds: if good, then good; if evil, then evil.

In both, the خبر alone is left standing and stays منصوب: خَاتَمًا, and خَيْرًا and شَرًّا.

The same حذف also occurs outside إن and لو, and there the grammarians divide.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿فَآمِنُوا خَيْرًا لَكُمْ﴾

### The Kufi reading

The تقدير is فَآمِنُوا يَكُنِ الإِيمَانُ خَيْرًا لَكُمْ. آمِنُوا is أمر, and يَكُنْ is its جواب الأمر. The فعل يَكُنْ is محذوف and its اسم الإِيمَان is محذوف with it, so خَيْرًا is what remains, standing as the خبر.

| فَآمِنُوا | [يَكُنِ الإِيمَانُ] | خَيْرًا لَكُمْ |
|---|---|---|
| أمر | فعل ناقص واسمه محذوفان | خبر كان |

### The Basri reading

The Basrians do not accept the حذف. On their view كان is not omitted with its اسم at all, and خَيْرًا is explained in one of two ways.

| Function of خَيْرًا | Reading | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| مفعول مطلق for آمنوا, with the مفعول مطلق itself محذوف and خَيْرًا standing as its صفة | أَيْ آمِنُوا إِيمَانًا خَيْرًا | Believe with a belief that is better |
| مفعول به for a فعل مضمر | أَيْ وَاقْصِدُوا خَيْرًا، أَوِ ائْتُوا خَيْرًا | Aim at good, or bring good |

On the first of these, فَآمِنُوا خَيْرًا لَكُمْ is a single جملة rather than an أمر followed by a جواب الأمر: believe firmly in Allah, which is better for you. On the second, two things are being asked for: bring إيمان, and do good.
