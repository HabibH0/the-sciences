# Lesson 119: الاشتغال

الاشتغال ("preoccupation") is the fourth of the خمسة مواضع under حَذْفُ عامِلِ المَفْعولِ بِهِ, and it is the most structurally involved of the five. A noun comes first in the sentence; a verb follows; but that verb is "busy" governing a **pronoun** referring back to the noun, instead of governing the noun itself directly.

> **Definition Box**
>
> الِاشْتِغال: أَنْ يَتَقَدَّمَ اسْمٌ على فِعْلٍ عامِلٍ في ضَميرِ ذلِكَ الاسْمِ، بِحَيْثُ لَوْ فُرِّغَ الفِعْلُ مِنَ العَمَلِ في ذلِكَ الضَّميرِ المَعْمولِ لَنَصَبَ العامِلُ ذلِكَ الاسْمَ المُقَدَّمَ — a noun comes before a verb that governs a pronoun referring back to that noun, such that *if* the verb were freed from governing that pronoun, it would govern the fronted noun directly instead.
>
> ﴿وَرُسُلًا قَدْ قَصَصْناهُمْ عَلَيْكَ﴾ — "And messengers We have already narrated to you [about]." رُسُلًا comes first; قَصَصْنا governs the pronoun هُمْ (referring back to رُسُلًا), not رُسُلًا itself. Strip away هُمْ, and قَصَصْنا would naturally govern رُسُلًا directly (قَصَصْنا رُسُلًا) — so the فعل is "occupied" (مُشْتَغِل) with the pronoun instead.

## أركان الاشتغال

> **Rule Box**
>
> لِلِاشْتِغالِ ثَلاثَةُ أَرْكانٍ:
>
> **١. المَشْغول** — the عامل itself (the verb), also called المُشْتَغِل ("the one preoccupied").
>
> **٢. المَشْغول بِه** — the pronoun the verb is "busy" governing, referring back to المشغول عنه.
>
> **٣. المَشْغول عَنْه** — the fronted noun itself, the one the verb is "distracted away from."

مَسْأَلَةٌ: الِاشْتِغال is also called **ما أُضْمِرَ عامِلُهُ على شَريطَةِ التَّفْسير** — "that whose عامل is hidden, on condition that [the following verb] explains it." When المشغول عنه is treated as منصوب (see below), its true governing verb is a *dropped* one — and that dropped verb's identity is only recoverable because the mentioned verb after it (governing the pronoun) explains/mirrors what it would have been.

## حكم المشغول عنه: الأصل هو الجواز

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجوزُ في المَشْغولِ عَنْهُ أَنْ يُرْفَعَ بِالِابْتِداءِ وَأَنْ يُنْصَبَ بِفِعْلٍ مَحْذوفٍ — by default, المشغول عنه may be either مَرْفوع (as مبتدأ, with the rest of the sentence as its خبر) or مَنْصوب (governed by a dropped verb that the mentioned verb "explains").

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿والْمَوْتى يَبْعَثُهُمُ اللهُ﴾ — "And the dead, Allah will resurrect them." الْمَوْتى can be read either way: **مرفوع** as مبتدأ (والموتى / يبعثهم الله = خبر), or **منصوب** by a dropped فعل that يَبْعَثُ mirrors (وَيَبْعَثُ المَوْتى يَبْعَثُهُمُ اللهُ).

## وجوب الرفع

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجِبُ الرَّفْعُ إِذا وَقَعَ المَشْغولُ عَنْهُ بَعْدَ أَداةٍ خاصٍّ بِالِاسْمِ — if المشغول عنه follows a particle that can *only* be followed by a noun (never a verb), رفع becomes obligatory — a dropped verb could never fit there in the first place.

> **Example Box**
>
> خَلَقَ اللهُ الإِنْسانَ وَأَنْعَمَ عَلَيْهِ فَإِذا الشَّيْطانُ يَتَّبِعُهُ — "Allah created man and blessed him — then, behold, Shayṭān follows him." إِذا الفُجائِيَّة (the "surprise" إذا) can only be followed by a noun — so الشَّيْطانُ **must** be مرفوع (مبتدأ); a hidden verb is grammatically impossible right after إذا الفجائية.

## وجوب النصب

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجِبُ النَّصْبُ إِذا وَقَعَ المَشْغولُ عَنْهُ بَعْدَ أَداةٍ خاصَّةٍ بِالفِعْلِ — the mirror-image case: if المشغول عنه follows a particle that can *only* be followed by a verb, نصب becomes obligatory (since a hidden verb must be assumed to satisfy that particle's requirement). This includes:
>
> **التَّحْضيض** (particles urging someone to act, like هَلّا) and **الشَّرْط** (شرط particles).

> **Example Box**
>
> **تحضيض:** هَلّا الجَنَّةَ طَلَبْتَها — "Why didn't you seek Paradise!" هَلّا can only be followed by a فعل, so الجَنَّةَ must be منصوب (by a dropped طَلَبْتَ that طَلَبْتَها mirrors).
>
> **شرط:** إِنِ الجَنَّةَ تُرِدْها فاعْمَلْ صالِحًا — "If you want Paradise, then do good." Likewise, إِنْ demands a verb after it, so الجَنَّةَ must be منصوب.

## رجحان النصب: حيث يُفضَّل النصب دون وجوبه

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَتَرَجَّحُ النَّصْبُ في المَشْغولِ عَنْهُ (both رفع and نصب remain grammatically valid, but نصب is the *better* choice) in three situations:
>
> **١. إِنْ كانَ العامِلُ فِعْلَ طَلَبٍ** — when the governing verb is a command or prohibition. A فعل أمر doesn't naturally pair with a plain اسمية-then-فعلية structure, so نصب (echoing the imperative directly) reads more naturally.
>
> **٢. أَوْ كانَ قَبْلَ المَعْمولِ جُمْلَةٌ فِعْلِيَّةٌ** — when the sentence just before is itself a جملة فعلية. Arabic favors balance/parallelism between coordinated clauses — فعلية pairs more naturally with فعلية than with اسمية.
>
> **٣. أَوْ كانَ قَبْلَ المَعْمولِ حَرْفٌ يَغْلِبُ دُخولُهُ على الفِعْلِ** — when a preceding particle usually (though not exclusively) introduces a verb — namely **ما النّافِيَة** and **هَمْزَةُ الِاسْتِفْهام**.

> **Example Box**
>
> **١ (فعل طلب):** اللهَ اعْبُدْهُ وَرَسولَهُ اتَّبِعْهُ — "Allah, worship Him; and His Messenger, follow him" — نصب preferred since اعْبُدْ/اتَّبِعْ are imperatives.
>
> **٢ (جملة فعلية قبله):** أَعْبُدُ اللهَ وَرَسولَهُ أَتَّبِعُهُ — "I worship Allah, and His Messenger I follow" — the first clause (أَعْبُدُ اللهَ) is فعلية, so نصب keeps the second clause فعلية too, for balance.
>
> **٣ أ (ما النافية):** ما الشَّيْطانَ أَتَّبِعُهُ — "Shayṭān, I do not follow" — ما most often introduces a verbal sentence.
>
> **٣ ب (همزة الاستفهام):** ﴿أَبَشَرًا مِنّا واحِدًا نَتَّبِعُهُ﴾ — "Are we to follow a single human being from among us?" — هَمْزَةُ الِاسْتِفْهام most often introduces a جملة فعلية too, favoring نصب for بَشَرًا.

## مسألة: الفعل المحذوف قد يكون مرادفًا للفعل المذكور، لا نفسه

> **Careful Box**
>
> مَسْأَلَةٌ: قَدْ يَكونُ الفِعْلُ المَحْذوفُ في الِاشْتِغالِ فِعْلًا مُرادِفًا لِلْعامِلِ المَشْغولِ — the dropped verb explained by the mentioned verb is not always the *very same* verb repeated; sometimes it must be a **synonym** instead, when the mentioned verb's own grammar wouldn't actually fit the fronted noun directly.
>
> ﴿والظّالِمينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذابًا أَليمًا﴾ — "And for the wrongdoers, He has prepared a painful punishment." الظّالِمينَ is منصوب by اشتغال — but the dropped verb explained by أَعَدَّ cannot be أَعَدَّ itself: أَعَدَّ takes its object *through* the preposition لِـ (أَعَدَّ لَهُمْ, "prepared *for* them"), so it could never govern الظّالِمينَ directly without a لام. الفعل المحذوف، فائِدَة notes، must therefore be a genuine synonym that *does* take its object directly, with no لام needed — something like **أَوْعَدَ** ("threatened"), **عَدَّ** ("counted/reckoned"), or **كافَأَ** ("recompensed") الظّالِمينَ.
>
> A خاطِر (reflective aside) in the matn itself raises one more possibility worth sitting with: perhaps a لام could instead be understood as dropped before الظّالِمينَ, making it منصوب بِنَزْعِ الخافِضِ (by omission of the preposition — the same phenomenon that turns دَخَلْتُ فِي الدّارِ into دَخَلْتُ الدّارَ through frequent use) rather than by true اشتغال with a synonym verb. The matn leaves this as an open, tentative thought (وَاللهُ أَعْلَمُ) rather than a settled answer — a good reminder that even a well-established rule like "use a synonym" can have a live, unresolved alternative explanation worth thinking through yourself.
