# Lesson 42: حرف الجر الثاني — مِنْ

مِنْ is the second of the thirteen حروف الجر. Its root meaning is ابْتِدَاء الْغَايَة.

> **Definition Box**
>
> **مَعْنَى مِنِ ابْتِدَاءُ الْغَايَةِ**

> **Careful Box**
>
> الغاية strictly means انْتِهَاء, an ending point — so "the beginning of the ending point," taken literally, contradicts itself; an ending point has no beginning. What is actually intended is ابتداء ذي الغاية, or ابتداء المُغَيَّا — the starting point of the thing that possesses that ending point, the distance being traversed. Shortening ابتداء ذي الغاية to ابتداء الغاية is itself a مَجَاز, a loose compression rather than a literally precise phrase — though it is the wording that has become standard.

## أدوار مِن ومجرورها

| Role | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| مفعول به | أَنَا تَائِبٌ مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ | I repent from every sin |
| مفعول فيه، مكان | ﴿مِنَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ﴾ | from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām |
| مفعول فيه، زمان | ﴿أُسِّسَ عَلَى التَّقْوَى مِنْ أَوَّلِ يَوْمٍ﴾ | founded upon taqwā from the very first day |
| مفعول له | ﴿يَجْعَلُونَ أَصَابِعَهُمْ فِي آذَانِهِمْ مِنَ الصَّوَاعِقِ﴾ | they put their fingers in their ears at the thunderclaps |
| تمييز | ﴿تَرَى أَعْيُنَهُمْ تَفِيضُ مِنَ الدَّمْعِ﴾ | you see their eyes overflow with tears |
| عوض / بدل | ﴿أَرَضِيتُمْ بِالْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا مِنَ الْآخِرَةِ﴾ | content with this life in place of the Hereafter? |
| آلة | ﴿يَنْظُرُونَ مِنْ طَرَفٍ خَفِيٍّ﴾ | they look with a furtive glance |

### مفعول به

أَنَا تَائِبٌ مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ: the sin is what the repentance moves away from. تَوْبَة happens away from ذَنب — الْمُجَاوَزَة, moving past something, sits underneath the surface reading of مفعول به here.

### مفعول فيه

﴿سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي أَسْرَى بِعَبْدِهِ لَيْلًا مِنَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ﴾ places مِنَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ as المكان, the starting point of the الإسراء journey. ﴿أُسِّسَ عَلَى التَّقْوَى مِنْ أَوَّلِ يَوْمٍ﴾ places مِنْ أَوَّلِ يَوْمٍ as الزمان instead — founded on taqwā beginning from the very first day.

### مفعول له

﴿يَجْعَلُونَ أَصَابِعَهُمْ فِي آذَانِهِمْ مِنَ الصَّوَاعِقِ﴾: they put their fingers in their ears because of the thunderclaps. مِنْ here carries سَبَبِيَّة and functions as مفعول له غير صريح — the same job الباء does for تعليل, done here through مِن instead.

### تمييز

> **Careful Box**
>
> ﴿تَرَى أَعْيُنَهُمْ تَفِيضُ مِنَ الدَّمْعِ﴾ describes sincere, tender-hearted people whose eyes overflow when the Book of Allah is recited to them. Said plainly, without مِنْ, one could say تَفِيضُ دَمْعًا, with دَمْعًا as a straightforward تمييز. Replacing that direct تمييز with مِنَ الدَّمْعِ raises a real question of بلاغة — what does مِنْ add here that a bare تمييز would not? No settled answer is on offer; it is a question worth sitting with rather than one with a ready response.

### عوض، بدل، مقابلة

> **Rule Box**
>
> **إِذَا كَانَتْ مِنْ وَمَجْرُورُهَا عِوَضًا يُقَالُ: مِنْ لِلْبَدَلِ أَوِ الْمُقَابَلَةِ**

﴿أَرَضِيتُمْ بِالْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا مِنَ الْآخِرَةِ﴾: content to take this life in exchange for the Hereafter? مِنَ الْآخِرَةِ is the عِوَض, the same role الباء played for عوض in the previous lesson.

> **Careful Box**
>
> A meaning close to عِوَض but distinct from it is مَكَان, "in place of." عِوَض carries a transaction, one thing traded for another; مَكَان carries only substitution, no exchange required. ﴿وَلَوْ نَشَاءُ لَجَعَلْنَا مِنْكُمْ مَلَائِكَةً فِي الْأَرْضِ﴾ is read by some this way — مِنْكُمْ meaning بَدَلَكُمْ or مَكَانَكُمْ, "had We willed, We would have placed angels on earth in your place." عِوَض and بَدَل sit close together in outcome even though one is a transaction and the other a plain substitution.

### آلة

﴿يَنْظُرُونَ مِنْ طَرَفٍ خَفِيٍّ﴾: "they look with a furtive glance." طَرَفٍ خَفِيٍّ names the means by which the looking happens — مِنْ here carries a sense close to اِسْتِعَانَة, the same role الباء plays for الآلة.

## هل المضاف إليه دائما مجرور بالإضافة فقط؟

> **Careful Box**
>
> A word can be مجرور two ways: بِحَرْفِ الْجَرِّ, by an explicit particle, or بِالْإِضَافَةِ, simply by standing as the second term of an إضافة. One line of thinking collapses these into a single mechanism — treating even a مضاف إليه as مجرور بحرف جر مَحْذُوف, a hidden مِن or a hidden فِي rather than a separate category called إضافة. If so, the hidden particle should carry the same meaning it would carry if spoken aloud. خَاتَمُ ذَهَبٍ, "a ring of gold," becomes, underneath, خَاتَمٌ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ.
>
> This is not just relabeling. Read the ordinary way, خَاتَمٌ is مُضَاف and ذَهَبٍ is مُضَاف إِلَيْهِ — one fused unit. Read as a hidden مِنْ بَيَانِيَّة, خَاتَمٌ moves closer to a described noun and ذَهَبٍ closer to what describes it: "a ring, [made] of gold." Books of إعراب keep writing it the first way. For actually grasping and translating the phrase, though, treating ذَهَبٍ as مضاف إليه غير صريح — standing in for a dropped حرف جر — is closer to the mark.

### التنكير في هذا التركيب

> **Careful Box**
>
> When a مضاف إليه is مَعْرِفَة, the مضاف before it becomes مَعْرِفَة automatically — كِتَابُ زَيْدٍ can only mean "the book of Zayd," never "a book of Zayd's." To get the نكرة sense, the explicit حرف جر has to be brought back: كِتَابٌ لِزَيْدٍ. The same happens with مِنْ. Read as plain إضافة, أَسَفَرٌ عَذَابٍ would force عَذَاب, and the whole phrase, into معرفة. Writing أَسَفَرٌ مِنَ الْعَذَابِ instead — the حرف جر restored — keeps it نَكِرَة: "a journey [that is] a portion of punishment," not "the journey of the punishment."
>
> Once the حرف جر drops again and only the shape of إضافة remains, the same rule already met with شِبْه الجملة generally applies: attached after a نكرة, it functions as its نَعْت; attached after a معرفة, it functions as its حَال. كِتَابًا لِلْمُصَنِّفِ, "a book of the author's" — one book among the many he wrote — keeps كِتَابًا نكرة, with the shibh al-jumla functioning as its نعت, even though the books of إعراب will keep writing المُصَنِّف as simply مجرور بحرف الجر.

## مواضع تُلَقَّب حالا وهي أقرب في المعنى إلى البدل

> **Careful Box**
>
> Several Qur'anic constructions are labelled حَال in the books of إعراب even though, for meaning, they work far more like بَدَل — substituting a specific content for a vague word that came before.
>
> ﴿وَمَا تُقَدِّمُوا لِأَنْفُسِكُمْ مِنْ خَيْرٍ تَجِدُوهُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ﴾: مَا is a vague مَوْصُول, "whatever" — مِنْ خَيْرٍ specifies exactly what that "whatever" is: good. Whatever it is filed as in إعراب — بَيَان, تَبْيِين, حَالٌ لِلنَّعْتِ — in meaning it functions as a بَدَل, substituting خير for the vague مَا rather than describing a state مَا happens to be in.
>
> ﴿يُحَلَّوْنَ فِيهَا مِنْ أَسَاوِرَ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ﴾: the people of Paradise are given jewelry — مِنْ ذَهَبٍ specifies gold. Some read أَسَاوِرَ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ as مضاف ومضاف إليه غير صريح, parallel to خَاتَمُ ذَهَبٍ; others read it outright as بَدَل — "jewelry, that is, gold."
>
> ﴿فَاجْتَنِبُوا الرِّجْسَ مِنَ الْأَوْثَانِ﴾: "shun الرِّجْس, [namely] the idols" — الرجس is a vague category of impurity; مِنَ الْأَوْثَانِ makes clear exactly what impurity is meant, functioning almost as بَدَل of الرجس.
>
> In each case, if the noun before مِنْ is نَكِرَة the شبه الجملة is filed as its نَعْت; if معرفة, as its حَال — matching the same رول already met with the dropped-مِنْ إضافة construction above. But this labelled حَال does not fit the ordinary تعريف of حَال, which is meant to describe the state of the فاعل or المفعول به at the time of the فعل — none of these examples do that. What gets called حَال here is a حَال إِعْرَابِيّ, chosen for the mechanics of parsing, rather than a حَال مَعْنَوِيّ in the fuller sense of the term. The mismatch matters less for how the sentence is parsed on paper than for understanding, and translating, what the sentence actually means.
