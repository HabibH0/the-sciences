# Lesson 20: الفعل المتعدي

`الفعل المتعدي` is the verb whose effect passes from the `فاعل` to a `مفعول به`.

> **Definition Box**
>
> الفِعْلُ المُتَعَدِّي: الَّذِي يَتَجَاوَزُ أَثَرُ فَاعِلِهِ إِلَى المَفْعُولِ بِهِ

The action does not remain with the doer only. It reaches an object.

> **Example Box**
>
> حَفِظَ الطَّالِبُ الدَّرْسَ
>
> the student memorised the lesson

The verb `حَفِظَ` passes from `الطَّالِبُ` to `الدَّرْسَ`. The word `الدَّرْسَ` is the `مفعول به`.

## Visual tarkeeb: حَفِظَ الطَّالِبُ الدَّرْسَ

| حَفِظَ | الطَّالِبُ | الدَّرْسَ |
|---|---|---|
| فعل متعدي | فاعل | مفعول به |

## Other names for الفعل المتعدي

`الفعل المتعدي` may also be called:

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| فعل واقع | it falls upon the `مفعول به` |
| فعل مجاوز | its effect passes beyond the `فاعل` to the `مفعول به` |

Both names describe the same feature: the verb's effect reaches a `مفعول به`.

## متعدي بنفسه

A verb may be `متعدي بنفسه`. This means that it reaches its `مفعول به` directly, without a `حرف جر`.

> **Example Box**
>
> الحَمْدُ للهِ الَّذِي أَذْهَبَ عَنَّا الحَزَنَ
>
> all praise is for Allah, who has removed grief from us

The verb `أَذْهَبَ` takes `الحَزَنَ` as its direct `مفعول به`. The verb reaches the object directly, so it is `متعدي بنفسه`.

| Example | Translation | Type |
|---|---|---|
| أَذْهَبَ عَنَّا الحَزَنَ | He removed grief from us | متعدي بنفسه |

## متعدي بواسطة حرف الجر

A verb may also be `متعدي بواسطة حرف الجر`. This means the verb reaches an object-like meaning through a preposition.

> **Example Box**
>
> ذَهَبَ اللهُ بِنُورِهِمْ
>
> Allah took away their light

The verb `ذَهَبَ` reaches `نُورِهِمْ` through the `باء`. In this structure, the object is not a direct `مفعول به صريح`; it is reached through a `حرف جر`.

This may be called:

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مفعول به بواسطة حرف الجر | an object reached through a `حرف جر` |
| متعلق بمعنى المفعول به | connected to the meaning of a `مفعول به` |
| مفعول به غير صريح | an indirect, non-explicit `مفعول به` |

## لازم in one sense, متعدي in another

Some verbs may be called `لازم` from one angle and `متعدي` from another.

If the focus is direct grammatical structure, a verb that does not take a direct `مفعول به صريح` is called `لازم`.

If the focus is meaning, and the verb reaches an object through a `حرف جر`, it may be called `متعدي بواسطة حرف الجر`.

These are descriptions from different angles.

| Angle | Description |
|---|---|
| direct `إعراب` | the verb is `لازم` if it does not take a direct `مفعول به صريح` |
| meaning | the verb may be `متعدي` if it reaches an object-like meaning through a `حرف جر` |

## Two categories of لازم verbs

`لازم` verbs may be understood in two broad categories:

| Type | Explanation |
|---|---|
| pure لازم verbs | they do not naturally become transitive even through a `حرف جر` |
| لازم verbs that reach through a حرف جر | they do not take a direct `مفعول به`, but they may reach an object-like meaning through a `حرف جر` |

The first type includes verbs that indicate intrinsic qualities or states, such as `كَرُمَ` and `حَسُنَ`. These do not naturally pass to an object.

The second type includes verbs that are `لازم` in direct structure but may reach an object through a preposition.

## One verb in two structures

A single verb may be transitive by itself in one structure and transitive through a `حرف جر` in another.

> **Example Box**
>
> اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ
>
> guide us to the straight path

Here the verb reaches both of its objects directly. `اهْدِ` is the أمر of `هَدَى يَهْدِي هِدَايَةً`. The pronoun `نا` is the `مفعول به أول`, and `الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ` is the `مفعول به ثانٍ`. Neither of them needs a `حرف جر`.

> **Example Box**
>
> وَهَدَاهُ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ
>
> and He guided him to a straight path

Here the pronoun `هُ` is still a direct `مفعول به أول`, but the second object, `صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ`, is reached through `إِلَى`. It is a `مفعول به غير صريح`.

## Visual tarkeeb: اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ

| اهْدِ | نَا | الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ |
|---|---|---|
| فعل متعدي | مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثانٍ، متصل بالفعل مباشرة |

## Visual tarkeeb: وَهَدَاهُ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ

| وَ | هَدَى | هُ | إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ |
|---|---|---|---|
| حرف عطف | فعل | مفعول به أول | جار ومجرور، في معنى المفعول به الثاني (غير صريح) |

The same verb appears with direct transitivity in one place and with prepositional transitivity in another.

## Difference in meaning between structures

Different Arabic structures may carry different shades of meaning.

When guidance is expressed directly, as in `اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ`, the request is for being placed firmly upon the straight path itself.

When `إلى` is used, as in `وَهَدَاهُ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ`, the meaning may include direction toward an endpoint or destination.

The `حرف جر` `إلى` often indicates `انتهاء الغاية`: reaching or moving toward an endpoint. Whether the endpoint itself is included may depend on context.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Direct transitivity and transitivity through `إلى` are not always identical in nuance. The presence or absence of a `حرف جر` can affect meaning.
>
> This reading of the difference is offered as a reflection drawn from the known قواعد of the language, not as a settled ruling, والله أعلم بالصواب.

## Example of إلى and inclusion

`إلى` may indicate movement up to an endpoint. Depending on context, the endpoint may or may not be included.

For example, in discussions of washing up to the elbows, the use of `إلى` may raise the question of whether the elbows are included. If the verb directly governed the object without `إلى`, the inclusion would be more direct.

This is why the presence or absence of a `حرف جر` can matter for meaning.

## Types of الفعل المتعدي

`الفعل المتعدي` may be divided according to the number of objects it takes:

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| متعدي إلى مفعول واحد | it takes one `مفعول به` |
| متعدي إلى مفعولين | it takes two `مفعول به` elements |
| متعدي إلى ثلاثة مفاعيل | it takes three `مفعول به` elements |

## متعدي إلى مفعول واحد

This is a verb that takes one `مفعول به`.

> **Example Box**
>
> خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ
>
> He created the heavens and the earth

The verb `خَلَقَ` is transitive. It takes its object in the phrase `السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ`.

The words `السَّمَاوَاتِ` and `الأَرْضَ` are connected by `عطف`, so the structure is still treated as one object category joined by coordination.

## Visual tarkeeb: خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ

| خَلَقَ | السَّمَاوَاتِ | وَ | الأَرْضَ |
|---|---|---|---|
| فعل متعدي | مفعول به | حرف عطف | معطوف على المفعول به |
