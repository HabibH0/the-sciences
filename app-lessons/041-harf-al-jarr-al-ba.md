# Lesson 41: حرف الجر الأول — الباء

Of the thirteen حروف الجر, الباء comes first. Every use of الباء, however far it drifts, keeps some trace of its root meaning: إِلْصَاق, attachment — one thing joined to, stuck to, another.

> **Definition Box**
>
> **مَعْنَى الْبَاءِ الْإِلْصَاقُ**

That single root sense of joining surfaces across a wide range of grammatical roles that الباء together with its مجرور can fill.

## المعاني التي تحملها الباء ومجرورها

| Role | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| مفعول مطلق | ﴿خُذِ الْكِتَابَ بِقُوَّةٍ﴾ | Hold the Book firmly |
| مفعول به (غير صريح) | ﴿فَلَمَّا ذَهَبُوا بِهِ﴾ | When they took him away |
| مفعول فيه، للمكان | ﴿وَلَقَدْ نَصَرَكُمُ اللَّهُ بِبَدْرٍ﴾ | Allah helped you at Badr |
| مفعول فيه، للزمان | ﴿يُسَبِّحُونَ لَهُ بِالَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ﴾ | They glorify Him by night and by day |
| مفعول له | ﴿فَأَهْلَكْنَاهُمْ بِذُنُوبِهِمْ﴾ | So We destroyed them for their sins |
| مفعول معه | ﴿وَقَدْ دَخَلُوا بِالْكُفْرِ﴾ | They came in still bound up in كفر |
| حال | ﴿ادْخُلُوهَا بِسَلَامٍ آمِنِينَ﴾ | Enter it in peace, secure |
| تمييز | يَمْلَأُ الْخُلُقُ الْحَسَنُ الْمِيزَانَ بِالْحَسَنَاتِ | Good character fills the scale with good deeds |
| آلة | ﴿يَكْتُبُونَ الْكُتُبَ بِأَيْدِيهِمْ﴾ | They write the scripture with their own hands |
| عوض | ﴿اشْتَرَوُا الضَّلَالَةَ بِالْهُدَى﴾ | They bought misguidance in exchange for guidance |

Each row is worth pausing on, since the same short particle is doing noticeably different grammatical work each time.

### المفعول المطلق

﴿خُذِ الْكِتَابَ بِقُوَّةٍ﴾ is Allah's command to يحيى عليه الصلاة والسلام. بِقُوَّةٍ describes the manner of the أَخْذ, the taking — hold it with a firm taking, as though the sentence meant خُذِ الْكِتَابَ أَخْذًا قَوِيًّا. Read this way, الباء ومجرورها describes the verb itself.

### المفعول به

ذَهَبَ alone means simply "to go." Add الباء, and ذَهَبَ بِهِ shifts to mean "to take him away" — the sense of أَخْذ, taking, is folded into the verb once الباء is attached. الباء ومجرورها here stands in the place of a مفعول به غير صريح, an indirect direct object.

### المفعول فيه: مكان أو زمان

بِبَدْرٍ in ﴿وَلَقَدْ نَصَرَكُمُ اللَّهُ بِبَدْرٍ﴾ names the مكان — Allah's aid came at Badr. بِالَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ in the second آية plays the same role for زمان rather than مكان. الباء ومجرورها can stand for مفعول فيه of either kind.

### المفعول له

> **Careful Box**
>
> بِذُنُوبِهِمْ in ﴿فَأَهْلَكْنَاهُمْ بِذُنُوبِهِمْ﴾ gives the reason for the destruction — their sins. الباء here carries تَعْلِيل, and الباء ومجرورها functions as مفعول له غير صريح. This تعليل reading only says the sin is مُلْصَق, attached, to what happened — it says nothing by itself about whether the sin continued afterward. Compare a plain لَام of تعليل: إِنِّي أُجَازِيهِ لِإِحْسَانِهِ إِلَيَّ, "I reward him for his kindness to me," does not on its own settle whether that kindness is ongoing. Whatever more specific sense is meant comes from context, بِالْقَرِينَةِ, not from the particle alone.

### المفعول معه

﴿وَقَدْ دَخَلُوا بِالْكُفْرِ﴾ intends مُلْتَبِسًا بِالْكُفْرِ — entangled with كفر, coming and going. They came to the Prophet ﷺ still bound up in كفر and left the same way, gaining nothing from the visit. الباء here carries مُصَاحَبَة, accompaniment, and الباء ومجرورها functions as مفعول معه غير صريح.

### الحال

بِسَلَامٍ in ﴿ادْخُلُوهَا بِسَلَامٍ آمِنِينَ﴾ describes the حال, the state the entrants are in — مُلْتَبِسًا بِهِ, bound up with سلام. Packing it into الباء this way reads more smoothly than spelling out سَالِمِينَ آمِنِينَ as two parallel أحوال side by side, and it marks سلام specifically as مُلْصَق, permanently attached to the entrants at that moment — unlike آمِنِينَ, the second حال, which concerns only the absence of an external threat, خوف.

### التمييز

يَمْلَأُ الْخُلُقُ الْحَسَنُ الْمِيزَانَ بِالْحَسَنَاتِ: الباء ومجرورها specifies what fills the scale — بِالْحَسَنَاتِ. It functions as تمييز غير صريح, clarifying the vague notion of "filling" with its specific content.

### الآلة

> **Rule Box**
>
> **إِذَا كَانَتِ الْبَاءُ وَمَجْرُورُهَا آلَةً يُقَالُ: الْبَاءُ لِلِاسْتِعَانَةِ**

بِأَيْدِيهِمْ in ﴿يَكْتُبُونَ الْكُتُبَ بِأَيْدِيهِمْ﴾ names the tool by which the writing happens — الباء here carries اِسْتِعَانَة, using something as a means.

### العوض

> **Rule Box**
>
> **إِذَا كَانَتِ الْبَاءُ وَمَجْرُورُهَا عِوَضًا يُقَالُ: الْبَاءُ لِلْمُقَابَلَةِ أَوِ الْبَاءُ لِلْبَدَلِ أَوِ الْبَاءُ لِلْعِوَضِ**

﴿اشْتَرَوُا الضَّلَالَةَ بِالْهُدَى﴾: "they bought misguidance in exchange for guidance." الباء marks الْعِوَض — what was given up, or paid, in the exchange.

## إعراب فسبح بحمد ربك

This phrase recurs often enough in the Qur'an to deserve its own treatment. Three وجوه have been proposed for بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ in ﴿فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ﴾.

> **Rule Box**
>
> **فِيهِ ثَلَاثَةُ أَوْجُهٍ**

On the first, شِبْه الجملة is a حال: فَسَبِّحْ حَامِدًا رَبَّكَ — "glorify while praising your Lord," the two actions folded into one.

On the second, شِبْه الجملة is a مفعول مطلق لِلنوع describing the kind of tasbih meant, with رَبُّكَ read as its فاعل in a محل مرفوع: فَسَبِّحْ كَمَا حَمِدَ رَبُّكَ نَفْسَهُ — "glorify with the very tasbih your Lord praised Himself with." Use the wording Allah Himself has used to glorify Himself, not wording you have invented and merely imagine to be praise.

On the third, شِبْه الجملة names an آلة: سَبِّحْهُ تَسْبِيحًا يَلْزَمُ مِنْهُ الْحَمْدُ لَا تَسْبِيحًا يَلْزَمُ مِنْهُ نَقْصٌ — glorify Him with a tasbih that necessarily carries حمد along with it, not one that only removes fault without also affirming praise. تسبيح alone, negating faults, is only a partial praise; حمد, affirming excellent qualities, completes it — الباء here ties the two together so the tasbih intended is inseparably حمد as well.

> **Careful Box**
>
> A further reading takes بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ to mean بِتَوْفِيقِ رَبِّكَ, "by your Lord's enabling grace" — a مجاز مرسل, where تَوْفِيق is named حَمْد because توفيق is itself a نعمة that obligates حمد in response. The support offered is ﴿فَتَسْتَجِيبُونَ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكُمْ﴾, where بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكُمْ is likewise read as بِتَوْفِيقِ رَبِّكُمْ.

## إعراب سبحانك اللهم وبحمدك

> **Rule Box**
>
> **فِيهِ طَرِيقَانِ**

The وَاو before بِحَمْدِكَ can be read two ways: زَائِدَة, a purely grammatical addition, giving سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ بِحَمْدِكَ; or عَاطِفَة, coordinating a second جملة whose عامل has been dropped — سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ as one complete جملة, then وَبِحَمْدِكَ سَبَّحْتُكَ with سَبَّحْتُكَ elided and understood from context.

On either path, the same three أوجه already given for فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ apply again — حال, مفعول مطلق للنوع مع فاعل مرفوع محلا, or آلة.

## مشكلة الآلة والعوض

> **Careful Box**
>
> الآلة, as a معنى, is almost never found outside شِبه الجملة — it is almost always الباء بمجرورها itself that carries the sense, not a bare اسم منصوب naming the tool on its own. One exception is quoted: ضَرَبْتُهُ سَوْطًا, "I struck him — a whip," with no الباء at all. Most take سَوْطًا here as مفعول مطلق on the assumption of a dropped noun, ضَرَبْتُهُ ضَرْبَ سَوْطٍ, "I struck him a whip's worth of striking." But this sits uneasily: if that were right, the same logic ought to apply to تمييز النسبة too — نَحْوُ زَادَكَ اللَّهُ زِيَادَةَ عِلْمٍ ought to be re-read the same way, and it is not. The better view is to treat الآلة and العِوَض each as its own independent محل منصوب, standing on the same footing as تمييز, rather than folding them into مفعول مطلق by way of a hidden noun. والله أعلم.

## السبب لا يتخلف عن مسببه، والعوض ليس كذلك

> **Rule Box**
>
> **الْمُسَبَّبُ لَا يَتَخَلَّفُ عَنِ السَّبَبِ، يَعْنِي إِذَا وُجِدَ السَّبَبُ يُوجَدُ الْمُسَبَّبُ، أَمَّا الْعِوَضُ فَلَا يَلْزَمُ مِنْهُ وُجُودُ الْمُعْطَى**

A سَبَب and its مُسَبَّب are bound together in a way that عِوَض and what is received in exchange for it are not. Whenever طُلُوع الشمس, sunrise, occurs, النهار, daylight, necessarily follows — the سبب guarantees its مسبب without exception. عِوَض does not work this way: handing over an agreed price does not guarantee receiving the goods, since the other party may still refuse regardless of what is offered, just as another might hand the goods over for nothing at all out of pure generosity. قَدْ يُعْطَى الْمُعْطَى بِالْعِوَضِ، وَقَدْ يُعْطَى بِغَيْرِ الْعِوَضِ، وَقَدْ لَا يُعْطَى بِالْعِوَضِ — sometimes it is given in return for the عوض offered, sometimes given with no عوض at all, and sometimes the عوض is offered and nothing is given back. Unlike a سبب, an عوض never forces the outcome.

> **Careful Box**
>
> فَعَلَى هَذَا, الباء في ﴿ادْخُلُوا الْجَنَّةَ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ﴾ لِلْعِوَضِ — وَيُقَالُ لَهُ الْمُقَابَلَةُ — لَا السَّبَبِ.
>
> On this reading, good deeds are the عِوَض offered for الجنة, not its سبب. Were they a سبب in the strict sense, entry into Paradise would follow automatically and without exception — yet a lifetime of عمل, however long, cannot be a fitting price for an eternal reward, and Paradise itself is beyond any price a created being could pay. Reading عمل as عِوَض rather than سبب is what lets this آية sit together with the حديث «لَنْ يُدْخِلَ أَحَدًا عَمَلُهُ الْجَنَّةَ», "No one's deeds will admit him into Paradise" — a سبب cannot fail to produce its مسبب, but an عوض, as already established, does not by itself guarantee the exchange. Entry into الجنة remains, in the end, a matter of Allah's فَضْل and grace, granted to whomever He wills, with عمل standing as the عوض one offers rather than a claim one can insist upon.
