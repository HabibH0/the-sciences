# Lesson 75: حروف تنصب الفعل المضارع — لَنْ

The third of the four particles that put the فعل المضارع into نصب is لَنْ. Its core job is simple: it negates the future — but it does so with more force than a plain لا.

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّالِثُ لَنْ، وهي لِتَأْكيدِ النَّفْيِ في المُسْتَقْبَلِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿فَلَنْ أُكَلِّمَ اليَوْمَ إِنسِيًّا﴾ — "So I will not speak to any human today." Maryam عليها السلام, having taken a vow of silence, uses لَنْ rather than لا. Saying لا أُكَلِّمُ اليَوْمَ إِنسِيًّا would still mean "I am not speaking today" — but it carries none of the emphatic force that لَنْ supplies. لَنْ makes her resolve to stay silent unmistakably firm; that extra تَأْكيد (emphasis) is simply absent from a plain لا.

## معنى لَنْ: تأكيد أم تأبيد؟

Grammarians disagree over exactly how strong لَنْ's negation is.

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ تَدُلُّ على التَّأْبيدِ بِقَرينَةٍ، نحو: ﴿لَنْ يَخْلُقوا ذُبابًا﴾.

The literal, base meaning (الحقيقة) of لَنْ is تأكيد النفي — emphasizing the negation, making it certain and firm, without necessarily claiming the negation holds forever. تأبيد (permanence, "never, for all eternity") is a stronger claim, and it is only understood when a قَرينة (contextual signal) supports it — it is not لَنْ's default meaning on its own.

> **Careful Box**
>
> ﴿لَنْ يَخْلُقوا ذُبابًا﴾ — "They will never create [so much as] a fly [even if they gathered together for that purpose]" — illustrates the تأبيد reading: the قرينة here (the impossibility, for all time, of any creature besides Allah truly creating life) makes "never, under any circumstance" the intended sense, not merely "not right now." By contrast, ﴿فَلَنْ أُكَلِّمَ اليَوْمَ إِنسِيًّا﴾ is bounded by اليَوْمَ ("today") — Maryam is not vowing eternal silence, only firm silence for that day; here لَنْ carries its baseline meaning of تأكيد النفي (emphatic negation) without تأبيد. The general position taught here: تأكيد النفي is لَنْ's default, حقيقي sense, while تأبيد is a context-dependent addition, not something لَنْ inherently guarantees every time it is used.
