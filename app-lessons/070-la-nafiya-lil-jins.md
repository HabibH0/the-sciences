# Lesson 70: لا النافية للجنس

The حروف مشبّهة بالفعل — إِنَّ، أَنَّ، كَأَنَّ، لَكِنَّ، لَعَلَّ، لَيْتَ — are now finished. There is one more particle in this same family of حروف تنصب الاسم وترفع الخبر: لا, when it is used to negate an entire جنس (category/kind) at once, rather than a single individual.

> **Definition Box**
>
> لا لِنَفْيِ الجِنْسِ: لا الَّتي تَنْصِبُ اسْمَها وَتَرْفَعُ خَبَرَها، وَتَنْفي جَميعَ أَفْرادِ الجِنْسِ نَفْيًا عامًّا.

> **Example Box**
>
> لا فاعِلَ شَرٍّ فائِزٌ — "No doer of evil is successful." This لا doesn't just deny success to one particular evildoer; it sweeps across every single member of that جنس (everyone who does شرّ), denying success to all of them at once.

## لماذا تُسمّى هذه الأسماء؟

This particle carries two names, and each name captures a different half of what it's doing.

> **Rule Box**
>
> تُسَمَّى لا لِنَفْيِ الجِنْسِ لِأَنَّها تَدُلُّ على تَنْصيصِ اسْتِغْراقِ النَّفْيِ لِأَفْرادِ الجِنْسِ كُلِّها، وَتُسَمَّى لامَ التَّبْرِئَةِ لِأَنَّها تُبَرِّئُ المُبْتَدَأَ عَنِ اتِّصافِهِ بِالْخَبَرِ.

لا لنفي الجنس ("لا for negating the جنس") names what the لا does to the meaning: تنصيص, explicitly spelling out, that the negation is اسْتِغْراقٌ, an inclusion sweeping over every single فرد of the جنس — not just some of them. لا التبرئة ("the acquitting لا," from بَرَّأَ, "to declare free of") names the effect on the sentence structure: it declares the مبتدأ بريء, entirely free and unconnected, from ever being described by that خبر. لا فاعِلَ شَرٍّ فائِزٌ doesn't just say "this evildoer failed" — it severs, entirely, any possible link between فاعل الشر (as a category) and فائز.

## إهمال لا لنفي الجنس

لا لنفي الجنس does not always keep its عمل (نصب الاسم ورفع الخبر). One situation strips it away entirely.

> **Rule Box**
>
> لا تَعْمَلُ لا لِنَفْيِ الجِنْسِ إِذا وَقَعَتْ بَيْنَ الجارِّ وَالمَجْرورِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> اللَّهُمَّ أَدْخِلْنا الجَنَّةَ بِلا حِسابٍ — "O Allah, admit us to Paradise without reckoning." Here لا sits sandwiched between the حرف الجر باء and the مجرور حساب. Because it has been pulled into the middle of a جار ومجرور unit rather than heading its own مبتدأ-خبر structure, it does no عمل at all — the real عامل here is الباء, and لا is simply غير عاملة, riding along for meaning only.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Grammarians disagree over what the لام attached before لا (as in بِلا) actually is. The Kufan school treats it as an اسْم — comparable to غَيْرَ — with what follows it being a مضاف إليه; everyone else treats it as a زائد حرف, a purely decorative letter with no independent grammatical role of its own. Either way, this لا itself does not govern anything in this construction.

## شروط عمل لا لنفي الجنس

Even outside the جار-مجرور situation, لا لنفي الجنس only governs — نصب اسمها ورفع خبرها — when two conditions are both satisfied.

> **Rule Box**
>
> يُشْتَرَطُ لِعَمَلِ لا لِنَفْيِ الجِنْسِ: أَنْ يَكونَ النَّفْيُ لِلْجِنْسِ، وَأَنْ يَكونَ اسْمُها مُتَّصِلًا بِها.

**Condition one — the negation must cover the whole جنس, not a single unit.** If what's actually being denied is just one فرد (and other members of the جنس are left untouched), لا switches roles entirely and starts behaving like لَيْسَ instead.

> **Example Box**
>
> لا رَجُلٌ قائِمًا بَلْ رَجُلانِ — "Not [one] man is standing, but rather two [are]." Here the speaker isn't denying that any رجل at all is standing — quite the opposite, two of them are. What's being denied is the unit "one," الوَحْدَة, not the جنس رجل itself. Because the negation targets a single individual rather than the whole category, لا here is لا مُشَبَّهَةٌ بِلَيْسَ (رَجُلٌ stays مرفوع, exactly as it would after لَيْسَ), not لا لنفي الجنس.

**Condition two — the اسم must sit immediately after لا, with nothing between them.** If a فاصل comes between لا and its اسم, لا loses its عمل and becomes مُهْمَلَة — and once مهملة in this way, it is obligated to repeat.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿لا فيها غَوْلٌ وَلا هُمْ عَنْها يُنْزَفونَ﴾ — "There is in it no headache, nor are they intoxicated by it." فيها comes directly after the first لا, separating it from its logical اسم غَوْلٌ; because of that separation, لا cannot govern here — غَوْلٌ stays مرفوع as a plain مبتدأ, and, as required whenever لا is separated from its اسم this way, لا repeats: it appears again before the second clause (وَلا هُمْ...).

## تكرار لا لنفي الجنس

Repetition of لا لنفي الجنس interacts with the فصل (separation) condition in a precise way.

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِذا تَكَرَّرَتْ لا بِغَيْرِ فَصْلٍ جازَ فيها الإِعْمالُ وَالإِهْمالُ.

When لا repeats with nothing separating it from its اسم each time, both options stay open — the speaker may either let لا govern (إعمال) or leave it without عمل (إهمال), in both occurrences.

> **Example Box**
>
> **الإعمال**: ﴿فَلا رَفَثَ وَلا فُسوقَ وَلا جِدالَ فِي الحَجِّ﴾ — "There is [to be] no obscenity, no wickedness, and no disputing during Hajj." Each اسم (رَفَثَ، فُسوقَ، جِدالَ) is بناء على الفتح, governed by its لا exactly as expected.
>
> **الإهمال**: ﴿فَلا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلا هُمْ يَحْزَنونَ﴾ — "No fear will there be upon them, nor will they grieve." Here خَوْفٌ stays مرفوع — لا is left مهملة, not governing, even though nothing separates it from خوف.

> **Careful Box**
>
> The same رَفَثَ/فُسوقَ/جِدالَ ayah has another reading, that of أَبو جَعْفَرٍ, which reads all three words مُنَوَّن (with تنوين) rather than مبني على الفتح — direct confirmation, within the Qur'an itself, that both الإعمال والإهمال are genuinely valid options once لا has repeated without a فاصل. A simpler everyday example of this same optionality is the phrase لا حَوْلَ وَلا قُوَّةَ إِلّا بِاللَّهِ, which is likewise heard recited both with عمل (حَوْلَ، قُوَّةَ) and without it (حَوْلٌ، قُوَّةٌ) — both stay correct.

## Summary table

| Case | Ruling | Example |
|---|---|---|
| لا negates a single unit (الوحدة), not the whole جنس | Not لا لنفي الجنس — behaves like لَيْسَ instead | لا رَجُلٌ قائِمًا بَلْ رَجُلانِ |
| لا لنفي الجنس, preceded by a حرف جر | No عمل | بِلا حِسابٍ |
| لا لنفي الجنس, not preceded by a حرف جر, separated from its اسم | No عمل, and لا must repeat | لا فيها حِسابٌ وَلا سُؤالٌ |
| لا لنفي الجنس, not preceded by a حرف جر, not separated from its اسم, repeated | Both عمل and no عمل allowed | لا حِسابَ وَلا سُؤالَ / لا حِسابٌ وَلا سُؤالٌ |
| لا لنفي الجنس, not preceded by a حرف جر, not separated from its اسم, not repeated | Governs (عمل) | لا حِسابَ |
