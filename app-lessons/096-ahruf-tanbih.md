# Lesson 96: أحرف التنبيه

The seventh قسم of الحرف غير العامل covers أَحْرُفُ التَّنْبيه — particles used to draw the listener's attention, roughly "listen up!" or "take note!" before an important statement. There are three: أَلا، أَما، and ها.

> **Definition Box**
>
> القسم السّابِعُ: أَحْرُفُ التَّنْبيهِ، وهي ثَلاثَةٌ: أَلا، أَما، ها.

## الأول: ألا

> **Rule Box**
>
> الْأَوَّلُ أَلا، نحو: ﴿أَلا إِنَّ نَصْرَ اللهِ قَريبٌ﴾.

This أَلا (distinct from both أَلّا with shaddah, the توبيخ/تحضيض particle from the previous lesson, and from لا النافية) opens a statement by calling for the listener's full attention — "Listen carefully: Allah's help is indeed near."

> **Rule Box**
>
> الْغالِبُ في الجُمْلَةِ بَعْدَها أَنْ يَكونَ فيها شَيْءٌ مِنَ التَّأْكيدِ.

Because أَلا itself already signals "pay close attention, something important follows," the sentence that follows tends, as a matter of style, to carry its OWN emphasis too — the two reinforce each other rather than one doing all the work.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلا إِنَّهُمْ هُمُ الْمُفْسِدونَ﴾ — "Indeed, it is they who are the corrupters." Notice the sentence after أَلا is already loaded with emphasis of its own: إِنَّ (توكيد) plus the pronoun-of-separation هُمُ (also توكيد, isolating and stressing الْمُفْسِدونَ as the predicate). أَلا's own attention-calling force and the sentence's built-in emphasis work together, matching in intensity.

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَتَدْخُلُ على الجُمْلَتَيْنِ الِاسْمِيَّةِ والفِعْلِيَّةِ.

أَلا is not restricted to one sentence type — it can introduce either a جملة اسمية (nominal sentence) or a جملة فعلية (verbal sentence). ﴿أَلا حينَ يَسْتَغْشونَ ثِيابَهُمْ يَعْلَمُ ما يُسِرّونَ وَما يُعْلِنونَ﴾ — "Unquestionably, when they cover themselves with their clothing, He (Allah) knows what they conceal and what they declare." Here أَلا introduces a جملة فعلية (يَعْلَمُ...), showing the particle isn't tied to a nominal structure the way إِنَّ or كانَ are.

## التمييز بين ألا للتنبيه وألا للتحضيض أو العرض

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَصِحُّ الكَلامُ بِدونِ أَلا لِلتَّنْبيهِ، بِخِلافِ أَلا لِلتَّحْضيضِ أَوِ الْعَرْضِ.

Since أَلا (without shaddah) also does double duty as a تَحْضيض/عَرْض particle (تحضيض being a firm demand, عرض a gentle one), a simple grammatical test tells them apart: for تَنْبيه, the sentence remains fully grammatical and meaningful with أَلا simply DELETED — it was only adding emphasis/attention, not carrying essential grammatical weight. For تَحْضيض/عَرْض, removing أَلا breaks the sentence, because there أَلا is fused with a negative meaning essential to the verb.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلا إِنَّهُمْ هُمُ الْمُفْسِدونَ﴾ — remove أَلا and you still have a complete, correct sentence: إِنَّهُمْ هُمُ الْمُفْسِدونَ ("they are indeed the corrupters"). This confirms أَلا here is تَنْبيه — purely added emphasis, grammatically optional.
>
> ﴿أَلا تَأْكُلونَ﴾ — "Will you not eat?" Remove أَلا and you are left with تَأْكُلونَ alone ("you eat"), which does NOT carry the intended meaning at all — the "not eat" sense collapses without أَلا. This confirms أَلا here is تَحْضيض/عَرْض (a firm or gentle push), not mere تنبيه — the negation is baked into the particle itself and cannot be removed without destroying the meaning.

## الثاني: أما

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّاني أَما. والْغالِبُ أَنْ يَليَها قَسَمٌ.

This أَما (distinct from أَمّا with shaddah, the أحرف الشرط particle covered two lessons ago) is most often followed immediately by a قَسَم (oath) — drawing attention right before Allah, or something sworn by, is invoked.

> **Example Box**
>
> أَما والَّذي أَبْكى وَأَضْحَكَ والَّذي أَماتَ وَأَحْيا والَّذي أَمْرُهُ الأَمْرُ — "Now, by the One who causes weeping and laughter, the One who gives death and life, the One whose command is the [true, final] command..." أَما opens the line, immediately calling attention before a chain of oaths describing Allah's attributes.

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَقَدْ تُحْذَفُ الهَمْزَةُ.

The initial hamza of أَما can be dropped, leaving just ما — context alone must then signal that this ما is really a shortened أَما (تنبيه), not the separate negating or relative ما.

> **Example Box**
>
> ما تَرَى الدَّهْرَ قَدْ أَبادَ مَعَدًّا وَأَبادَ السَّراةَ مِنْ عَدْنانِ — "Have you not seen how time has destroyed Ma'add, and destroyed the noble leaders of 'Adnān?" Despite lacking the initial أ, the intended meaning is أَما تَرى كَيْفَ أَهْلَكَ حَوادِثُ الدَّهْرِ مَعَدًّا والرُّؤَساءَ مِنْ عَدْنانَ — "now, do you not see...?" — the hamza of أَما has simply been elided, a poetic economy recoverable from context.

## الثالث: ها الداخلة

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّالِثُ ها الدّاخِلَةُ — وَتَدْخُلُ على أَرْبَعَةِ أَشْياءَ: اسْمِ الإِشارَةِ القَريبَةِ، والضَّميرِ المَرْفوعِ، والماضي المَقْرونِ بِقَدْ، وَأَيٍّ أَوْ أَيَّةٍ قَبْلَ المُعَرَّفِ بِأَلْ.

ها التَّنْبيه attaches onto four different kinds of word, each drawing the listener's attention right before the item in question.

> **Example Box**
>
> **مع اسم الإشارة القريبة:** ﴿هٰؤُلاءِ قَوْمُنا﴾ — "These [right here] are our people." ها fuses onto أُولاءِ (the base إشارة word for "these"), sharpening the sense of nearness/immediacy: "here, THESE."
>
> **مع الضمير المرفوع:** ﴿ها أَنْتُمْ هٰؤُلاءِ﴾ — "Here you are..." / "Indeed, it is you who..." ها attaches directly before the raised (مرفوع) pronoun أَنْتُمْ, again sharpening presence/immediacy — "right here, YOU [are the ones]..."
>
> **مع الماضي المقرون بقد:** ها قَدْ غَفَرَ اللهُ لِلتّائِبينَ — "Listen: Allah has [indeed] forgiven those who repent." Here ها precedes a ماضي verb that is itself already marked by قَدْ (which, recall from the أحرف التأكيد lesson, signals تحقيق/certainty with a past-tense verb) — ها adds attention-calling on top of that certainty.
>
> **مع أيّ / أيّة قبل المعرّف بأل:** ﴿يا أَيُّهَا النّاسُ﴾ — "O mankind!" and ﴿يا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ المُطْمَئِنَّةُ﴾ — "O reassured soul!" Arabic cannot directly attach the vocative يا to a noun already defined by أَلْ (you cannot say يا النّاسُ) — so أَيّ (masculine) or أَيَّةٌ (feminine) is inserted as a bridge, and ها attaches onto that أَيّ/أَيَّة, giving the familiar formula يا أَيُّهَا / يا أَيَّتُهَا before the أَلْ-defined noun.
