# Lesson 19: الفعل اللازم

Verbs are among the main `عوامل` in Arabic grammar. A `فعل` can bring grammatical effect to what comes after it: it raises the `فاعل` or `نائب الفاعل`, and it may make certain complements `منصوب`, such as `المفاعيل` and `الحال`.

The governing verb is of two types: `الفعل القياسي` and `الفعل السماعي`. What follows is about `الفعل القياسي`, the verb whose grammatical action follows a regular rule; `الفعل السماعي` is taken up separately.

> **Core Text**
>
> القِيَاسِيُّ مَا لَهُ قَاعِدَةٌ مُطَّرِدَةٌ
>
> It runs on a consistent pattern, such as the known أبواب and conjugations.

`الفعل القياسي` is divided into two main types:

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الفعل اللازم | a verb whose effect does not pass to a direct `مفعول به` |
| الفعل المتعدي | a verb whose effect passes to a `مفعول به` |

## Definition of الفعل اللازم

> **Definition Box**
>
> الفِعْلُ اللَّازِمُ: الَّذِي لا يَتَجَاوَزُ أَثَرُ فَاعِلِهِ إِلَى المَفْعُولِ بِهِ

`الفعل اللازم` is the verb whose effect does not pass from its `فاعل` to a direct `مفعول به`.

This means the verb is complete with its `فاعل`. It does not need a direct object for its structure to be complete.

> **Example Box**
>
> وَلا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ
>
> nor will they grieve

In this example, `يَحْزَنُونَ` is `لازم`. The meaning remains with the doers themselves: they do not grieve. The verb does not pass to a direct `مفعول به`.

| Example | Translation | Type | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| يَحْزَنُونَ | they grieve | فعل لازم | its effect remains with the `فاعل` and does not pass to a direct `مفعول به` |

## Direct and indirect connection

When a verb is called `لازم`, the usual meaning is that it does not take a `مفعول به صريح`.

`مفعول به صريح` means a direct object without the mediation of a `حرف جر`.

Some `لازم` verbs may still reach an object-like meaning through a `حرف جر`. This does not make them `متعدي` in the direct grammatical sense.

> **Careful Box**
>
> A verb may be `لازم` in direct `إعراب`, while still reaching a meaning through a `حرف جر`.

## Transitivity through a حرف جر

Many `لازم` verbs may become `متعدي` in meaning through a `حرف جر`.

> **Example Box**
>
> فَاسْتَمِعُوا لَهُ

The verb `استمع` does not take a direct object here. It reaches the object-like meaning through the `لام`.

> **Example Box**
>
> يَسْتَمِعُونَ إِلَيْكَ

Here the object-like meaning is reached through `إلى`.

This is called `التعدي بواسطة حرف الجر`.

| Structure | What happens |
|---|---|
| فعل + مفعول به صريح | the verb reaches the object directly |
| فعل + حرف جر | the verb reaches an object-like meaning through the `حرف جر` |

The verb remains `لازم` with respect to direct `إعراب`, because it does not take a direct `مفعول به صريح`. In meaning, however, it may reach something through a preposition.

## Pure لازم verbs

Some `لازم` verbs are more purely intransitive. They do not naturally take even an indirect object-like meaning. These often describe intrinsic qualities or states.

Examples include:

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| كَرُمَ | to be noble |
| حَسُنَ | to be beautiful or good |
| فَقُهَ | to understand deeply |

Their meanings remain with the `فاعل` and do not pass to a `مفعول به`.

## Example: غضب

The verb `غَضِبَ` means to be angry. In direct grammatical transitivity, it is `لازم` because it does not take a direct `مفعول به صريح`.

It may still connect to another element through a `حرف جر`:

> **Example Box**
>
> غَضِبَ اللهُ عَلَيْهِمْ

The meaning reaches them through `على`.

This is also seen in:

> **Example Box**
>
> المَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ

The verb is `لازم` in direct structure, but it has a transitive meaning through the `حرف جر`.

## Other names for الفعل اللازم

`الفعل اللازم` may also be called:

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الفعل القاصر | its effect falls short of passing to a direct `مفعول به` |
| الفعل غير المتعدي | it does not pass directly to a `مفعول به` |

Both names point to the same idea: the verb does not pass directly to a `مفعول به`.

## Other complements with a لازم verb

A `لازم` verb does not take a direct `مفعول به`, but it may still be accompanied by other `منصوب` complements.

It may connect to:

| Complement | Role |
|---|---|
| مفعول فيه | gives a time or place setting |
| حال | describes the state |
| مفعول له | gives the reason or purpose |

> **Example Box**
>
> جَلَسَ الطَّالِبُ أَمَامَ المُعَلِّمِ مُتَأَدِّبًا تَبْجِيلًا لِلْعِلْمِ

## Visual tarkeeb: جَلَسَ الطَّالِبُ أَمَامَ المُعَلِّمِ مُتَأَدِّبًا تَبْجِيلًا لِلْعِلْمِ

| جَلَسَ | الطَّالِبُ | أَمَامَ المُعَلِّمِ | مُتَأَدِّبًا | تَبْجِيلًا لِلْعِلْمِ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| فعل لازم | فاعل | مفعول فيه | حال | مفعول له |

`جَلَسَ` is `فعل لازم`: it does not take a direct `مفعول به`. Still, it can appear with other complements. `أَمَامَ المُعَلِّمِ` gives the place, `مُتَأَدِّبًا` describes the state, and `تَبْجِيلًا لِلْعِلْمِ` gives the reason.
