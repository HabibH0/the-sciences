# Lesson 24: ألم ترى and أرأيت

## ألم ترى

The expression ألم ترى is built from رأى, which functions here as `فعل قلب`. In this fixed form, its meaning is:

> **Core Text**
>
> أَلَمْ تَرَ مَعْنَاهُ أَلَمْ تَعْلَمْ وَيَتَضَمَّنُ مَعْنَى التَّعَجُّبِ، وَعُبِّرَ عَنِ الْعِلْمِ بِالرُّؤْيَةِ لِأَنَّهُ أَقْوَى سَبَبِ الْعِلْمِ

*Do you not know? — with تعجب built in. Knowledge is expressed through the wording of seeing because sight is the strongest cause of knowledge.*

The reason رأى is used rather than عَلِمَ is that sight produces the most direct and certain knowledge. Of the five senses, the eye yields the most knowledge, which is why it stands in for `علم` here. Saying ألم ترى is like saying: do you not know, as surely as if you had seen it with your own eyes?

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَابِ الْفِيلِ﴾
>
> Do you not know what your Lord did to the people of the elephant?

ألم ترى is not a conjugation that can be freely substituted the way an ordinary verb can. A plain form such as لم يروا carries only its ordinary meaning, while ألم ترى and ألم تروا have become a صيغة with a meaning of their own, used specifically in مقام التعجب: situations calling for reflection, awe, or a sense of the remarkable.

## أرأيت

When أرأيت functions as a `فعل قلب`, it means:

> **Core Text**
>
> يَأْتِي أَرَأَيْتَ بِمَعْنَى أَخْبِرْنِي فِي الْأَحْوَالِ الْعَجِيبَةِ

*أرأيت comes with the meaning of أخبرني — inform me — in remarkable or astonishing situations.*

It is only used where something unusual, troubling, or striking is being raised. For an ordinary question, such as whether a door is open, أرأيت would not be appropriate.

### Structure

أرأيت as `فعل قلب` takes two objects:

1. **مفعول به أول** — the thing or person being referred to
2. **مفعول به ثاني** — typically a `جملة استفهامية` (an interrogative clause)

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿أَرَأَيْتَ مَنِ اتَّخَذَ إِلَهَهُ هَوَاهُ أَفَأَنْتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيْهِ وَكِيلًا﴾

| أَرَأَيْتَ | مَنِ اتَّخَذَ إِلَهَهُ هَوَاهُ | أَفَأَنْتَ تَكُونُ عَلَيْهِ وَكِيلًا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل قلب بمعنى أخبرني | مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثان (جملة استفهامية) |

*Inform me regarding the one who has taken his desires as his god: if he rejects your message, will you be his guardian, the one answerable for him?*

In بلاغة this verse is also discussed as a case of `قلب`. The wording is `مَنِ اتَّخَذَ إِلَهَهُ هَوَاهُ`, "the one who took his god as his desire," where the expected order would be `مَنِ اتَّخَذَ هَوَاهُ إِلَهَهُ`. The reversal is treated as a form of `تأكيد`.

### Omission of objects

In many Quranic instances, one or both objects of أرأيت are omitted and recovered from context.

**First object present, second omitted:**

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَرَأَيْتَكَ هَذَا الَّذِي كَرَّمْتَ عَلَيَّ﴾
>
> مفعول به أول: هَذَا الَّذِي كَرَّمْتَ عَلَيَّ
>
> مفعول به ثان: omitted, understood from context as `لِمَ كَرَّمْتَهُ عَلَيَّ` — why did you honour him above me?

This is the speech of Shaytan; he is the `فاعل`, and he is asking about Adam. What follows in the passage, where he asks for the chance to lead Adam's progeny astray, is the `قرينة` that supplies the missing question. There is اختلاف over the إعراب of this verse, and the analysis given here is the most straightforward one.

**Both objects omitted:**

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَرَأَيْتَكُمْ إِنْ أَتَاكُمْ عَذَابُ اللهِ أَوْ أَتَتْكُمُ السَّاعَةُ أَغَيْرَ اللهِ تَدْعُونَ﴾
>
> Both objects are omitted. The first is understood as `آلِهَتَكُمْ`, your gods. The second is the rhetorical question that follows: would those gods avail you anything?

Of the three patterns, the first is the rarest: both objects stated. Stating the first and omitting the second is common. Most common of all is أرأيت followed by إن, where both objects go and the answer implied by the following question serves as the `قرينة` for what was left out.

## أرأيتك — with كاف الخطاب

> **Core Text**
>
> يَأْتِي أَرَأَيْتَ مَعَ كَافِ الْخِطَابِ لِتَوْكِيدِ الْخِطَابِ، فَلَا يَتَغَيَّرُ ضَمِيرُهُ الْمَرْفُوعُ، بَلِ الْكَافُ

*أرأيت may come with كاف الخطاب for تأكيد of address. The raised pronoun does not change; the كاف changes.*

There is اختلاف over the إعراب of this كاف. The Kufis treat it as a `ضمير مفعول به`. The other view, easier to follow, is that it is not a `مفعول به` at all but `حرف خطاب`, a particle that emphasizes who is being addressed, in the way the كاف of `ذلك` and `ذلكم` directs the address. Adding it intensifies the address: not just *inform me* but *listen, you: inform me*.

This is `تأكيد لفظي`. In the Quran أرأيتك appears where the context calls for that extra weight, and a single passage may open with أرأيت and then repeat with أرأيتك.

| Structure | What changes | Forms |
|---|---|---|
| without كاف الخطاب | the تاء changes according to the addressee | أرأيتَ، أرأيتِ، أرأيتُما، أرأيتُم، أرأيتُنَّ |
| with كاف الخطاب | the تاء stays as أرأيتَ; the كاف changes | أرأيتَكَ، أرأيتَكِ، أرأيتَكُما، أرأيتَكُم، أرأيتَكُنَّ |

> **Careful Box**
>
> When كاف الخطاب is present, the تاء is always أرأيتَ regardless of the addressee. It is the كاف that tells you who is being addressed, not the تاء.
>
> أرأيتَكُم addresses a group of men, not one man, even though the تاء is singular masculine.
