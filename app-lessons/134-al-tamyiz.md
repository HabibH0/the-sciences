# Lesson 134: At-Tamyiz (التمييز)

التمييز ("the specifier," or the specifying accusative) is the seventh of the منصوبات, coming right after الحال in the sequence. Its job is to remove ambiguity (إبهام) that would otherwise linger in a sentence — whether the vagueness sits inside a single word (مفرد مبهم) or inside the very relation holding two parts of a sentence together (نسبة مبهمة). From counted numbers and measured quantities to sentences "transformed" from الفاعل, المفعول به, or المبتدأ purely for rhetorical effect, this lesson works through every shape التمييز can take — including the several places where, despite its name among المنصوبات, it actually turns up مجرور instead.

> **Definition Box**
>
> التَّمْييزُ: هُوَ ما يَرْفَعُ الإِبْهامَ عَنْ مُفْرَدٍ مُبْهَمٍ أَوْ نِسْبَةٍ مُبْهَمَةٍ — that which lifts the ambiguity (إبهام) from either a single, vague word (مفرد مبهم) or a vague relation (نسبة مبهمة) between two parts of a sentence. In other words: an اسم نكرة (indefinite noun) named specifically to clarify what an earlier word or clause meant — meaning that would otherwise stay genuinely unclear without it.
>
> التمييز splits into two main kinds: **تمييز المفرد**, which clarifies a single word (typically a number or a quantity/measure), and **تمييز النسبة** (also called تمييز الجملة), which clarifies a vague relation between the two sides of a sentence.

## تمييز المفرد

### العدد

> **Example Box**
>
> - **العَدَدُ الصَّريحُ** (an explicit, specific number), as in ﴿رَأَيْتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوْكَبًا﴾ — "I saw eleven stars." كَوْكَبًا is what clarifies the counted thing (المعدود) — without it, أَحَدَ عَشَرَ alone wouldn't tell you eleven of *what*.
> - **العَدَدُ المُبْهَمُ** (a vague/indefinite number, as with the interrogative كَمْ), as in ﴿كَمْ آتَيْنَاهُم مِّنْ آيَةٍ بَيِّنَةٍ﴾ — "How many a clear sign did We give them."

### المقدار وما يشبهه

> **Rule Box**
>
> المِقْدارُ ("measure/quantity") is whatever is quantified by a specific instrument (آلة). It comes in three types:
> - **الكَيْلُ** (volume/capacity), as in عندي لترٌ لبنًا — "I have a liter of milk."
> - **الوَزْنُ** (weight), as in ﴿مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا﴾ — "an atom's weight of good."
> - **المِساحَةُ** (area), as in عندي جريبٌ نخلًا — "I have a جَريب of palm trees" (الجَريب being a traditional unit of land area, roughly comparable to a feddan/acre).
>
> **ما يُشْبِهُ المِقْدارَ** ("what resembles a measure") covers what is quantified not by a real instrument but by rough sensory approximation — a hand's span, a palm's width — as in ما في السماء قدرُ راحةٍ سحابًا — "There isn't in the sky a palm's-width of cloud."
>
> **ما اُجْرِيَ مَجْرى المَقاديرِ** ("what is treated on the pattern of measures") extends the category to anything that simply needs a تمييز to clarify it, even without being a true measure at all, as in ﴿وَلَوْ جِئْنَا بِمِثْلِهِ مَدَدًا﴾ — "even if We brought the like of it in reinforcement."
>
> **ما هُوَ فَرْعٌ لِلتَّمْييزِ** ("what is a subordinate/derivative case of تمييز") appears in phrases like عندي خاتمٌ فضةً — "I have a ring [of] silver" — though this meaning is usually expressed instead through الإِضافَةُ (a construct/genitive phrase), saying خاتمُ فضةٍ rather than reaching for تمييز.

> **Careful Box**
>
> The precise line between ما اُجْرِيَ مَجْرى المَقاديرِ and ما هُوَ فَرْعٌ لِلتَّمْييزِ isn't perfectly sharp across the grammar books — sources disagree on how to classify certain examples between the two. What matters practically is recognizing what both types share: the preceding noun needs a تمييز to clarify what's actually meant by it.

### تمييز النسبة (تمييز الجملة)

> **Rule Box**
>
> تَمْييزُ النِّسْبَةِ clarifies vagueness not in a single word but in the نِسْبَة — the relation/predication holding between the two sides of a sentence. It comes in two kinds:
>
> **أ) غَيْرُ مُحَوَّلٍ** — "not transformed" (its origin was never anything else that it shifted away from), as in لله دَرُّه فارسًا — "What a horseman he is!" (أي: ما أعظم فروسيته — "how great is his horsemanship").
>
> **ب) مُحَوَّلٌ** — "transformed": its origin held a different إعراب position in the sentence, and the grammarians analyzed (حَوَّلوا) it as having shifted into تمييز for a rhetorical purpose (بَلاغِيّ) — تَوْكيد وتَخْصيص, emphasis and specification. It comes from three sources:
>
> - **مُحَوَّلٌ مِنَ الفاعِلِ** (transformed from الفاعل), as in ﴿وَاشْتَعَلَ الرَّأْسُ شَيْبًا﴾ — "and my head has flared up with white hair" — أَيْ شابَ شَيْبُ الرَّأْسِ ("the whiteness of the head has turned white"). The origin is شَيْبُ الرَّأْسِ as الفاعل; it's transformed into تمييز, and الاشتعال (the "flaring") is instead attributed to الرَّأْسُ itself, for the sake of مُبالَغة (rhetorical intensification).
>
> - **مُحَوَّلٌ مِنَ المَفْعولِ بِهِ** (transformed from المفعول به), as in ﴿فَجَّرْنَا الْأَرْضَ عُيُونًا﴾ — "and We caused the earth to burst forth with springs" — أَيْ فَجَّرْنا عُيونَ الأَرْضِ. The origin is عُيونُ الأَرْضِ as المفعول به; it's transformed into تمييز, and المفعول به becomes الأَرْضُ as a whole — again for مُبالَغة, this time intensifying the sheer abundance.
>
> - **مُحَوَّلٌ مِنَ المُبْتَدَأِ** (transformed from المبتدأ), as in ﴿أَنَا أَكْثَرُ مِنكَ مَالًا﴾ — "I have more wealth than you" — أَيْ مالي أَكْثَرُ مِنْ مالِكَ ("my wealth is more than your wealth").
>
> - **وَما في حُكْمِ المُبْتَدَأِ** — and whatever functions like المبتدأ (such as اسم كان or اسم إنّ), as in ﴿وَكَانَ الْإِنسَانُ أَكْثَرَ شَيْءٍ جَدَلًا﴾ — "and man is, of all things, most disputatious" — أَيْ جَدَلُ الإِنْسانِ أَكْثَرُ شَيْءٍ فيهِ ("man's disputatiousness is the greatest thing about him").

## مسألتان عامتان

> **Rule Box**
>
> **١)** الأَصْلُ في التَّمْييزِ أَنْ يَكونَ نَكِرَةً — the default is for التمييز to be indefinite.
>
> **٢)** التمييز is listed among المنصوبات only تَغْليبًا (by the "dominant case" — i.e. as a generalization covering the majority of instances). Otherwise, in several of its forms (as covered next, under تمييز العدد), التمييز actually shows up مَجْرور rather than مَنْصوب.

## إعراب تمييز العدد الصريح

> **Rule Box**
>
> - **مَجْرورٌ بِالإِضافَةِ** (مجرور through الإضافة construction) — either جَمْع (plural) with the numbers 3 through 10, as in ﴿ثَلَاثَةَ أَيَّامٍ﴾ — "three days"; or مُفْرَد (singular) with المِائَة ("hundred"), as in ﴿فَأَمَاتَهُ اللَّهُ مِائَةَ عَامٍ﴾ — "so Allah caused him to die for a hundred years" — and with الأَلْف ("thousand"), as in ﴿كَأَلْفِ سَنَةٍ﴾ — "like a thousand years."
> - **مَنْصوبٌ مُفْرَدٌ** for everything else — typically the numbers 11 through 99 — as in ﴿إِنّي رَأَيْتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوْكَبًا﴾ — "Indeed, I saw eleven stars."

## إعراب تمييز العدد المبهم

> **Rule Box**
>
> **مَعَ كَمِ الاسْتِفْهامِيَّةِ** (with the interrogative "how many"): مَنْصوبٌ مُفْرَدٌ, as in كَمْ يَوْمًا لَبِثْتَ — "How many days did you stay?" Its تمييز may be dropped when context makes it recoverable (عِنْدَ وُجودِ قَرينَةٍ), as in ﴿كَمْ لَبِثْتُمْ﴾ — "How long did you remain?" — أَيْ كَمْ يَوْمًا لَبِثْتُمْ (i.e., "how many days did you remain").
>
> **مَعَ كَمِ الخَبَرِيَّةِ** (the "informative" كم, expressing abundance rather than a question): مَجْرورٌ by a زائِد (extra/augmenting) preposition — either ظاهِر (overtly present), as in ﴿كَمْ مِّن فِئَةٍ قَلِيلَةٍ غَلَبَتْ فِئَةً كَثِيرَةً بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ﴾ — "How many a small party has overcome a large party by permission of Allah" — or مُقَدَّر (understood but not written), as in كم مُلوكٍ باد مُلكُهم ونعيمِ سوقةٍ بادوا — "How many kings, their kingdom perished — and the splendor of common folk, they too perished."

## بقية أحكام كم الخبرية

> **Careful Box**
>
> The default (الأَصْل) for تمييز كَمِ الخَبَرِيَّةِ is to be مُفْرَد نَكِرَة (singular, indefinite) and directly attached to كَمْ with nothing in between. But it's permitted for it to:
> - be جَمْع (plural),
> - be مُعَرَّف (definite),
> - or be مَفْصول — separated from كَمْ by something coming between them. When it's separated, the تمييز **must** be مَجْرور by an overt preposition (ظاهِر, not مُقَدَّر), as in ﴿كَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا قَبْلَهُمْ مِّنَ الْقُرُونِ﴾ — "How many generations before them have We destroyed" — here the تمييز, القُرون, is separated from كَمْ by the verb-and-adverbial phrase أَهْلَكْنا قَبْلَهُمْ sitting in between.
>
> It's also permitted to drop تمييز كم الخبرية altogether when context supplies it (عِنْدَ قَرينَةٍ تَدُلُّ عَلَيْهِ), as in the line of poetry:
>
> وكم علَّمتُه نظمَ القوافي     فلمّا قال قافيةً هجاني
>
> "How many times did I teach him the composing of verses — and when he [finally] composed a verse, he satirized me!" أي: كم مرةٍ علَّمته — meaning "how many times did I teach him," with مرةٍ (the dropped تمييز) understood.

## التمييز للتأكيد

> **Example Box**
>
> التمييز can sometimes function as مُؤَكِّد (an emphatic, confirming element) rather than as مُفَسِّر of any real ambiguity, as in ﴿إِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُورِ عِندَ اللَّهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا﴾ — "Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months." The word الشُّهور has already removed any ambiguity about what's being counted, so شَهْرًا is no longer strictly needed to lift confusion — it arrives instead simply to confirm and reinforce the meaning already established.
