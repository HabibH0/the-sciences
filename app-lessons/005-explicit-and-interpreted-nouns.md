# Lesson 5: الاسم الصريح and الاسم المؤول

## ضربا الاسم باعتبار إفراده وتركيبه

When looking at whether an `اسم` appears as a single word or as a تركيب, أسماء divide into two types:

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الاسم الصريح | the اسم by itself |
| الاسم المؤول | a حرف مصدر together with the sentence after it |

This division is about form. Sometimes the `اسم` is visible as one word. Sometimes a whole تركيب takes the place of an `اسم` in the جملة.

This is still a discussion of أسماء. What settles it is not whether the expression looks like one simple `اسم`, but whether it occupies the grammatical position of an `اسم`.

## الاسم الصريح

Definition:

> **Definition Box**
>
> الاسمُ الصَّرِيحُ: وَهُوَ الاسمُ وَحْدَهُ

An `اسم صريح` is simply the `اسم` by itself.

> **Example Box**
>
> `الصَّوْمُ` is an `اسم صريح`.  
> It appears as one `اسم` and takes its grammatical place directly.

There is no need to reinterpret the phrase. The word itself is already an `اسم`.

`الصوم` is a clear example because it is a single `اسم` and can directly take a grammatical role, such as مبتدأ, فاعل, or مفعول به depending on the جملة.

## الاسم المؤول

Definition:

> **Definition Box**
>
> الاسمُ المُؤَوَّلُ: وَهُوَ حَرْفُ مَصْدَرٍ مَعَ الجُمْلَةِ بَعْدَهَا

An `اسم مؤول` is made from:

1. a `حرف مصدر`
2. the جملة or verbal structure after it

Together, they are interpreted as a `مصدر`.

The word `مؤول` means that the construction is understood as something else for analysis. Here, the construction is understood as a مصدر meaning.

## Example: وأن تصوموا خير

Use this example:

> **Example Box**
>
> وَأَنْ تَصُومُوا خَيْرٌ

`أن` is the `حرف مصدر`, and `تصوموا` is the جملة after it. Neither part is the `اسم مؤول` on its own; the two together make it. The whole unit then fills one `اسم` slot in the جملة.

It can be understood as:

> **Meaning Box**
>
> `أن تصوموا` = `صيامكم`  
> "that you fast" = "your fasting"

This is why it can stand where a مبتدأ stands. A مبتدأ has to be an `اسم`, so the construction is interpreted as a `مصدر` before the جملة is analysed.

## Visual tarkeeb: وَأَنْ تَصُومُوا خَيْرٌ

| أَنْ تَصُومُوا | خَيْرٌ |
|---|---|
| اسم مؤول في محل رفع مبتدأ | خبر |

Inside the first cell, the parts work like this:

| أَنْ | تَصُومُوا |
|---|---|
| حرف مصدر | فعل مضارع مع فاعله |

Some grammatical positions accept only an `اسم`. When a phrase such as `أن تصوموا` appears in one of those positions, it is treated as an `اسم مؤول`, and the analysis follows the interpreted مصدر meaning while still recognising the actual words present in the جملة.
