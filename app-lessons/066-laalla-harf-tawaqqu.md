# Lesson 66: لعلّ — حرف التوقّع

The fifth of the seven حروف تنصب الاسم وترفع الخبر is لَعَلَّ.

> **Definition Box**
>
> لَعَلَّ حَرْفٌ لِلتَّوَقُّعِ

التَّوَقُّع, "expectation," is the umbrella term — but under that umbrella, لَعَلَّ can point in two different emotional directions, toward something hoped for or something feared, and beyond that it stretches into two further senses again.

## المرغوب: الترجّي

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَاتَّقُوا لَعَلَّكُمْ تُرْحَمُونَ﴾
>
> And fear Allah, that you may receive mercy.

Where لَعَلَّ points toward something المَرْغوب — something hoped for and welcomed — it is called تَرَجٍّ. الرجاء is not يَقِين; it carries the flavor of hope rather than certainty. That hope can belong either to المُتَكَلِّم or to المُخاطَب. Here, Allah — الْمُتَكَلِّم — knows with certainty that تقوى brings رحمة; the ترجّي phrasing is for the sake of المخاطب, framing the mercy as something contingent on their own تقوى, so that it reads like a genuine incentive rather than a flat guarantee.

## المكروه: الإشفاق

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ عَلَىٰ آثَارِهِمْ﴾
>
> Perhaps you would kill yourself with grief over their footsteps.

Where لَعَلَّ points toward something المكروه — something feared or dreaded rather than hoped for — it is called إِشْفاق. This آية reflects the Prophet's ﷺ intense concern for his people's guidance; the fear is that this very concern might push him toward destroying himself with grief. الإشفاق names exactly that anxious, worried undertone.

## التعليل

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَافْعَلُوا الْخَيْرَ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ﴾
>
> And do good that you may succeed.

لَعَلَّ can also work لِلتَّعْلِيلِ, giving the reason for a command, much as كَيْ or لِ would — "do good, so that you may succeed." One could argue this is simply المرغوب dressed differently — the same hope for a good outcome — but the شراح still list تعليل as its own recognized use.

## بمعنى كأنّ

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَتَتَّخِذُونَ مَصَانِعَ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَخْلُدُونَ﴾
>
> And you take for yourselves strongholds that perhaps you will abide eternally.

Here the مفسرون read لَعَلَّكُمْ two ways. One reading keeps the ordinary sense of لعلّ — hoping the strongholds might protect against threats and enemies. The other reads لَعَلَّ بِمَعْنَى كَأَنَّ: "you build these strongholds as though (كَأَنَّكُمْ) you were going to live forever" — a rebuke, since no fortress protects against death. Both readings are attested; knowing that لعلّ can carry this كأنّ sense helps make sense of a تفسير that takes it that way.

## العطف بلعلّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> تَقَعُ الجُمْلَةُ المَنْسُوخَةُ بِلَعَلَّ مَعْطُوفَةً عَلَى المَفْعُولِ لَهُ الصَّرِيحِ وَغَيْرِ الصَّرِيحِ

A جملة headed by لَعَلَّ can be joined by عطف onto a المفعول لأجله — whether that مفعول لأجله is صريح (a bare مصدر standing alone) or غَيْر صريح (linked instead by a حرف جر, e.g. لِ).

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَأَنزَلْنَا إِلَيْكَ الذِّكْرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَلَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ﴾
>
> And We sent down to you the message that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them, and that they may reflect.

الذِّكْر here was sent down for two purposes: لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ, "so that you clarify it to them" (المفعول لأجله غير الصريح, linked by لِ), and وَلَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ, "and so that they reflect" — a second purpose joined onto the first by عطف, even though it is phrased with لعلّ rather than as another بواسطة clause.

## تخفيف لعلّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجُوزُ فِي لَعَلَّ أَنْ تَقُولَ عَلَّ

لَعَلَّ can be lightened to simply عَلَّ, dropping the initial لام — a form attested in older poetry, though it has largely fallen out of use in later Arabic.
