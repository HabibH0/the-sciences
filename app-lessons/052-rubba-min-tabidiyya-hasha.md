# Lesson 52: حرف الجر الشبيه بالزائد — رُبَّ، مِنْ التبعيضية، حاشا

The thirteen حروف الجر met so far are all حرف جر أصلي — each carries its own distinct meaning, and its مجرور is always عمل, tied to a verb or something behaving like one. A second class now opens: حَرْف الجَرّ الشَّبِيه بِالزَّائِد, "the حرف جر resembling the زائد type."

> **Definition Box**
>
> حَرْفُ الجَرِّ الشَّبِيهُ بِالزَّائِدِ مَا يُفِيدُ مَعْنًى خَاصًّا، وَلَا يَكُونُ مَجْرُورُهُ مَعْمُولًا لِفِعْلٍ أَوْ شِبْهِ الفِعْلِ

This class sits between two poles: it resembles حرف الجر الأصلي because it still carries a specific meaning of its own — unlike a purely زائد particle, which adds only emphasis and no meaning. But it resembles حرف الجر الزائد in that its مجرور does not have to be عملا, governed by a verb or شبه الفعل. It occupies a middle ground: real meaning, without the syntactic attachment an أصلي particle would require. There are three such particles: رُبَّ, مِنْ in its تبعيضية use, and حَاشَا.

## الأول: رُبَّ

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّ شَهْوَةٍ تُورِثُ حُزْنًا طَوِيلًا
>
> Many a desire brings about lasting grief.

### معنى رُبَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَهِيَ لِتَكْثِيرِ مَجْرُورِهَا أَوْ تَقْلِيلِهِ، وَالقَرِينَةُ تُعَيِّنُ المُرَادَ

رُبَّ carries two opposite meanings at once: تكثير, "many," and تقليل, "few." The علماء differ on which is the أصل — some hold تكثير is the real meaning and تقليل only occasional, others hold the reverse, and a third view treats رُبَّ as مشترك, genuinely carrying both, with the قرينة, the surrounding context, alone deciding which is meant in a given sentence.

| Opinion | Core meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | تكثير (many) is basic; تقليل is occasional |
| 2 | تقليل (few) is basic; تكثير is occasional |
| 3 | مشترك — both meanings equally available; context (القرينة) decides |

In رُبَّ شَهْوَةٍ تُورِثُ حُزْنًا طَوِيلًا, the sense leans toward تكثير — many desires end in lasting grief.

### صدرها للكلام

> **Rule Box**
>
> لَا تَقَعُ رُبَّ إِلَّا فِي صَدْرِ الكَلَامِ، وَقَدْ يَتَقَدَّمُهَا حَرْفُ النِّدَاءِ

Unlike most of the أصلي particles, which can appear anywhere in a sentence, رُبَّ only ever opens one. The sole thing permitted to precede it is حرف النداء, يَا:

> **Example Box**
>
> يَا رُبَّ كَاسِيَاتٍ فِي الدُّنْيَا عَارِيَاتٍ فِي الآخِرَةِ
>
> Many a woman clothed in this world will be naked in the Hereafter.

> **Careful Box**
>
> The يَا here is not the ordinary نداء used to call a person by name — there is no addressee being summoned. Instead, the يَا is read as حرف تنبيه that adds a sense of تَحَسُّر, of lament: it signals that what follows is being said with a note of regret or warning, not simply reported as fact.

### مجرور رُبَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَلَا يَكُونُ مَجْرُورُهَا إِلَّا اسْمًا ظَاهِرًا نَكِرَةً، أَوْ ضَمِيرًا مُبْهَمًا مُفَسَّرًا بِتَمْيِيزٍ

The noun after رُبَّ is always نَكِرَة — never معرفة, and, like several particles before it, never an ordinary pronoun either. The one exception is ضمير مُبْهَم, a pronoun with no antecedent at all, which must then be clarified by a تمييز that follows it:

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّهُ رَجُلًا كَرِيمًا لَقِيتُهُ
>
> Many a generous man I have met.

Here the هُ attached to رُبَّ has no مرجع — it refers to nothing — so رَجُلًا comes right after as تمييز to spell out what that hidden pronoun actually meant.

> **Careful Box**
>
> The grammarians differ on whether this ضمير مُبهَم ever changes form to agree with its تمييز.

| School | Behavior | Example |
|---|---|---|
| البصريون | ضمير stays مفرد مذكر regardless of the تمييز | رُبَّهُ رِجَالًا لَقِيتُهُمْ / رُبَّهُ نِسَاءً لَقِيتُهُنَّ |
| الكوفيون | ضمير changes to match the تمييز in number and gender | رُبَّهُمْ رِجَالًا لَقِيتُهُمْ / رُبَّهُنَّ نِسَاءً لَقِيتُهُنَّ |

> **Rule Box**
>
> الغَالِبُ أَنْ تَكُونَ مَجْرُورُهَا مَوْصُوفًا

Most commonly, the نكرة after رُبَّ is مَوصوف, carrying a صفة of its own — either مفرد, a single word:

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّ رَجُلٍ كَرِيمٍ لَقِيتُهُ
>
> Many a generous man I have met.

Or an entire جملة functioning as the صفة:

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّ رَجُلٍ يَفْعَلُ الخَيْرَ لَقِيتُهُ
>
> Many a man who does good I have met.

### ما الكافة

> **Rule Box**
>
> وَيُزَادُ مَا الكَافَّةُ عَلَى رُبَّ فَتَكُفُّهَا عَنِ العَمَلِ، وَيَدُلُّ عَلَى تَكْثِيرِ الفِعْلِ أَوْ تَقْلِيلِهِ

مَا الكَافَّة can attach to رُبَّ, and when it does, two things change. First, it "restrains" رُبَّ — كَفَّ literally means to stop or hold back — so رُبَّ no longer requires عمل on a following noun the way it normally would. Second, the تكثير/تقليل meaning shifts target: not how many of a thing, but how often or how rarely an action happens.

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّمَا يَصُومُ زَيْدٌ
>
> Zayd often fasts.

This is what allows رُبَّمَا to attach to a full جملة فعلية, something plain رُبَّ could never do on its own. مَا الكَافَّة also opens the door to attaching before a معرفة, again something plain رُبَّ — restricted to نكرة — could never do:

> **Example Box**
>
> رُبَّمَا زَيْدٌ يَصُومُ
>
> Zayd often fasts.

> **Careful Box**
>
> When مَا الكَافَّة enters on رُبَّ, the dominant reading leans toward تقليل rather than تكثير — even though رُبَّ on its own is often read toward تكثير. This raises a puzzle in ﴿رُبَمَا يَوَدُّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَوْ كَانُوا مُسْلِمِينَ﴾, "Often those who disbelieved will wish they had been Muslims" — this wishing will happen constantly on the Day of Judgment, which looks like تكثير. Three answers are given: رُبَّمَا can carry تكثير in some contexts despite the general tendency toward تقليل; or their preoccupation with the punishment leaves them no room for regret except in scattered, infrequent moments, which is genuine تقليل; or the تقليل is used rhetorically, for تهكم, mockery and warning — the way a speaker says "perhaps you'll regret this" to someone about to err, not because regret is in doubt, but to press home that a wise person avoids the possibility of regret altogether.

### تخفيف رُبَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ تُخَفَّفُ بَاؤُهَا

The ب of رُبَّ can be lightened from a doubled بّ to a single ب, giving رُبَ instead of رُبَّ — as in the مصحف spelling رُبَمَا يَوَدُّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا.

### حذف رُبَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ تُحْذَفُ رُبَّ، وَيَبْقَى عَمَلُهَا بَعْدَ الوَاوِ كَثِيرًا، وَبَعْدَ الفَاءِ قَلِيلًا

رُبَّ itself can be dropped entirely while its عمل, its جر, stays behind — most often after a وَاو, more rarely after a فَاء:

> **Example Box**
>
> وَلَيْلٍ كَمَوْجِ الْبَحْرِ أَرْخَى سُدُولَهُ عَلَيَّ بِأَنْوَاعِ الْهُمُومِ لِيَبْتَلِيَ
>
> Many a night like the waves of the sea has let down its curtains over me with every kind of worry, to test me.

لَيْلٍ here is مجرور with no visible حرف جر governing it — the وَاو stands where رُبَّ would have stood.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Two ways of accounting for the جر are current. One holds that the وَاو itself is a حرف جر carrying the meaning of رُبَّ. The other holds that رُبَّ is simply مَحْذُوف, dropped but still silently doing its عمل, with the وَاو functioning only as the ordinary connective. Either analysis reaches the same مجرور; the difference is purely in how the sentence is diagrammed.

### مِمّا بمعنى رُبَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِذَا وَقَعَتْ مَا بَعْدَ مِنْ قَدْ تُفِيدُ مَعْنَى رُبَّ

مِمَّا — مِنْ plus مَا — ordinarily functions as a plain حرف جر followed by المَا المَوْصُولَة. But in a handful of حديث structures, context shows مِمَّا has shifted to carry the meaning of رُبَّ, "often":

> **Example Box**
>
> كَانَ ﷺ مِمَّا يُحَرِّكُ شَفَتَيْهِ
>
> The Prophet ﷺ would often move his lips.

This describes how the Prophet ﷺ, while receiving وَحْي from جبريل, would move his lips repeating the words for fear of forgetting them — until he was told لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِ لِسَانَكَ. مِمَّا يُحَرِّكُ here does not mean "from that which moves" — it means "he would often move," exactly the sense رُبَّ carries.

> **Careful Box**
>
> An alternative analysis treats this the same way as رُبَّمَا: مِنْ itself carries the meaning of رُبَّ, and the مَا attached to it is مَا الكَافَّة, restraining مِنْ from its usual عمل exactly as مَا الكَافَّة restrains رُبَّ. Either reading lands on the same meaning: "often."

## الثاني: مِنْ التبعيضية

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَلَقَدْ جَاءَكَ مِنْ نَبَإِ الْمُرْسَلِينَ﴾
>
> And there has certainly come to you some of the news of the messengers.
>
> عِنْدِي مِنْ مَاءٍ
>
> I have some water.

Here مِنْ does not carry its usual أصلي senses — ابتداء الغاية and the rest met earlier. Instead it functions as مِنْ التَّبْعِيضِيَّة, "partitive مِنْ," marking that only part of something is meant: not all the news of the messengers, only some of it; not water in general, but some water.

## الثالث: حَاشَا

> **Example Box**
>
> هَلَكَ النَّاسُ حَاشَا العَالِمَ
>
> People are ruined, except the scholar.

> **Rule Box**
>
> حَاشَا لِتَنْزِيهِ المُسْتَثْنَى مِنَ العَيْبِ وَالذَّمِّ الَّذِي نُسِبَ إِلَى مَا قَبْلَهَا

حَاشَا functions as an أداة استثناء, an exception particle, much like إِلَّا — but it carries an extra nuance.

| | إِلَّا | حَاشَا |
|---|---|---|
| Function | plain exception | exception + declares the exempted item free of the fault |
| جَاءَ النَّاسُ إِلَّا زَيْدًا | Zayd simply didn't come — no judgment implied | — |
| هَلَكَ النَّاسُ حَاشَا العَالِمَ | — | the scholar is affirmed free of the ruin/fault described |

> **Careful Box**
>
> هَلَكَ النَّاسُ حَاشَا العَالِمَ does not describe physical death — هَلَكَ here means "ruined," spiritually and otherwise, through lacking true مَعْرِفَة of الله. حَاشَا العَالِمَ does not just exclude the عالم from the group; it affirms the عالم is untouched by the very fault — the lack of معرفة — attributed to everyone else. حَاشَا's root sense, from حَاشِيَة, "the margin, the edge of a page," is "to set at a distance" — exactly its grammatical job here.

## فائدة: تصنيف الكاف

> **Careful Box**
>
> اخْتَلَفُوا فِي الكَافِ أَهِيَ أَصْلِيٌّ أَوْ شَبِيهٌ بِالزَّائِدِ فَقَوْلَانِ

الكاف, already studied earlier as a حرف جر أصلي (تشبيه), is itself a point of disagreement over exactly this classification. In الْعِلْمُ كَالنُّورِ, "knowledge is like light":

| Reading | الكاف treated as | إعراب of النور |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | حرف جر أصلي | مُتَعَلِّق بظرف مستقر محذوف (as if العلمُ ثابتٌ كالنور) |
| 2 | شَبِيه بالزائد, meaning مِثْل | فِي محل خبر, directly |

Both readings reach the same meaning; they differ only in how much extra machinery the إعراب requires to get there.
