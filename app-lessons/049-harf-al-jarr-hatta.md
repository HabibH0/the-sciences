# Lesson 49: حرف الجر التاسع — حَتَّى

حَتَّى is the ninth of the thirteen حروف الجر. Its root meaning is انْتِهَاء الْغَايَة — the same root sense already met with إِلَى, the ending point of the مُغَيَّا.

> **Definition Box**
>
> **وَهِيَ لِانْتِهَاءِ الْغَايَةِ**

## التعظيم والتحقير مع المفرد

> **Rule Box**
>
> **إِذَا كَانَ مَجْرُورُهَا مُفْرَدًا غَيْرَ زَمَانٍ وَلَا مَكَانٍ يَكُونُ مَجْرُورُهَا غَايَةً فِي الرُّتْبَةِ**

When a single word — not itself a place or a time — follows حتى, it marks the outer edge of a scale of rank, at either its top or its bottom.

> **Example Box**
>
> مَاتَ النَّاسُ حَتَّى الْأَنْبِيَاءِ
>
> People have died — even the prophets.

الْأَنْبِيَاء sits at the very top of the scale — if even prophets could not escape death, no one can. This use is لِلتَّعْظِيمِ, marking the highest extreme.

> **Example Box**
>
> يَرْزُقُ اللَّهُ حَتَّى الظَّالِمِينَ
>
> Allah provides for everyone — even the wrongdoers.

الظَّالِمِينَ sits at the opposite end — if even wrongdoers receive Allah's provision, everyone does. This use is لِلتَّحْقِيرِ, marking the lowest extreme.

## أدوار حتى ومجرورها

| Role | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| مفعول فيه، مكان | سِرْنَا حَتَّى مَكَّةَ | we traveled until Mecca |
| مفعول فيه، زمان | ﴿هِيَ حَتَّى مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ﴾ | it is [peace] until the rising of dawn |
| مفعول له | ﴿وَقَاتِلُوهُمْ حَتَّى لَا تَكُونَ فِتْنَةٌ﴾ | fight them until there is no more fitnah |

﴿وَقَاتِلُوهُمْ حَتَّى لَا تَكُونَ فِتْنَةٌ﴾: this gives the objective of the fighting, not merely its endpoint in time — the reason war is waged: to remove فِتْنَة, corruption, from the land. Read this way, حَتَّى ومجرورها functions as مفعول له غير صريح.

## مسألة: لا يكون مجرورها إلا اسما ظاهرا

> **Rule Box**
>
> **لَا يَكُونُ مَجْرُورُهَا إِلَّا اسْمًا ظَاهِرًا**

Like الكاف before it, حَتَّى never attaches to a pronoun — there is no حَتَّاهُ, only an اسم ظاهر follows it. A few poetic lines seem to break this, but these are treated as شَاذّ, irregular exceptions, not real counter-examples.

## الفرق بين حتى وإلى

> **Rule Box**
>
> **لَا يَكُونُ مَا بَعْدَ حَتَّى إِلَّا مَا كَانَ آخِرًا لِمَا قَبْلَهَا، أَوْ مُتَّصِلًا بِآخِرِهِ**

إِلَى is loose about what follows it — almost any endpoint works. حَتَّى is far stricter: what follows it must be either the actual last part of what came before, or something directly adjoining that last part.

> **Example Box**
>
> قُمْتُ اللَّيْلَ حَتَّى آخِرِهِ
>
> I stood in prayer through the night, until its very end.

آخِرِهِ is literally the last part of اللَّيْل — satisfying حتى's requirement directly.

> **Example Box**
>
> قُمْتُ اللَّيْلَ حَتَّى الصَّبَاحِ
>
> I stood in prayer through the night, until morning.

الصَّبَاح is not itself part of اللَّيْل, but it is مُتَّصِل بِآخِرِهِ, adjoining the night's last moment directly, picking up exactly where the night ends. This also satisfies the requirement.

> **Careful Box**
>
> قُمْتُ اللَّيْلَ حَتَّى نِصْفِهِ, "I stood in prayer through the night, until its half," does not work the same way. نِصْفُهُ, the halfway point, is neither the last part of اللَّيْل nor connected to its last part — it falls squarely in the middle. حَتَّى demands the true endpoint, or what directly continues from it; even a substantial midpoint like "half" falls short of that requirement.
