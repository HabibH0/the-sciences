# Lesson 21: الفعل المتعدي إلى مفعولين

`الفعل المتعدي` may take one `مفعول به`, two `مفعول به` elements, or three `مفاعيل`.

The second of these is:

> **Core Text**
>
> الضَّرْبُ الثَّانِي: الفِعْلُ المُتَعَدِّي إِلَى مَفْعُولَيْنِ

`الفعل المتعدي إلى مفعولين` is a transitive verb that takes two objects. It divides into two categories:

| Type | Explanation |
|---|---|
| مفعولاه ليسا مبتدأ وخبر في الأصل | the two objects were not originally a `مبتدأ` and `خبر` |
| مفعولاه مبتدأ وخبر في الأصل | the two objects were originally a `مبتدأ` and `خبر` |

## Two objects not originally مبتدأ and خبر

> **Core Text**
>
> فِعْلٌ لَيْسَ مَفْعُولَاهُ مُبْتَدَأً وَخَبَرًا فِي الأَصْلِ

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَهَدَيْنَاهُمَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ﴾
>
> And We guided them both to the straight path.

| وَ | هَدَيْنَا | هُمَا | الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ |
|---|---|---|---|
| حرف عطف | فعل متعدي إلى مفعولين | مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثان |

Neither object here was ever a `مبتدأ` and `خبر`. The meaning is that guidance was given to them and directed toward the straight path: `هَدَيْنَاهُم` in the sense of `أَعْطَيْنَاهُمُ الهِدَايَةَ`.

This pattern shows up in verbs of giving, granting, clothing, teaching, naming, providing, or directing something to someone. One object is the receiver; the other is the thing given.

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| أَعْطَى | to give |
| مَنَحَ | to grant |
| وَهَبَ | to gift |
| كَسَا | to clothe |
| أَلْبَسَ | to dress or clothe |
| سَمَّى | to name |
| عَلَّمَ | to teach |
| رَزَقَ | to provide |

## Omitting one or both objects

A verb that can take two objects does not have to mention both every time. One or both may be dropped when the meaning is understood from context.

> **Core Text**
>
> يَجُوزُ حَذْفُ المَفْعُولِ الأَوَّلِ ... أَوِ المَفْعُولِ الثَّانِي ... أَوْ كِلاهُمَا

| Omission | Example | Translation | What is understood |
|---|---|---|---|
| first object omitted | ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهْدِي إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ﴾ | and indeed you guide to a straight path | the guided people are understood |
| second object omitted | ﴿أُولَئِكَ الَّذِينَ هَدَاهُمُ اللهُ﴾ | those are the ones Allah guided | what they were guided to is not stated |
| both objects omitted | ﴿أَعْطَى كُلَّ شَيْءٍ خَلْقَهُ ثُمَّ هَدَى﴾ | He gave everything its creation, then guided | the context carries the omitted objects |

> **Careful Box**
>
> `متعدي إلى مفعولين` describes what a verb *can* take, not what must appear in every sentence.

### Omitting the first object

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهْدِي إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ﴾

The understood meaning is:

> وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهْدِي النَّاسَ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ

The omitted first object is `النَّاسَ`. The phrase `إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ` then carries the second object indirectly, through the `حرف جر` `إلى`.

## Direct and indirect second object

| Form | Example | Translation | Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| direct | ﴿وَهَدَيْنَاهُمَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ﴾ | and We guided them both to the straight path | `الصراط` is `مفعول به ثان صريح` |
| indirect | ﴿لَتَهْدِي إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ﴾ | you guide to a straight path | the meaning reaches the second object through `إلى` |

When the object arrives through a `حرف جر`, it is not `مفعول به صريح`. It is `مفعول به غير صريح`, also described as `مفعول بواسطة حرف الجر`.

## The meaning of إلى

`إلى` indicates `انتهاء الغاية`: movement or direction toward an endpoint. Whether the endpoint itself is included depends on context.

The same ambiguity appears in the verse of `وضوء`:

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ إِلَى المَرَافِقِ﴾
>
> and your arms up to the elbows.

Are the elbows themselves washed? The majority of scholars say yes, and treat washing them as `فرض`. One or two have held otherwise, precisely on the grounds that `إلى` does not necessarily include the endpoint.

That same ambiguity creates a real difference in meaning here:

| Wording | Translation | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ | guide us to the straight path | direct request to be placed on the path itself |
| تَهْدِي إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ | you guide to a straight path | direction toward the path; arriving on it is a further matter |

In `دعاء` the direct form is used: `اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ`, not `اهْدِنَا إِلَى الصِّرَاطِ المُسْتَقِيمِ`. Asking to be placed upon the path carries more `تضرع`, and it leaves no doubt about reaching the objective.

The description of the Prophet ﷺ uses the other form: `وَإِنَّكَ لَتَهْدِي إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ`. He shows people the path and brings them to it; whether they step onto it is their own choice.

### Omitting the second object

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أُولَئِكَ الَّذِينَ هَدَاهُمُ اللهُ﴾
>
> Those are the ones Allah guided.

The first object `هُم` is present. The second is omitted: what they were guided to is not stated.

## Two objects originally مبتدأ and خبر

> **Core Text**
>
> فِعْلٌ مَفْعُولَاهُ مُبْتَدَأٌ وَخَبَرٌ فِي الأَصْلِ

> **Example Box**
>
> ظَنَّ أَحْمَدُ خَالِدًا صَادِقًا
>
> Ahmad thought Khalid truthful.

| ظَنَّ | أَحْمَدُ | خَالِدًا | صَادِقًا |
|---|---|---|---|
| فعل متعدي إلى مفعولين | فاعل | مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثان |

The original structure was `خَالِدٌ صَادِقٌ`. Once `ظَنَّ` enters, that `مبتدأ` and `خبر` become the two objects.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَمَا أَظُنُّ السَّاعَةَ قَائِمَةً﴾
>
> And I do not think the Hour will be established.

The original structure was `السَّاعَةُ قَائِمَةٌ`. After `أَظُنُّ`, `السَّاعَةَ` is `مفعول به أول` and `قَائِمَةً` is `مفعول به ثان`.

## فعل الجارحة, فعل القلب, and فعل التحويل

> **Core Text**
>
> يُسَمَّى الفِعْلُ الَّذِي لَيْسَ مَفْعُولَاهُ مُبْتَدَأً وَخَبَرًا فِي الأَصْلِ فِعْلَ جَارِحَةٍ، وَهِيَ مَا يُزَاوَلُ بِالجَوَارِحِ مِنَ الأَفْعَالِ الحِسِّيَّةِ
>
> يُسَمَّى الفِعْلُ الَّذِي مَفْعُولَاهُ مُبْتَدَأٌ وَخَبَرٌ فِي الأَصْلِ فِعْلَ قَلْبٍ أَوْ فِعْلَ تَحْوِيلٍ

`جرح يجرح جرحا` means to wound. `الجوارح` are the limbs, the parts of the body that carry out actions, so `فعل الجارحة` covers what is performed with the limbs among the `أفعال حسية`.

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| فعل الجارحة | an outward, sensory action performed by the limbs |
| فعل القلب | a verb of inner perception, knowledge, belief, or judgement |
| فعل التحويل | a verb of making or turning something into something else |

Which of the last two labels applies depends on the meaning the verb carries.

## When the label matters

`فعل الجارحة` is the default, so most verbs are never labelled with it. `سَمِعَ`, `مَشَى`, `أَكَلَ` and `شَمَّ` are all `أفعال الجوارح`, but nobody adds the label when using them.

The label earns its place when a verb runs both ways.

| Use of the verb | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| physical seeing | رأى الجارحة | seeing with the eye |
| inner judgement or knowledge | رأى القلبية | knowing, thinking, or considering |

Because `رأى` can be either, the label has to be stated to show which reading is intended.
