# Lesson 9: أركان الجملة

## What is a جملة?

> **Definition Box**
>
> الجُمْلَةُ مَا اشْتَمَلَتْ عَلَى مُسْنَدٍ وَمُسْنَدٍ إِلَيْهِ

A `جملة` must contain `إسناد`: something is attributed to something else.

The two essential parts are:

| Term | Meaning | Common form |
|---|---|---|
| مسند إليه | the part being judged about | مبتدأ، فاعل |
| مسند | the judgement made about it | خبر، فعل |

Together, these two are called `العمدة`, the essential core of the جملة.

A string of words is not a full جملة just because it contains several words. It must contain this relationship of إسناد: something being said about something.

## المسند إليه

`المسند إليه` is the thing being spoken about. It may also be called:

> **Core Text**
>
> مَحْكُومٌ عَلَيْهِ

In `زَيْدٌ قَائِمٌ`, the statement is about Zayd. So `زيد` is the `مسند إليه`.

## المسند

`المسند` is what is attributed to the `مسند إليه`. It may also be called:

> **Core Text**
>
> مَحْكُومٌ بِهِ

In `زَيْدٌ قَائِمٌ`, standing is attributed to Zayd. So `قائم` is the `مسند`.

## Visual tarkeeb: زَيْدٌ قَائِمٌ

| زَيْدٌ | قَائِمٌ |
|---|---|
| مسند إليه / مبتدأ | مسند / خبر |

## Visual tarkeeb: قَامَ زَيْدٌ

| قَامَ | زَيْدٌ |
|---|---|
| مسند / فعل | مسند إليه / فاعل |

## فضلة

Anything outside the `مسند إليه` and `مسند` is called `فضلة` or `قيد`.

`فضلة` does not mean useless. It may be important for the meaning, but it is not part of the minimum structure needed for a جملة.

> **Example Box**
>
> `ضَرَبَ زَيْدٌ عَمْرًا`
>
> The core is `ضرب زيد`: فعل and فاعل.  
> `عمرا` is the مفعول به, so it is فضلة.

## Visual tarkeeb: ضَرَبَ زَيْدٌ عَمْرًا

| ضَرَبَ | زَيْدٌ | عَمْرًا |
|---|---|---|
| مسند / فعل | مسند إليه / فاعل | فضلة / مفعول به |

## Omitted core elements

Sometimes one of the essential parts is not spoken but is understood from context.

> **Example Box**
>
> If someone asks `هل أكلت؟`, the answer may be `لا`.
>
> The fuller meaning is `لا، لم آكل`.

The omitted words are understood. This does not remove the need for `مسند` and `مسند إليه`; it only means one part is supplied by context.

## Incomplete expressions

A single اسم such as `بيت` is not normally a complete جملة by itself. It names a thing, but it does not make a حكم.

To become a جملة, it needs إسناد:

| Example | Translation | Predication |
|---|---|---|
| هذا بيت | this is a house | `بيت` is attributed to `هذا` |
| البيت كبير | the house is big | `كبير` is attributed to `البيت` |
