# Lesson 117: الإغراء

الإغراء is the second of the خمسة مواضع under حَذْفُ عامِلِ المَفْعولِ بِهِ, and it is التحذير's mirror image: instead of warning the listener away from something disliked, it **encourages** them toward something good.

> **Definition Box**
>
> الإِغْراء: نَصْبُ الاسْمِ بِفِعْلٍ مَحْذوفٍ يُفيدُ التَّرْغيبَ والتَّشْويقَ إِلى مَرْغوبٍ لِيَفْعَلَهُ المُخاطَبُ — a noun made مَنْصوب by a dropped فعل, conveying encouragement and eagerness toward something desirable, so the listener goes and does it.

## أركان الإغراء

> **Rule Box**
>
> Exactly parallel to أركان التحذير, but flipped toward the positive:
>
> **١. فِعْلُ الإِغْراءِ المُقَدَّر** — the dropped verb: اِلْزَمْ، اُطْلُبْ، خُذْ، اِفْعَلْ, or similar.
>
> **٢. المُغْري** — the encourager: المُتَكَلِّم (the speaker).
>
> **٣. المُغْرَى** — the one being encouraged: المُخاطَب (the listener).
>
> **٤. المُغْرَى بِهِ** — the good thing being urged: whatever the speaker wants the listener to pursue.

## مسألة: وجوب حذف العامل

> **Rule Box**
>
> Exactly as with التحذير, فِعْل الإغراء must be dropped (not just optionally omitted) in two situations:
>
> **إِذا تَكَرَّرَ المُغْرَى بِهِ** — when المغرى به is repeated.
>
> **وَإِذا عُطِفَ عَلَيْهِ** — when something else is joined onto it by عطف.

> **Example Box**
>
> **تكرار:** الصَّبْرَ الصَّبْرَ — "Patience, patience!"
>
> **عطف:** العِلْمَ والْحِلْمَ — "[Pursue] knowledge and forbearance!"

## مسألة: الإغراء بلا حذف الفعل

> **Careful Box**
>
> Just as with التحذير, sometimes a sentence carries the plain meaning of encouragement without following the technical أسلوب الإغراء (which requires the verb to be dropped). ﴿يا يَحْيى خُذِ الْكِتابَ بِقُوَّةٍ﴾ — "O Yaḥyā, take the Book with strength" — genuinely encourages taking hold of the Book, but خُذِ is a fully spoken command verb, not a dropped one. This is إغراء in plain meaning, not إغراء اصطلاحًا (the specific grammatical construction this lesson defines) — the same distinction drawn for قوا أنفسكم وأهليكم نارا in the previous lesson on التحذير.

## ما يُلحَق بالتحذير والإغراء

> **Rule Box**
>
> يُلْحَقُ بِالتَّحْذيرِ والْإِغْراءِ نَصْبٌ — a small set of fixed expressions follow the very same عامل-محذوف pattern (a noun made منصوب by an unspoken verb) without themselves being a warning or an encouragement — they are simply attached (مُلْحَق) to this family of constructions because they work the same grammatical way:
>
> **مَرْحَبًا، وَأَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا** — بِتَقْديرِ أَصَبْتَ ("[you have found/attained] a spacious place, a welcoming family, level ground") — the dropped verb is أَصَبْتَ, "you have attained/found."
>
> **وَامْرَءًا وَنَفْسَهُ** — بِتَقْديرِ دَعْ ("leave a man to himself/his own affairs") — the dropped verb is دَعْ, "leave!"
