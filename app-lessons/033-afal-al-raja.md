# Lesson 33: أفعال الرجاء

أفعال الرجاء are the second group of الأفعال الناقصة, following on from كان وأخواتها. Each one is coined to show that the متكلم hopes for, or expects, something to occur. There are three: عسى, اخلولق, and حرى.

> **Definition Box**
>
> **وَهِيَ مَا وُضِعَتْ لِلدَّلَالَةِ عَلَى الرَّجَاءِ**  
> These are verbs coined to express رجاء, hope.

## الأول: عسى

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿عَسَى أَنْ يَنْفَعَنَا أَوْ نَتَّخِذَهُ وَلَدًا﴾
>
> Perhaps he will benefit us, or we may adopt him as a son.

These are the words of the wife of فرعون, آسية, about the infant موسى after he was drawn out of the river. She hopes that he will bring benefit, or, going further still, that they might take him as their own son.

## معنى عسى

> **Rule Box**
>
> **تَضَمَّنُ عَسَى مَعْنَى الْمُقَارَبَةِ، وَتَدُلُّ عَلَى...**  
> عسى carries رجاء built on a further sense, مُقَارَبَة: the outcome hoped for is close at hand. Within that it points to three distinct senses.

| Sense | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| التَّرَجِّي فِي الْمَحْبُوبِ | hope for something loved | ﴿عَسَى اللهُ أَنْ يَعْفُوَ عَنْهُمْ﴾ | Perhaps Allah will pardon them |
| الْإِشْفَاقُ مِنَ الْمَكْرُوهِ | concern or dread about something disliked | ﴿فَهَلْ عَسَيْتُمْ إِنْ تَوَلَّيْتُمْ أَنْ تُفْسِدُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَتُقَطِّعُوا أَرْحَامَكُمْ﴾ | Would you, if you turned away, cause corruption in the land and sever your ties of kinship? |
| التَّعْلِيلُ | giving a reason | ﴿وَمِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَتَهَجَّدْ بِهِ نَافِلَةً لَّكَ عَسَى أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا﴾ | And rise in the night, as an extra prayer for you; it may be that your Lord will raise you to a praised station |

> **Careful Box**
>
> عسى is not reserved for good news. When the outcome named after it is مَكْرُوه, disliked, the same verb carries إِشْفَاق — apprehension that it might happen — rather than a wish that it would.

In the تعليل example, the command to keep the night prayer of تَهَجُّد is followed by عسى giving the إِلَّة, the reason, behind it: reaching مَقَام مَحْمُود. The outcome itself is certain — it is established in the hadith that the Prophet ﷺ will attain that station — but it is phrased with عسى to give him تَرْغِيب, encouragement to persevere with the تهجد because of what it leads to.

## الفرق بين عسى ولعلَّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> **تَدُلُّ عَسَى عَلَى الْمُقَارَبَةِ دُونَ لَعَلَّ**  
> عسى carries الْمُقَارَبَة; لعلَّ does not.

عسى and لعلَّ overlap: both can carry التَّرَجِّي and, where the outcome is disliked, الْإِشْفَاق. The difference is that عسى additionally signals that the hoped-for or feared outcome is near. لعلَّ marks الرجاء or الإشفاق on its own, without saying anything about timing.

## معنى عسى إذا نُسب إلى الله

> **Rule Box**
>
> **الْغَالِبُ فِي عَسَى إِذَا أُسْنِدَ إِلَى اللهِ أَنْ تَكُونَ لِلْيَقِينِ، وَالرَّجَاءُ مِنْ قِبَلِ الْمُخَاطَبِ**  
> When عسى is attributed to Allah, the outcome is usually certain. The hoping belongs to the one addressed, not to Allah.

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿عَسَى أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا﴾

When Allah Himself says عسى, the outcome named is, in most cases, certain rather than merely hoped for. Allah's knowledge is not acquired by an event occurring; He knows what will happen before it happens, without that knowledge depending on the occurrence itself. عسى here does not describe uncertainty on Allah's part. The رجاء carried by the wording belongs to the مُخَاطَب, the one being addressed. In ﴿عَسَى أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا﴾, the fact is يَقِين: the Prophet ﷺ will reach that مقام. Expressing it through عسى gives him تَرْغِيب — continue on this course, and this is the reward waiting at the end of it.

## الثاني: اخلولق

> **Core Text**
>
> اِخْلَوْلَقَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ أَنْ يُحَافِظُوا عَلَى الصَّلَاةِ
>
> The Muslims became worthy that they should maintain the prayer.

اخلولق is rarely used. It does not occur in القرآن and is seldom found in literature, though it is recorded in the books of نحو.

> **Meaning Box**
>
> **مَعْنَى اِخْلَوْلَقَ صَارَ خَلِيقًا**  
> اخلولق is built from خَلِيق: fitting, befitting, worthy of one's character. Unlike عسى, it is never used for إِشْفَاق — it carries only رجاء, and specifically a رجاء that the outcome is something worthy and fitting. اِخْلَوْلَقَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ أَنْ يُحَافِظُوا عَلَى الصَّلَاةِ means that guarding the صلاة is a quality befitting believers, hoped for on that basis.

## الثالث: حرى

> **Core Text**
>
> حَرَى الْمُذْنِبُ أَنْ يَتُوبَ
>
> The sinner became worthy that he should repent.

> **Meaning Box**
>
> **مَعْنَى حَرَى صَارَ حَرِيًّا**  
> حرى carries a meaning close to اخلولق, from صَارَ حَرِيًّا, to become worthy of. Like اخلولق, it is seldom used in literature.

## أَنْ في خبر أفعال الرجاء

> **Rule Box**
>
> **يَكْثُرُ حَرْفُ أَنْ فِي خَبَرِ عَسَى، وَيَجِبُ فِي خَبَرِ اِخْلَوْلَقَ وَحَرَى**

A فعل مضارع on its own carries two possible senses: حَال, describing something happening now, or اِسْتِقْبَال, describing something expected in the future. Putting أَنْ before the مضارع fixes it to the اِسْتِقْبَال sense — it marks a delay, something not yet occurring. That fits أفعال الرجاء exactly, since hope by its nature points to something that has not yet happened; nobody hopes for something already the case. That is why أَنْ accompanies the خبر of عسى in most instances, and why it is required, not merely frequent, with اخلولق and حرى.

| Verb | Status of أن in the خبر |
|---|---|
| عسى | frequent, not obligatory |
| اخلولق | obligatory |
| حرى | obligatory |

## تمام أفعال الرجاء

> **Rule Box**
>
> **قَدْ تَكُونُ أَفْعَالُ الرَّجَاءِ تَامَّةً، نَحْوُ: ﴿عَسَى أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا﴾**

Like كان وأخواتها, أفعال الرجاء can also occur تامة, satisfied by a single فاعل, with no separate اسم and خبر.

Read as ناقصة:

| عَسَى | رَبُّكَ | أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص | اسم عسى | اسم مؤول في محل نصب خبر عسى |

Read as تَامَّة:

| عَسَى | أَنْ يَبْعَثَكَ رَبُّكَ مَقَامًا مَّحْمُودًا |
|---|---|
| فعل تام | اسم مؤول في محل رفع فاعل |

Both readings carry the same meaning. The difference is only in how the sentence is analysed: whether عسى takes a separate اسم and خبر, or whether the whole اسم مؤول is treated as its فاعل.
