# Lesson 22: أفعال القلوب and أفعال اليقين

`أفعال القلوب` are verbs of inner perception: knowledge, belief, assumption, or judgement. Their two objects were originally a `مبتدأ` and `خبر`.

> **Core Text**
>
> أَفْعَالُ القُلُوبِ اثْنَانِ

`أفعال القلوب` divide into two kinds according to how strongly the meaning is held in the mind:

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| أفعال اليقين | verbs that indicate certain knowledge |
| أفعال الظن / أفعال الرجحان | verbs that indicate assumption or likelihood |

## أفعال اليقين

These are verbs where the speaker treats the relationship between the first and second object as established with certainty. The seven verbs listed are:

> **Core Text**
>
> وَجَدَ، وَأَلْفَى، وَرَأَى، وَعَلِمَ، وَعَرَفَ، وَدَرَى، وَتَعَلَّمْ

## وجد

The original meaning of `وجد` is physical finding: locating something through sense perception, `وجدان حسي`. Whenever one of these verbs is examined, the original meaning has to be kept in view, because it never fully disappears from the way the verb is used.

> **Example Box**
>
> وجدتُ القلمَ — I found the pen.

When used as a `فعل قلب`, the meaning shifts from physical finding to certain knowledge. The idea is that the matter is found firmly established in the mind, as though the certainty of knowledge is like the sureness of physically holding something.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَإِنْ وَجَدْنَا أَكْثَرَهُمْ لَفَاسِقِينَ﴾
>
> We found most of them to be defiantly disobedient.

Here, the `إن` with `لام الفارقة` confirms this is a `فعل قلب` — that particle only attaches to `أفعال القلوب` and their like, so the verb cannot be `فعل جارحة`.

| أَكْثَرَهُمْ | لَفَاسِقِينَ |
|---|---|
| مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثان |

## ألفى

`ألفى` appears in the Quran in contexts of physically finding or encountering, not inner knowledge.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلْفَوْا آبَاءَهُمْ ضَالِّينَ﴾
>
> They found their fathers astray.

Whether this constitutes physical finding (`فعل جارحة`) or inner knowing (`فعل قلب`) is a matter of اختلاف, and the difference shows up in the تفاسير. The verb is listed among أفعال اليقين in the grammatical tradition, but in the Quran it is generally used for `مشاهدة` and `معاينة`, actually seeing and encountering, which has led some to say that `ألفى` is not a `فعل قلب` at all.

> **Careful Box**
>
> When encountering `ألفى` in a text, assess the context. The grammatical analysis, including whether one or two objects are present, depends on which classification is applied.

## رأى

`رأى` comes in three distinct uses, and the grammatical analysis differs for each.

### رؤية بصرية

Seeing with the eyes: `عمل العين`. This is `فعل جارحة`, and the verb takes **one** object.

> **Example Box**
>
> رأيتُ زيدًا — I saw Zayd.

### رؤية قلبية

Inner seeing: to think, consider, know, or judge something to be something. This is `عمل القلب`, so the verb is `فعل قلب` and takes **two** objects. The meaning here is carried over from `رؤية بصرية`, so it retains the force of knowing something with certainty.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنَّهُمْ يَرَوْنَهُ بَعِيدًا وَنَرَاهُ قَرِيبًا﴾
>
> They consider it far, and We consider it near.

| إِنَّهُمْ | يَرَوْنَهُ | بَعِيدًا |
|---|---|---|
| إنّ واسمها | فعل قلب وفاعله ومفعوله الأول (الهاء) | مفعول به ثان |

Another example:

> رأيتُ زيدًا عالِمًا — I considered Zayd to be knowledgeable.

The underlying structure is: زيدٌ عالمٌ. After `رأى` enters as `فعل قلب`, both terms become objects.

### رؤية منامية

Seeing in a dream. This usage is between the two above, and scholars differ on how to classify it.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنِّي أَرَى سَبْعَ بَقَرَاتٍ سِمَانٍ يَأْكُلُهُنَّ سَبْعٌ عِجَافٌ﴾

If `رأى` here is treated as `فعل قلب`:

| سَبْعَ بَقَرَاتٍ سِمَانٍ | يَأْكُلُهُنَّ سَبْعٌ عِجَافٌ |
|---|---|
| مفعول به أول | جملة في محل مفعول به ثان |

If treated as `فعل جارحة`:

| سَبْعَ بَقَرَاتٍ سِمَانٍ | يَأْكُلُهُنَّ سَبْعٌ عِجَافٌ |
|---|---|
| مفعول به (وسِمان نعت) | حال |

The حال reading is available because `سَبْعَ بَقَرَاتٍ سِمَانٍ` is `نكرة مخصوصة`, and a `نكرة مخصوصة` can take a `حال`.

The grammatical labelling changes depending on which classification is chosen, and both positions appear in the commentaries.

## علم and عرف

These two verbs are often discussed together because they are easy to confuse.

`علم` as a `فعل قلب` means to know a quality, judgement, or fact about something. It connects the `مبتدأ` to its `خبر` with certainty.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَيَعْلَمُونَ أَنَّهَا الحَقُّ﴾ — and they know that it is the truth.
>
> عَلِمْتُ زيدًا كريمًا — I knew Zayd to be noble.

`عرف`, by contrast, is normally used for recognising the ذات of something — identifying a person or thing distinctly, not predicating a quality about them.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿يَعْرِفُونَهُ كَمَا يَعْرِفُونَ أَبْنَاءَهُمْ﴾ — They recognise him as they recognise their own sons.
>
> عَرَفْتُ زيدًا — I recognised Zayd.

Because `عرف` tends toward recognition of ذات rather than predication of صفة, it is not typically used as a two-object `فعل قلب` the way `علم` is. Its listing among أفعال اليقين in the grammatical tradition is noted, but the classical examples in the Quran show it functioning as `فعل جارحة`.

| Verb | Typical use | Takes two objects? |
|---|---|---|
| علم | knowing a صفة or judgement about something | yes, as فعل قلب |
| عرف | recognising the ذات of something | typically one object |

## درى

`درى` means to know, with the added sense of coming to know after not knowing: `كنت لا أدري ثم دريت`. The knowledge is acquired through being informed, discovering, or figuring something out.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَمَا أَدْرِي مَا يُفْعَلُ بِي وَلا بِكُمْ﴾
>
> And I do not know what will be done with me or with you.

This connotation of acquired knowledge distinguishes `درى` from `علم`. Because `درى` implies a cause that precedes the knowledge, whether being told or working something out, it is not used of Allah, whose knowledge is not preceded by ignorance and does not arise through causes. Allah knows `مطلقا`, without `سبب`, so `علم` is used and `درى` is not.

## تعلَّم

`تعلَّم` in certain Arabic usages carries the meaning of knowing with certainty. It may take two objects in that use.

> **Example Box**
>
> تَعَلَّمْ شِفَاءَ النَّفْسِ قَهْرَ عَدُوِّهَا
>
> Know that the cure of the soul is subduing its enemy.

| شِفَاءَ النَّفْسِ | قَهْرَ عَدُوِّهَا |
|---|---|
| مفعول به أول | مفعول به ثان |

This usage is rare in Quranic Arabic but found in classical literature.
