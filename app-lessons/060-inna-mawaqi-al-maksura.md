# Lesson 60: إنّ ومواقعُ كسرِ همزتها

With حروف الجر complete and إلّا — the single حرف that gives نصب to one noun — finished as well, a new and much larger category opens: حُروفٌ تَنْصِبُ الِاسْمَ وَتَرْفَعُ الخَبَرَ, particles that give نَصْب to a first noun (called اسمها) and رَفْع to a second (called خبرها). Unlike إلّا, this category is not a category of one — it holds seven members. The first of the seven is إِنَّ.

> **Definition Box**
>
> إِنَّ حَرْفٌ مِنْ حُرُوفٍ سَبْعَةٍ تَنْصِبُ الِاسْمَ وَتَرْفَعُ الخَبَرَ، وَهِيَ لِلتَّأْكِيدِ، وَيُقَالُ لِلتَّحْقِيقِ

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَحِيمٌ﴾
>
> Indeed, Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.

## إنّ أم أنّ؟

The genuinely new question this لفظ raises is not what إنّ does — تأكيد, or تحقيق, is straightforward enough — but when to write إِنَّ, with هَمْزَة مَكْسورَة, and when to write أَنَّ, with هَمْزَة مَفْتوحَة. Both spellings exist; the choice between them is not free variation but follows a rule.

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِنَّ المَكْسورَةُ هَمْزَتُها تَقَعُ فِي مَوْضِعِ الجُمْلَةِ — أَيْ حَيْثُ يَبْدَأُ الكَلَامُ، حَقِيقَةً أَوْ حُكْمًا

Where إِنَّ occupies the position of a جملة — where what follows it stands as its own sentence, either genuinely at the start of the speech or treated as if it were — the هَمْزَة is كَسْر. Where the word occupies the position of a مُفْرَد instead — a single noun-equivalent, مُؤَوَّل بِمَصْدَر — the هَمْزَة is فَتْح, and the particle is أَنَّ. (أَنَّ itself, with its own مواقع, is the second of the seven أخوات and is taken up separately; this lesson maps out only where إِنَّ belongs.)

## الابتداء الحقيقي

The plainest case is حَقِيقَةُ الِابْتِداءِ — إِنَّ genuinely opens the sentence, with nothing at all preceding it.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنَّا أَنْزَلْنَا إِلَيْكَ الْكِتَابَ بِالْحَقِّ﴾
>
> Indeed, We have sent down to you the Book in truth.

## الابتداء الحكمي: بعد حرف تنبيه أو حرف زجر

الابتداء can also be حُكْمًا — إِنَّ is treated as sentence-initial even though something precedes it, because what precedes it has لا مَحَلَّ لَهُ مِنَ الْإِعْرَابِ and so does not disturb the ابتداء ruling. Two such حروف are أَلَا, a حَرْفُ تَنْبِيهٍ ("indeed, take heed"), and كَلَّا, a حَرْفُ زَجْرٍ ("no, certainly not"). Neither is فاعل, مفعول, مبتدأ, nor خبر of anything; each simply has no إعرابي role at all, which is exactly why the جملة after it is still counted as beginning.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلَا إِنَّهُمْ هُمُ الْمُفْسِدُونَ﴾ — Unquestionably, it is they who are the corrupters.
>
> ﴿كَلَّا إِنَّ الْإِنْسَانَ لَيَطْغَىٰ﴾ — No! Indeed, man does transgress.

## بعد القسم: جواب القسم

Similarly حُكْمًا, إِنَّ appears after a قَسَم, in جَوَابُ الْقَسَمِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَالْقُرْآنِ الْحَكِيمِ إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ﴾
>
> By the wise Qur'an, indeed you are among the messengers.

وَالْقُرْآنِ الْحَكِيمِ is the قَسَم itself; إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ answers it. جَوَابُ القَسَمِ is not, strictly, ابتداء — it sits inside the larger أُسْلُوبُ القَسَمِ, in the middle of the structure, not at its head. But because جواب القسم has its own independent standing within that structure, it is treated as ابتداءً حُكْمًا, and إِنَّ, not أَنَّ, follows.

## بعد القول: مقول القول

The same holds بَعْدَ القَوْلِ, after a verb of saying, where what follows is مَقُولُ القَوْلِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿قَالَ إِنِّي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ﴾
>
> He said, "Indeed, I am the servant of Allah."

Grammatically the quotation sits مَحَلًّا as the مفعول به of قَالَ — a whole جملة filling a single slot. Even so, the quotation itself stands on its own: it is not absorbed into the structure of the verb the way an ordinary مفعول is. That independence is enough to count it ابتداءً حُكْمًا, so إِنَّ follows القول just as it follows a genuine sentence-opening.

## بعد واو الحال

بَعْدَ واوِ الحالِ works the same way. The حال جملة sits mid-sentence, introduced by a واو, yet the جملة itself is self-contained.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿أَلَا إِنَّهُمْ مِّنْ إِفْكِهِمْ لَيَقُولُونَ وَلَدَ اللَّهُ وَإِنَّهُمْ لَكَاذِبُونَ﴾
>
> Unquestionably, it is out of their falsehood that they say, "Allah has begotten," and indeed they are liars.

وَإِنَّهُمْ لَكَاذِبُونَ is a حال describing the disbelievers even as they utter their claim — it occupies a مَحَلٌّ مَنْصُوب as حال, and yet is a جملة within that slot. That combination is again enough for حُكْمًا ابتداء, and إِنَّ, not أَنَّ, follows الواو الحالية.

## بعد حتى الابتدائية

حَتَّى splits into two distinct particles: a حَرْفُ جَرٍّ (studied earlier among حروف الجر) and a separate حَتَّى الِابْتِدائِيَّةُ, which opens a genuinely new sentence rather than governing a single noun.

> **Example Box**
>
> تَابَ النَّاسُ حَتَّى إِنَّ الْمُشْرِكَ تَابَ
>
> The people repented, to the point that even the polytheist repented.

Here حَتَّى الِابْتِدائِيَّةُ introduces a fresh جملة — إِنَّ الْمُشْرِكَ تَابَ stands as its own sentence, not as an object of حَتَّى — so إِنَّ, not أَنَّ, follows it.

## في الصلة

إِنَّ can also appear في الصِّلَةِ, as the صلة of an اسم موصول.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَآتَيْنَاهُ مِنَ الْكُنُوزِ مَا إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُ لَتَنُوءُ بِالْعُصْبَةِ أُولِي الْقُوَّةِ﴾
>
> And We gave him of treasures whose keys would burden a company of strong men.

> **Careful Box**
>
> One reading treats مَا here as مَوْصُولَة, with إِنَّ مَفَاتِحَهُ لَتَنُوءُ... as its صِلَة — and صِلَةُ المَوْصُولِ, being a جملة in the position of a جملة, takes إِنَّ. A second reading is possible too: مَا as نَكِرَةٌ تَامَّةٌ بِمَعْنَى شَيْءٍ ("a thing"), in which case إِنَّ وَمَا بَعْدَهَا becomes a نَعْتٌ لَهَا rather than a صِلَة. Either way the wording stays إِنَّ, but the two readings assign the جملة a different grammatical job.

## في النعت

إِنَّ appears directly في النَّعْتِ as well, describing an indefinite noun.

> **Example Box**
>
> نَظَرْتُ إِلَى رَجُلٍ إِنَّهُ صَالِحٌ
>
> I looked at a man — indeed, he is righteous.

إِنَّهُ صَالِحٌ describes رَجُلٍ; as a جملة in the position of a نعت, it again takes إِنَّ rather than أَنَّ.

## قبل اللام المعلقة للعامل اللفظي عن المعمول

Certain verbs — يَعْلَمُ among them — normally take أَنَّ as their second مفعول به, since أَنَّ وَمَا بَعْدَهَا is مُؤَوَّلٌ بِمَصْدَرٍ, a single مُفْرَد filling the مفعول slot. But when a لَامُ التَّوْكِيدِ — also called اللَّامُ المُزَحْلَقَةُ — attaches to the خبر that follows, that لام has the effect of تَعْلِيق: it suspends the عَامِل اللَّفْظِيّ of the verb from operating over the following جملة, even though the جملة still sits, مَحَلًّا, in the same مفعول slot. Once the عامل is suspended in this way, أَنَّ can no longer stand; إِنَّ takes its place.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ إِنَّكَ لَرَسُولُهُ﴾
>
> And Allah knows that you are indeed His messenger.

## بعد إلا للحصر

بَعْدَ إِلَّا لِلْحَصْرِ — where إِلَّا functions not as الاستثناء but as حَصْر, restricting the sense of the sentence rather than excepting a noun from a group — إِنَّ follows.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا قَبْلَكَ مِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ إِلَّا إِنَّهُمْ لَيَأْكُلُونَ الطَّعَامَ وَيَمْشُونَ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ﴾
>
> And We did not send before you any of the messengers except that they ate food and walked in the markets.

> **Careful Box**
>
> بِخِلَافِ إِلَّا لِلِاسْتِثْنَاءِ — plain الاستثناء, excepting one noun from another — takes أَنَّ instead: هُوَ شُجَاعٌ إِلَّا أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ بِكَرِيمٍ, "he is brave, except that he is not generous." The same word إِلَّا governs two different continuations depending on whether it restricts (حَصْر, → إِنَّ) or excepts (اسْتِثْنَاء, → أَنَّ).

## بعد حيث

بَعْدَ حَيْثُ, إِنَّ is الرَّاجِح, though the point is مُخْتَلَفٌ فِيهِ.

> **Example Box**
>
> جَلَسْتُ حَيْثُ إِنَّ عَلِيًّا جَالِسٌ
>
> I sat where Ali is sitting.

> **Careful Box**
>
> حَيْثُ is اسْمٌ مُضَافٌ, and the disagreement is over what it may be مضاف to. One view holds حَيْثُ is مضاف only to a جُمْلَة — so what follows it must itself be a full sentence, which forces إِنَّ. A second view allows حَيْثُ to be مضاف to a مُفْرَد as well, in which case what follows can be read مَحَلًّا as a مفرد, and أَنَّ becomes possible too. Because the first view is stronger, إِنَّ is راجح, but not without dissent.

## في خبر اسم عين

Finally, the choice between إِنَّ and أَنَّ can turn on what kind of thing the مبتدأ names. An اسْمُ عَيْنٍ names something concrete — a person, a group, a solid entity — while an اسْمُ مَعْنًى names something abstract. When the خبر belongs to an اسْم عَيْن, إِنَّ is used.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَالَّذِينَ هَادُوا وَالصَّابِئِينَ وَالنَّصَارَىٰ وَالْمَجُوسَ وَالَّذِينَ أَشْرَكُوا إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَفْصِلُ بَيْنَهُمْ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ﴾
>
> Indeed, those who believed and those who were Jews and the Sabians and the Christians and the Magians and those who associated with Allah — Allah will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection.

> **Careful Box**
>
> بِخِلَافِ اسْمِ الْمَعْنَى: when the مبتدأ is instead an abstract concept — الرَّجَاء, "expectation," rather than a person — أَنَّ is used: المَرْجُوُّ أَنَّهُ مَغْفُورٌ, "what is hoped is that he is forgiven." المَرْجُوُّ names an abstraction, not a solid entity, so its خبر takes أَنَّ rather than إِنَّ.
