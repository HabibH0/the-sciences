# Lesson 118: الاختصاص

الاختصاص is the third of the خمسة مواضع under حَذْفُ عامِلِ المَفْعولِ بِهِ. Its job is different from التحذير and الإغراء: it exists to **pin down exactly who a pronoun refers to**, whenever that pronoun on its own could be ambiguous.

> **Definition Box**
>
> الِاخْتِصاص: نَصْبُ الاسْمِ بِفِعْلٍ مَحْذوفٍ يُعَيِّنُ المُرادَ مِنَ الضَّميرِ قَبْلَهُ, مِثْلُ أَخُصُّ أَوْ أَعْني — a noun made مَنْصوب by a dropped verb (typically أَخُصُّ, "I specifically mean," or أَعْني, "I mean") that clarifies exactly who is intended by a pronoun just before it.

## لماذا نحتاج الاختصاص

Pronouns like نَحْنُ or a plural كُمْ/أَنْتُم can leave real ambiguity about exactly *who* is included. الاختصاص resolves this the same way تمييز clarifies an otherwise vague noun: by adding a specifying noun immediately after the pronoun, understood as the object of a dropped أَخُصُّ/أَعْني.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿رَحْمَتُ اللهِ وَبَرَكاتُهُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ﴾ — "The mercy of Allah and His blessings be upon you, O people of the house." عَلَيْكُمْ on its own could refer broadly to الصَّحابة, المُهاجِرون, or الأَنْصار — أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ (منصوب) pins the pronoun down specifically: أَعْني أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ, "I specifically mean the people of the house."
>
> A parallel case from حديث: نَحْنُ مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْبِياءِ — "We — the community of prophets..." مَعْشَرَ الأَنْبِياءِ specifies exactly who نَحْنُ refers to, on the same pattern.

## مسألة: الاختصاص بلفظ أيّها/أيّتها

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ يَكونُ الِاخْتِصاصُ بِلَفْظِ أَيُّها وَأَيَّتُها بِغَيْرِ يا — الاختصاص can also be built with أَيُّها/أَيَّتُها, but *without* the يا of نداء (calling out to someone). وَيَجْري عَلَيْهِ أَحْكامُ النِّداءِ — even without يا, this construction still follows the same إعراب rules as نداء (i.e., what follows أَيُّها is مرفوع, behaving as if it were being called out to, even though no actual calling is happening).

> **Example Box**
>
> وَنَهى رَسولُ اللهِ ﷺ المُسْلِمينَ عَنْ كَلامِنا أَيُّها الثَّلاثَةُ — "And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ forbade the Muslims from speaking to us, the three [of us]" (referring to Ka'b ibn Mālik and his two companions during their period of boycott). أَيُّها الثَّلاثَةُ specifies exactly who كَلامِنا ("our speech," i.e. "speaking to us") refers to — no يا is present, yet أَيُّها still behaves by نداء's rules.
