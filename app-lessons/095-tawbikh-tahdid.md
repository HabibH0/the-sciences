# Lesson 95: أحرف التوبيخ والتحضيض

The fifth قسم of الحرف غير العامل covers أَحْرُفٌ لِلتَّوْبيخِ والتَّحْضيضِ — particles that either scold someone for a mistake already made, or urgently push someone toward an action not yet done. This lesson focuses on the first three of the five: هَلّا، أَلّا، and especially لَوْلا, which carries by far the richest range of meanings.

> **Definition Box**
>
> التَّوْبيخُ لَوْمٌ على الخَطَإِ، وَتَقَعُ أَحْرُفُهُ قَبْلَ الماضي لَفْظًا وَمَعْنًى. والتَّحْضيضُ طَلَبٌ بِشِدَّةٍ، وَتَقَعُ أَحْرُفُهُ قَبْلَ المُضارِعِ.

The two meanings split cleanly along tense. التَّوْبيخ ("reproach/scolding") is blame for a mistake — and you can only blame someone for something already done, so these particles precede a ماضي (past-tense) verb, whether that verb is literally past-tense in form, or simply past in meaning. التَّحْضيض ("urgent incitement") is a forceful push toward action — and you can only urge someone toward something not yet done, so these particles precede a مُضارِع (present/future-tense) verb. Same five particles, opposite direction, determined entirely by which tense follows.

## الأول: هلّا

> **Rule Box**
>
> الْأَوَّلُ هَلّا، نحو: هَلّا صَلَّيْتَ، وَنحو: هَلّا تُصَلّي.

هَلّا صَلَّيْتَ — "Why did you not pray?" (توبيخ: صَلَّيْتَ is ماضي, so this scolds a missed prayer already past). هَلّا تُصَلّي — "Why do/will you not pray?" (تحضيض: تُصَلّي is مُضارِع, so this urges a prayer not yet performed).

> **Careful Box**
>
> أَصْلُ هَلّا هَلِ الِاسْتِفْهامِيَّةُ وَحَرْفُ نَفْيٍ — هَلّا is historically a fusion of the interrogative هَلْ ("is it...?") and the negating حرف لا ("not"). "Is it [that you did] not [pray]?" naturally sharpens, through use, into a reproach or a push: "Why didn't you...?" / "Why don't you...?"

## الثاني: ألّا

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّاني أَلّا، نحو: أَلّا أَنْفَقْتَ على الفُقَراءِ، وَنحو: أَلّا تُنْفِقُ على الفُقَراءِ.

أَلّا works identically to هَلّا — same توبيخ/تحضيض split by tense — just built from a different interrogative root. أَلّا أَنْفَقْتَ على الفُقَراءِ — "Why did you not spend on the poor?" (توبيخ, ماضي). أَلّا تُنْفِقُ على الفُقَراءِ — "Why do you not spend on the poor?" (تحضيض, مُضارِع).

## الثالث: لولا

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّالِثُ لَوْلا، نحو: لَوْلا صُمْتَ، وَنحو: لَوْلا تَصومُ.

Note carefully: this لَوْلا is a DIFFERENT function from the شَرْطِيَّة لَوْلا covered two lessons ago (لَوْلا فَضْلُ اللهِ عَلَيْكُمْ...) — same spelling, entirely different job. Here, exactly like هَلّا and أَلّا, لَوْلا splits by tense: لَوْلا صُمْتَ (توبيخ, "why did you not fast?") versus لَوْلا تَصومُ (تحضيض, "why do you not fast?").

## أصل معنى لولا وتفريعاتها

> **Rule Box**
>
> أَصْلُ مَعْنى لَوْلا تَحْضيضُ المُخاطَبِ على فِعْلٍ في المُسْتَقْبَلِ، وَقَدْ يَكونُ لِلتَّعْجيزِ.

The ORIGINAL, base sense of this لَوْلا is تَحْضيض — pushing the person addressed toward a future action. From that base sense, a secondary, figurative use (مَجاز) can emerge: تَعْجيز, "rendering [someone] incapable" — issuing a "challenge" you already know cannot be met, exposing the addressee's inability rather than genuinely urging them to act.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿لَوْلا يُكَلِّمُنَا اللهُ أَوْ تَأْتينا آيَةٌ﴾ — "Why does Allah not speak to us, or a sign come to us?" The Makkan disbelievers were not sincerely requesting proof — they believed no such sign could or would come, so their real intent was to portray the Prophet ﷺ as unable to produce one. That shift from a genuine push (تحضيض) to a rhetorical challenge assuming failure is تَعْجيز.

> **Rule Box**
>
> فَإِذا دَخَلَتْ على فِعْلٍ فاتَ وُقوعُهُ [أي: ماضٍ]، فَإِنْ كانَ المُتَكَلِّمُ أَعْلى مِنَ المُخاطَبِ يَكونُ لِلتَّوْبيخِ (وَيُقالُ لَهُ أَيْضًا التَّغْليطُ والتَّنْديمُ)؛ وَإِنْ كانَ مُساوِيًا أَوْ دونَهُ يَكونُ لِلتَّمَنّي؛ وَإِذا تَوَجَّهَ الكَلامُ إلى غَيْرِ صاحِبِ الفِعْلِ يَكونُ لِلتَّعَجُّبِ.

When لَوْلا instead attaches to a verb whose action is already past (فاتَ وُقوعُهُ), THREE different meanings become possible, and the deciding factor each time is who is speaking to whom:

1. If the speaker outranks (أَعْلى مِنَ) the addressee, it is genuine توبيخ (also called التَّغْليط والتَّنْديم — "harsh correction and making [someone] regret") — a superior scolding a subordinate for a real mistake.
2. If the speaker is EQUAL to, or LOWER in rank than, the addressee, true توبيخ would be inappropriate (you cannot reprimand someone above you) — so the meaning shifts instead to تَمَنّي, wishing.
3. If the speech is not even directed at the person who performed the action (غَيْرِ صاحِبِ الفِعْلِ) — i.e., you're discussing someone's mistake with a THIRD party, not the person themselves — it becomes تَعَجُّب, an expression of astonishment, since you cannot meaningfully "scold" or "urge" someone who isn't there to hear it.

> **Example Box**
>
> **توبيخ (متكلم أعلى):** ﴿لَوْلا إِذْ سَمِعْتُموهُ ظَنَّ الْمُؤْمِنونَ وَالْمُؤْمِناتُ بِأَنْفُسِهِمْ خَيْرًا﴾ — "Why, when you heard it, did not the believing men and believing women think good of themselves?" Allah — the highest possible authority — is directly reprimanding the believers for their lapse during the slander of ʿĀʾishah رضي الله عنها.
>
> **تمنٍّ (متكلم مساوٍ أو دونه):** ﴿لَوْلا أَخَّرْتَنا إِلٰى أَجَلٍ قَريبٍ﴾ — "If only You had delayed us for a short time [more]!" This is spoken by the soul at the point of death to the angel taking it — clearly not a position from which one can "scold" (تَوْبيخ); instead it reads as a wish for more time, expressed too late.
>
> **تعجب (الكلام موجَّه لغير صاحب الفعل):** ﴿فَلَوْلا إِذْ جاءَهُمْ بَأْسُنا تَضَرَّعوا﴾ — "Then why did they not humble themselves when Our punishment came to them?" This is Allah describing past nations to US, the readers/listeners — not addressing those nations directly — so the sense is astonished commentary on their failure, not a scolding delivered to their face.

## لولا للشرط مقابل لولا للتوبيخ/التحضيض: كيف نميّز؟

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِذا كانَ لَوْلا لِلشَّرْطِ تَدُلُّ على وُقوعِ ما بَعْدَها لِعَدَمِ وُقوعِ جَوابِها، وَإِذا كانَ لِغَيْرِ الشَّرْطِ تَدُلُّ على عَدَمِ وُقوعِ ما بَعْدَها.

Since the same word لَوْلا now covers two entirely different jobs (الشَّرْطِيَّة from two lessons ago, and التَّوْبيخِيَّة/التَّحْضيضِيَّة here), this rule gives a reliable test based on POLARITY:

> **Example Box**
>
> | حالة لولا | ما بعدها (الجملة التالية لها مباشرة) | جواب الشرط |
> |---|---|---|
> | لَوْلا لِلشَّرْطِ | وَقَعَ (حَدَثَ فعلًا) | لَمْ يَقَعْ |
> | لَوْلا لِلتَّوْبيخِ/التَّحْضيضِ | لَمْ يَقَعْ (هذا هو بيت القصيد) | لا يُذكَر جواب أصلًا |
>
> لَوْلا الْقُرْآنَ لَما اهْتَدَيْنا — "Were it not for the Qur'an, we would not have been guided" — here لَوْلا is شَرْطِيَّة: القرآن (ما بعد لولا) genuinely EXISTS/happened, and that existence is precisely what prevented the جواب (عَدَم الهداية) from happening — so the جواب is explicitly stated (لَما اهْتَدَيْنا).
>
> لَوْلا قَرَأْتَ لَفَشِلْتَ — spoken as a warning, this instead means "you should have read, [or] you would have failed" — here القراءة (ما بعد لولا) did NOT happen (that is exactly the point being made — "why didn't you read?"), and there is no separate جواب clause functioning as a real شرط-response; the sentence is فَعْلًا توبيخي in shape, not a true شرط sentence.

> **Careful Box**
>
> إِذا كانَتْ لَوْلا وَلَوْما لِلشَّرْطِ يَكونُ الجَوابُ مَذْكورًا بَعْدَهُ، وَإِذا كانَ لِلتَّوْبيخِ لا يُذْكَرُ لَهُ جَوابٌ — the single most reliable test: does an explicit جواب clause follow? If yes, you are looking at شَرْطِيَّة لولا. If the sentence stops after the verb with no separate response clause, you are looking at تَوْبيخِيَّة/تَحْضيضِيَّة لولا.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿فَلَوْلا كانَتْ قَرْيَةٌ آمَنَتْ فَنَفَعَها إيمانُها إِلّا قَوْمَ يونُسَ﴾ — "Then why has there not been a town that believed [before seeing the punishment] so that its faith would benefit it, except the people of Yūnus?" The meaning is: لَمْ يَكُنْ قَرْيَةٌ آمَنَتْ قَبْلَ نُزولِ العَذابِ بِها إِلّا قَوْمَ يونُسَ ("no town believed [in time] before the punishment came upon it, except the people of Yūnus"). Even though كانَتْ is not grammatically negated on its face, the تحضيض/توبيخ sense here forces a NEGATIVE meaning onto the verb (تَحْضيضُ المُخاطَبِ على فِعْلٍ ... أَوْ تَوْبيخُهُ ... يَلْزَمُ نَفْيَ الفِعْلِ) — and since the speech here is addressed to us, not to those long-gone nations, the overall effect is تَعَجُّب (astonishment) at how rare that early, saving belief was.
