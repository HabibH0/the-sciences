# Lesson 1: Why Study الكبرى في النحو?

## The point of the book

الكبرى في النحو is a grammar text for the student who has already met the foundations of Arabic grammar and now needs more detail.

It comes after simpler stages of study. الصغرى gives the student the basic structure of grammar, and الوسطى adds more of the main discussions. الكبرى keeps that path, but gives the extra issues, examples, and details that appear when a student starts reading more demanding books.

الكبرى follows the path of الوسطى, but adds the issues needed by a more advanced student:

> **Core Text**
>
> سَلَكْتُ فِيهِ مَا سَلَكْتُ فِي الوُسْطَى مُضِيفًا إِلَيْهِ مَا يَحْتَاجُ إِلَيْهِ المُتَقَدِّمُ

In plain terms: this book keeps the earlier structure, then adds the grammar discussions that become necessary when reading advanced Arabic texts.

The author also describes the work as a تذكرة, a reminder for himself and for later students. That shapes how it is written. It is arranged as a study aid, a way to gather the discussions an advanced student repeatedly needs, rather than as a detached encyclopedia.

## What makes it different

The book is not trying to list every possible grammar issue. Its main concern is the grammar that helps a student read:

| Area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| القرآن | Many subtle meanings depend on grammar. |
| تفسير | Tafsir books often discuss grammatical possibilities. |
| إعراب القرآن | These works expect the reader to know grammar terms and analysis. |
| Advanced grammar books | The student needs clear categories before entering longer discussions. |

The material is drawn from grammar works and Qur'anic explanation works, including:

| Field | Examples | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar texts | ألفية ابن مالك، أوضح المسالك، هداية النحو، الكافية | Alfiyyah of Ibn Malik, Awdah al-Masalik, Hidayat al-Nahw, al-Kafiyah |
| Tafsir works | تفسير الجلالين، البيضاوي، التحرير والتنوير | Tafsir al-Jalalayn, al-Baydawi, al-Tahrir wa al-Tanwir |
| Qur'anic grammar | كتب إعراب القرآن | works on the grammatical analysis of the Qur'an |

This matters because some terms appear often in tafsir and إعراب works, even if they are not explained fully in beginner books. A student may meet discussions such as `ظرف مستقر`, `حروف متداخلة`, or `حروف مترادفة` while reading, and needs a grammar foundation broad enough to follow them.

## A linguistic doorway into tafsir

The work is described as:

> **Core Text**
>
> مُقَدِّمَةٌ لُغَوِيَّةٌ لِتَفْسِيرِ القُرْآنِ

That means the book is a **linguistic introduction to tafsir of the Qur'an**. It teaches the grammar needed to understand how Qur'anic language is analysed.

## How the book teaches

Its teaching method is:

> **Core Text**
>
> آثَرْتُ تَوْضِيحَ العِبَارَةِ عَلَى إِيجَازِهَا وَإِيرَادَ الأَمْثِلَةِ

The method is clear explanation before extreme brevity, with examples instead of leaving the reader to guess.

> **Example Box**
>
> A short grammar text may say only:  
> `الكلمة قول مفرد`
>
> الكبرى will still give the short definition, but it also explains what `قول` means, what `مفرد` means, and how to tell the difference between `مفرد` and `مركب`.

This style makes the work useful as a reference. The student should be able to return to a topic later and recover both the rule and the reason behind it.
