# Lesson 91: أحرف التأكيد

A new قسم (the third) of الحرف غير العامل begins: أَحْرُفُ التَّأْكيدِ (also called التَّوْكيد — both words mean the same thing, "emphasis/confirmation"). There are five of them, and this lesson covers all five.

> **Definition Box**
>
> القسم الثالث: أَحْرُفُ التَّأْكيدِ، وَهِيَ خَمْسَةٌ: لامُ الِابْتِداءِ، اللّامُ المُزَحْلَقَةُ، اللّامُ في جَوابِ القَسَمِ، نونا التَّأْكيدِ، قَدْ.

## الأول: لام الابتداء

> **Rule Box**
>
> لامُ الِابْتِداءِ تَدْخُلُ على المُبْتَدَأِ، وَتُفيدُ التَّأْكيدَ.

This ل simply attaches to the very beginning of a nominal sentence, onto the مبتدأ, adding emphasis without changing the sentence's basic meaning.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَلَدارُ الْآخِرَةِ خَيْرٌ لِلَّذينَ اتَّقَوْا﴾ — "And the home of the Hereafter is surely better for those who fear Allah." لَدارُ opens the sentence with لام الابتداء attached directly to the مبتدأ (دار), stressing the certainty of the claim that follows.

## الثاني: اللام المزحلقة

> **Rule Box**
>
> تَدْخُلُ اللّامُ المُزَحْلَقَةُ على خَبَرِ إِنَّ إِذا كانَ اسْمًا، أَوْ فِعْلًا مُضارِعًا، أَوْ شِبْهَ جُمْلَةٍ. وَتُسَمّى لامُ الِابْتِداءِ واللّامُ المُزَحْلَقَةُ مَعًا لامَ التَّوْكيدِ.

This second ل is closely related to the first — both are ultimately forms of لام التوكيد — but it attaches specifically to the خبر of إِنَّ, and only in three situations: when that خبر is an اسم, a فعل مضارع, or شبه جملة (a prepositional/adverbial phrase acting as predicate). Notably, it does NOT attach when the خبر is a فعل ماضي.

> **Example Box**
>
> **خبر إنّ اسمًا:** ﴿إِنَّ رَبّي لَسَميعُ الدُّعاءِ﴾ — "Indeed my Lord is surely All-Hearing of supplication." لَسَميعُ is the اسم-خبر of إِنَّ, carrying اللام المزحلقة. Likewise ﴿إِنَّ في ذٰلِكَ لَآياتٍ﴾ — the ل falls on لَآياتٍ, the (delayed) اسم serving as خبر.
>
> **خبر إنّ فعلًا مضارعًا:** ﴿وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَيَعْلَمُ ما تُكِنُّ صُدورُهُمْ وَما يُعْلِنونَ﴾ — "And indeed your Lord surely knows what their breasts conceal and what they reveal." لَيَعْلَمُ is a فعل مضارع serving as خبر, carrying the ل.
>
> **خبر إنّ شبه جملة:** ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلٰى خُلُقٍ عَظيمٍ﴾ — "And indeed you are of a great moral character." لَعَلٰى خُلُقٍ عَظيمٍ is a شبه جملة (جار ومجرور) serving as خبر, carrying the ل.

## لماذا "المزحلقة"؟

> **Rule Box**
>
> هِيَ في الْأَصْلِ لامُ الِابْتِداءِ تَزَحْلَقَتْ (سَلِسَتْ وَانْتَقَلَتْ) مِنْ صَدْرِ الكَلامِ إِلٰى الخَبَرِ، كَراهِيَةَ اجْتِماعِ مُؤَكِّدَيْنِ (إِنَّ واللّام) في صَدْرِ الكَلامِ.

مُزَحْلَقَة literally means "slid" or "shifted." This ل is, historically, the very same لام الابتداء — but when إِنَّ (itself a تأكيد particle) opens a sentence, having ANOTHER emphasis-word (لام الابتداء) immediately next to it at the very front would pile up two مُؤَكِّدات (emphasis devices) side by side, which Arabic dislikes (كَراهِيَة اجْتِماعِ مُؤَكِّدَيْنِ). So the ل "slides" away from the front of the sentence, landing instead on the خبر — hence لام مُزَحْلَقَة, "the ل that slid."

> **Example Box**
>
> Consider لَزَيْدٌ قائِمٌ ("Zayd is surely standing," with plain لام الابتداء at the very front). Now add إِنَّ: you would NOT say إِنَّ لَزَيْدٌ قائِمٌ (إِنَّ and لام right next to each other at the start — disliked). Instead, the ل slides down onto the خبر: إِنَّ زَيْدًا لَقائِمٌ — "Indeed Zayd is surely standing." The same emphasis is preserved, just relocated.

## الثالث: اللام في جواب القسم

> **Rule Box**
>
> اللّامُ في جَوابِ القَسَمِ تَدْخُلُ على جَوابِ القَسَمِ، وَتُفيدُ التَّأْكيدَ.

When an oath (قَسَم) is sworn, its جواب (the statement being sworn to) often carries this emphatic ل.

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿تَاللَّهِ لَتُسْأَلُنَّ عَمّا كُنْتُمْ تَفْتَرونَ﴾ — "By Allah, you will surely be questioned about what you used to invent." تَاللَّهِ is the oath (قَسَم); لَتُسْأَلُنَّ (carrying the ل) is its جواب, emphasized.

## الرابع: نونا التأكيد

> **Rule Box**
>
> نونا التَّأْكيدِ هُما الخَفيفَةُ والثَّقيلَةُ، تَدْخُلانِ على آخِرِ الفِعْلِ المُضارِعِ لِإِفادَةِ التَّأْكيدِ.

Two forms of an emphatic ن attach to the end of a فعل مضارع: النون الخفيفة (light, a single ن with سكون) and النون الثقيلة (heavy, a doubled ن with شدّة).

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿لَيُسْجَنَنَّ وَلَيَكونَنْ مِنَ الصّاغِرينَ﴾ — "He will surely be imprisoned and will surely be among the humiliated." لَيُسْجَنَنَّ carries النون الثقيلة (doubled ن); لَيَكونَنْ carries النون الخفيفة (single ن) — both words are pronounced essentially the same way despite the different spelling, and both add emphasis to their verb.

> **Careful Box**
>
> لَيَكونَنْ أَصْلُهُ لَيَكونَنَّ — the spelling with a plain ن (لَيَكونَنْ) is itself derived from an original doubled-ن form; recognizing this helps when parsing the word's root letters, even though the two forms sound alike.

## الخامس: قَدْ

قَدْ is the most semantically rich of the five أحرف التأكيد — its meaning shifts depending on whether it attaches to a فعل ماضي or a فعل مضارع.

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ تَدْخُلُ على الماضي، وَتَكونُ لِلتَّحْقيقِ (دائمًا). وَتَدْخُلُ على المُضارِعِ، وَتَكونُ لِلتَّحْقيقِ، أَوْ لِلتَّوَقُّعِ، أَوْ لِلتَّكْثيرِ، أَوْ لِلتَّقْليلِ (وَهُوَ الغالِبُ).

With a فعل ماضي, قَدْ always means تَحْقيق (certainty/confirmation — "indeed, certainly"). With a فعل مضارع, قَدْ can carry any of FOUR different senses, but the DEFAULT/most common one is تَقْليل (rarity/infrequency) — the other three are less common but genuine.

> **Example Box**
>
> **مع الماضي — التحقيق (always):** ﴿قَدْ أَفْلَحَ المُؤْمِنونَ﴾ — "The believers have certainly succeeded." قَدْ here simply confirms the success as a settled fact.
>
> **مع المضارع — التحقيق:** ﴿قَدْ يَعْلَمُ اللَّهُ﴾ — "Allah certainly knows." Context (the subject being Allah, whose knowledge is never uncertain) tells us this قَدْ means certainty, not rarity, even though it precedes a مضارع.
>
> **مع المضارع — التوقّع** (anticipating a future occurrence): قَدْ يَبْرَأُ المَريضُ — "The patient may [hopefully, is expected to] recover." This قَدْ expresses waiting/expectation that the action will occur in the future — a sense of hopeful anticipation.
>
> **مع المضارع — التكثير** (frequency/repetition — "often"): ﴿قَدْ نَرٰى تَقَلُّبَ وَجْهِكَ في السَّماءِ﴾ — "We have certainly seen the turning of your face toward the sky [repeatedly]." This describes the Prophet ﷺ repeatedly turning his face to the heavens, hoping for revelation changing the قِبلة — قَدْ here signals that the action happened often, not just once.
>
> **مع المضارع — التقليل (الغالب — the default sense):** قَدْ يَصْدُقُ الكَذوبُ — "Even the habitual liar sometimes tells the truth." This is the DEFAULT reading of قَدْ before a مضارع — it signals something happens only occasionally, rarely, as an exception rather than the rule.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Because التقليل is قَدْ's DEFAULT sense with a مضارع, always check context carefully before assuming a stronger meaning (تحقيق, توقّع, or تكثير) is intended — those three require some contextual signal (like علم اللَّه being inherently certain, or explicit repetition being described) to override the default "rarity/occasional" reading.
