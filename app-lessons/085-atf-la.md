# Lesson 85: أحرف العطف — لا

The seventh of the nine أحرف العطف is لا. Where the other particles seen so far AFFIRM a shared حكم between two things, لا does the opposite — it takes the ruling that was just given and explicitly DENIES it of the second item.

> **Definition Box**
>
> السّابِعُ لا، وهي لِنَفْيِ الحُكْمِ عَنِ المَعْطوفِ، نحو: اِعْمَلْ صالِحًا لا سَيِّئًا.

> **Example Box**
>
> اِعْمَلْ صالِحًا لا سَيِّئًا — "Do [what is] good, not [what is] evil." The command (اعمل) applies to صالِحًا (المعطوف عليه); لا then strips that same command away from سَيِّئًا (المعطوف) — you are being told, in one breath, both to do the first and NOT to do the second.
>
> رَأَيْتُ عَمْرًا لا خالِدًا — "I saw 'Amr, not Khalid." The seeing (الرؤية) happened to the first name, and is explicitly denied of the second.

## شرائط لا العاطفة

> **Rule Box**
>
> يُشْتَرَطُ لِـ لا العاطِفَةِ أَنْ يَتَقَدَّمَها إِثْباتٌ أَوْ أَمْرٌ. فَإِنْ تَقَدَّمَها نَفْيٌ أَوْ نَهْيٌ وَجَبَتِ الْواوُ (لا تعمل لا وحدها؛ بل تصير وَلا).

لا العاطِفَة only works this way — negating the second item while the FIRST item stays affirmed — when what comes before it is already positive: either إِثْبات (a positive statement) or أَمْر (a positive command). If what precedes is already negative (نَفْي or نَهْي), then bringing لا on its own would be redundant/confusing (two negatives colliding), so واو must be added before it instead: وَلا.

> **Example Box**
>
> **بعد إثبات أو أمر (لا alone):** اِعْمَلْ صالِحًا لا سَيِّئًا — the command أَعْمَلْ is positive, so لا alone correctly negates سيّئًا.
>
> **بعد نفي أو نهي (واجب الواو):** ﴿وَلَنْ تَرْضىٰ عَنْكَ الْيَهودُ وَلَا النَّصارىٰ﴾ — "And never will the Jews or the Christians approve of you." لَنْ تَرْضٰى is already negative (a فعل منفي with لَنْ), so الْيَهودُ's negation must be followed by وَلَا النَّصارىٰ, WITH the واو — you could not correctly say لَنْ تَرْضَى عَنْكَ الْيَهودُ لا النَّصارىٰ without the واو.

## تكرار لا العاطفة

Beyond the واو requirement, there is a further rule about REPEATING لا itself.

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجِبُ تَكْرارُ لا مَعَ الْواوِ إِذا دَخَلَتْ عَلٰى خَبَرٍ مَنْفِيٍّ، أَوْ نَعْتٍ مَنْفِيٍّ، أَوْ حالٍ مَنْفِيٍّ.

When لا is negating a خَبَر (predicate), a نَعْت (adjective/descriptor), or a حال (circumstantial description) — rather than a simple object — and there is more than one such negated item joined together, لا must be repeated before EACH one, each time preceded by واو.

> **Example Box**
>
> **نعت منفي:** ﴿وَفاكِهَةٍ كَثيرَةٍ لا مَقْطوعَةٍ وَلا مَمْنوعَةٍ﴾ — "and abundant fruit, neither limited nor forbidden" [describing the fruits of Paradise]. Both مَقْطوعَة and مَمْنوعَة are نَعْت (descriptions) of فاكِهَة, each negated — so لا appears before BOTH: لا مَقْطوعَةٍ ... وَلا مَمْنوعَةٍ, not just once.
>
> **حال منفي:** صَلّٰى لا غافِلًا وَلا لاهِيًا — "He prayed, neither heedless nor distracted." غافِلًا and لاهِيًا are both حال (circumstantial descriptions of his state while praying), each negated in turn: لا appears before both.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Do not assume a doubled لا...ولا always means simply "neither...nor" in a flat, interchangeable sense — check whether each negated item is being denied INDIVIDUALLY or whether the negation is really about the COMBINATION of the two. Compare: لا تَأْكُلْ سَمَكًا وَلَبَنًا ("don't eat fish and milk [together]") most naturally forbids the COMBINATION — eating either one alone may still be fine, only eating them together is what's prohibited. But لا تَأْكُلْ سَمَكًا وَلا لَبَنًا (repeating لا before the second item) forbids EACH one individually — neither the fish nor the milk may be eaten, together or separately. Bringing the second لا is precisely what signals that the negation applies to each item on its own, not merely to their combination — this is part of why تكرار لا matters grammatically, not just stylistically.

## Summary table

| Rule | Condition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| لا alone | Preceded by إثبات or أمر (positive) | اِعْمَلْ صالِحًا لا سَيِّئًا |
| وَلا (واو obligatory) | Preceded by نَفْي or نَهْي (negative) | وَلَنْ تَرْضىٰ عَنْكَ الْيَهودُ وَلَا النَّصارىٰ |
| تكرار لا (repeat before each item) | لا negates a خبر / نعت / حال with more than one item | وَفاكِهَةٍ كَثيرَةٍ لا مَقْطوعَةٍ وَلا مَمْنوعَةٍ؛ صَلّٰى لا غافِلًا وَلا لاهِيًا |
| Effect of repeating لا | Denies each item individually, not just their combination | لا تَأْكُلْ سَمَكًا وَلا لَبَنًا (neither, ever) vs. لا تَأْكُلْ سَمَكًا وَلَبَنًا (not together) |
