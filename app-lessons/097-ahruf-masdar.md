# Lesson 97: أحرف المصدر (غير العاملة)

The eighth قسم of الحرف غير العامل covers أَحْرُفُ المَصْدَر — particles that turn the جملة (sentence) following them into the equivalent of a single مَصْدَر (verbal noun), letting that whole clause fill one grammatical slot in a larger sentence. This lesson covers the three that are غَيْر عامِل (do not themselves grammatically govern the verb after them) — ما، لَوْ بعد وَدَّ، and هَمْزَةُ التَّسْوِيَة.

> **Definition Box**
>
> القسم الثّامِنُ: أَحْرُفُ المَصْدَرِ. تُسَمّى أَحْرُفُ المَصْدَرِ مَوْصولاتٍ حَرْفِيَّةً وَحُروفًا مَوْصولَةً. وهي ثَلاثَةٌ (غَيْرَ عامِلَةٍ): ما، وَلَوْ بَعْدَ وَدَّ، وَهَمْزَةُ التَّسْوِيَةِ.

These particles carry three overlapping names in the grammar tradition — حَرْفٌ مَصْدَرِيّ, مَوْصولٌ حَرَفِيّ, and حَرْفٌ مَوْصولٌ — all pointing at the same function: like the الَّذي-type اسم موصول (relative pronoun) turns a clause into something that behaves like a noun, these particles do the same job using a حرف instead of an اسم, and specifically produce a مَصْدَر (verbal-noun) meaning rather than a simple noun meaning.

## الأول: ما المصدرية

> **Rule Box**
>
> ما المَصْدَرِيَّةُ نَوْعانِ: الظَّرْفِيَّةُ، وَغَيْرُ الظَّرْفِيَّةِ.

ما المصدرية splits into two types based on whether it carries a TIME meaning (ظَرْفِيَّة, "adverbial-of-time") or not.

> **Example Box**
>
> **الظرفية:** ﴿وَأَوْصٰني بِالصَّلٰوةِ والزَّكٰوةِ ما دُمْتُ حَيًّا﴾ — "And He has enjoined upon me prayer and zakāh as long as I remain alive." ما دُمْتُ حَيًّا converts into the meaning "for the DURATION that I am alive" (مُدَّةَ حَياتي) — a measure of TIME, which is why this ما is labelled ظَرْفِيَّة. It typically (though not always) pairs with the verb دامَ/دُمْتُ, "to remain/continue."
>
> **غير الظرفية:** ﴿فَصَبَروا على ما كُذِّبوا﴾ — "So they were patient in the face of being denied." ما كُذِّبوا converts into a plain مصدر meaning — "[their] being denied" (تَكْذيبِهِمْ) — with no sense of duration or time at all; it simply names an event/state, filling a normal grammatical slot (here, the object of على) the way any ordinary noun would.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Don't confuse either of these with ما المَوْصولَة (the RELATIVE ما, "that which..."), which behaves very differently: a مَوْصول ما needs something afterward to refer BACK to it (a عائِد). فَصَبَروا على ما كُذِّبوا only works as مَصْدَرِيَّة precisely because there is no missing "referring-back" element — كُذِّبوا itself, wholesale, becomes the "being denied," with nothing left over needing a pronoun to point back to ما.

## الثاني: لو بعد ودّ

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّاني لَوْ بَعْدَ وَدَّ، نحو: ﴿وَدّوا لَوْ تَكْفُرونَ كَما كَفَروا﴾.

When لَوْ directly follows a form of the verb وَدَّ ("to wish/love [that]"), it stops functioning as the conditional لَوْ met earlier in this course, and instead converts its following clause into a مَصْدَر — filling the object slot of وَدَّ. ﴿وَدّوا لَوْ تَكْفُرونَ كَما كَفَروا﴾ — "They wish that you would disbelieve just as they have disbelieved." لَوْ تَكْفُرونَ here converts into وُدّوا كُفْرَكُمْ, "they wished for your disbelief" — تَكْفُرونَ effectively becomes a مصدر (كُفْر) functioning as the direct object of وَدّوا, not a شرط clause with its own جواب.

## الثالث: همزة التسوية

> **Rule Box**
>
> الثّالِثُ هَمْزَةُ التَّسْوِيَةِ، نحو: ﴿وَسَواءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنْذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنْذِرْهُمْ﴾.

This همزة LOOKS identical to the ordinary هَمْزَةُ الِاسْتِفْهام (the hamza that opens a yes/no question), but here it is not asking a real question at all — instead, together with the أَمْ that follows shortly after, it converts BOTH options it connects into a single مَصْدَر phrase expressing that the two options are EQUAL (تَسْوِيَة, "equalization").

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿وَسَواءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنْذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنْذِرْهُمْ﴾ — "It is all the same to them whether you warn them or do not warn them." ءَأَنْذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنْذِرْهُمْ together convert into a single مصدر phrase, roughly "[your] warning them or not warning them" (إِنْذارُهُمْ وَعَدَمُ إِنْذارِهِمْ), and that whole compound phrase becomes the مُبْتَدَأ مُؤَخَّر (delayed subject) of سَواءٌ ("[the two are] equal" / "it makes no difference"). No real answer is being sought — the hamza's grammatical FORM is that of a question, but its FUNCTION is entirely different.

> **Careful Box**
>
> This lesson covers only the غَيْرَ عامِل (non-governing) أحرف المصدر. The remaining ones — كَيْ، أَنْ، and أَنَّ — ARE عامِل (they actively govern the mood/case of what follows: أَنْ nasbs a following مضارع, أَنَّ nasbs its اسم the way إِنَّ does) and belong to a separate discussion of عامِل particles, not this section on غير العامل.
