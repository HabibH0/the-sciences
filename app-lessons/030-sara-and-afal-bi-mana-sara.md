# Lesson 30: صار وأفعال بمعنى صار

## صار

صار is the seventh of the أفعال الناقصة. Its meaning is تحول — to become, to pass from one حال to another. As a فعل ناقص it takes the مبتدأ as its اسم and the خبر as its خبر, and what it reports is that the اسم has come to be the خبر.

> **Definition Box**
>
> صَارَ: لِتَحَوُّلِ الذَّاتِ وَتَحَوُّلِ الصِّفَاتِ

Two different kinds of change fall under it.

| Kind | What changes | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| تحول الذات | The thing itself. One item becomes another item, essence and all | صَارَ الخَمْرُ خَلًّا | The wine became vinegar |
| تحول الصفات | Only the صفة. The entity stays what it was | صَارَ المُذْنِبُ مُتَّقِيًا | The sinner became God-fearing |

In صَارَ الخَمْرُ خَلًّا the whole substance has turned into something else. In صَارَ المُذْنِبُ مُتَّقِيًا it is the same person throughout, and what has moved is the description attached to him: مذنب out, متقٍ in.

| صَارَ | الخَمْرُ | خَلًّا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص | اسم صار مرفوع | خبر صار منصوب |

| صَارَ | المُذْنِبُ | مُتَّقِيًا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص | اسم صار مرفوع | خبر صار منصوب |

> **Careful Box**
>
> The wording as it stands is صَارَ الخَمْرُ خَلًّا. خمر is مؤنث, so صَارَتِ الخَمْرُ خَلًّا is what would be expected here. The form without the تاء looks like a slip.

## The six أفعال ناقصة that can carry the meaning of صار

> **Rule Box**
>
> قَدْ تَكُونُ كَانَ وَأَصْبَحَ وَأَضْحَى وَأَمْسَى وَظَلَّ وَبَاتَ بِمَعْنَى صَارَ

Each of these six may report a change from one state to another, exactly as صار does.

## What survives when one word carries another word's meaning

> **Rule Box**
>
> إِذَا كَانَتْ كَلِمَةٌ بِمَعْنَى كَلِمَةٍ أُخْرَى لَا يَزُولُ عَنْهَا مَعْنَاهَا الأَصْلِيُّ زَوَالًا تَامًّا

Saying that one word carries the meaning of another never means it has swapped identities with it. Its own original meaning stays present in some measure. If أصبح carries the sense of صار, the صبح inside it is still doing work, otherwise there would have been no reason to reach for أصبح when صار was available. The same holds for أمسى, for ظل, and for the rest.

> **Careful Box**
>
> Not every occurrence has a settled published answer for what the retained meaning contributes. Hold the principle, and where a particular verse raises the question, keep the question and look for the answer over time.

### كان بمعنى صار

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿فَسَجَدَ الْمَلَائِكَةُ كُلُّهُمْ أَجْمَعُونَ إِلَّا إِبْلِيسَ اسْتَكْبَرَ وَكَانَ مِنَ الْكَافِرِينَ﴾
>
> The angels prostrated to Ādam, all of them together, except إبليس. He refused out of استكبار, and he was among the disbelievers.

إبليس is the استثناء from the general سجود.

| وَكَانَ | (هُوَ) | مِنَ الْكَافِرِينَ |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص | اسم كان، ضمير مستتر | شبه جملة في محل نصب خبر كان |

The مفسرون differ over what كان is doing here.

| Reading | What it says about إبليس |
|---|---|
| كان on its حقيقي meaning | He always was among the كافرين. The سجود and عبادة seen from him were outward appearance only |
| كان بمعنى صار, a مجازي reading | He became one of the كافرين, after having been in سجود and عبادة |

On the second reading the obvious question is why كان was used at all, if صار is what is meant. The answer is that the ماضي sense built into كان does not leave. It says that this outcome was already destined for him, so that although he became a kafir, nothing was overturned in the process. The change came out of his own nature.

> **Rule Box**
>
> كَانَ بِمَعْنَى صَارَ يَدُلُّ عَلَى أَنَّ التَّحَوُّلَ مِنْ طَبِيعَتِهِ

### أصبح بمعنى صار

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿كُنْتُمْ أَعْدَاءً فَأَلَّفَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِكُمْ فَأَصْبَحْتُمْ بِنِعْمَتِهِ إِخْوَانًا﴾
>
> You were enemies, and He joined your hearts together, and by His favour you became brothers.

| فَأَصْبَحْتُمْ | تُمْ | بِنِعْمَتِهِ | إِخْوَانًا |
|---|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص بمعنى صار | اسم أصبح | جار ومجرور | خبر أصبح منصوب |

فَصِرْتُمْ بِنِعْمَتِهِ إِخْوَانًا was available, and أصبح was used instead. What follows is a suggested reading, not one found in the books.

أصبح is built on صبح, which carries freshness, morning, new light, the turn out of darkness into brightness. The people addressed had been living in يثرب with war, bloodshed, enmity, rancour and hatred among them. فَأَصْبَحْتُمْ pictures them waking to find themselves brothers, after the Prophet ﷺ had worked on them, taken their بيعة and reconciled them. A fresh dawn for المدينة المنورة, and the change reached well past the city, to the face of the whole world. صرتم would report the change and carry none of that. والله أعلم.

### ظل بمعنى صار

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿وَإِذَا بُشِّرَ أَحَدُهُمْ بِمَا ضَرَبَ لِلرَّحْمَٰنِ مَثَلًا ظَلَّ وَجْهُهُ مُسْوَدًّا وَهُوَ كَظِيمٌ﴾
>
> When one of them is given news of the very thing he strikes as a likeness for the Most Merciful, his face turns dark and he is choked with rage.

The likeness they strike for Allah is that the ملائكة are His daughters, بنات الله. The verse turns that on them: give one of them the same news about himself, and his own face goes dark.

| ظَلَّ | وَجْهُهُ | مُسْوَدًّا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص بمعنى صار | اسم ظل مرفوع | خبر ظل منصوب |

Two reasons for ظل here rather than أصبح or صار:

- ظل is built on ظِلّ, shade and darkness, which suits مسودًّا, darkening and blackness. أصبح would have brought morning brightness into a verse about a face going dark, pulling against the image instead of with it.
- ظل carries امتداد, a stretch of time. The state holds through the day and does not come to an end. صار reports the change and says nothing about how long it lasts.

والله أعلم.

> **Rule Box**
>
> لَعَلَّ الفَائِدَةَ فِي كَلِمَةِ ظَلَّ أَنَّهَا تُنَاسِبُ كَلِمَةَ مُسْوَدًّا، وَكَذَلِكَ فِي ظَلَّ امْتِدَادٌ لَيْسَ فِي صَارَ

## أفعال بمعنى صار

Outside كان وأخواتها there are further أفعال that come in the meaning of صار.

| Term | Example | Translation | What it adds beyond صار |
|---|---|---|---|
| ارتدّ | ﴿فَارْتَدَّ بَصِيرًا﴾ | He became seeing | Becoming something one had been before |
| عاد | ﴿أَوْ لَتَعُودُنَّ فِي مِلَّتِنَا﴾ | Or you will return into our ملة | Returning to a former state, here only on the speakers' own زعم |
| استحال | إِنَّ العَدَاوَةَ تَسْتَحِيلُ مَوَدَّةً | Enmity turns into affection | A state turning into its opposite |

### ارتدّ

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿فَارْتَدَّ بَصِيرًا﴾

| فَارْتَدَّ | (هُوَ) | بَصِيرًا |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص بمعنى صار | اسم ارتدّ، ضمير مستتر | خبر ارتدّ منصوب |

يعقوب عليه السلام had lost his sight from grief over يوسف: ﴿وَابْيَضَّتْ عَيْنَاهُ مِنَ الْحُزْنِ فَهُوَ كَظِيمٌ﴾ — his eyes turned white, meaning he could no longer see. When the shirt was sent back and cast over his face, فَارْتَدَّ بَصِيرًا: he became seeing.

ارتدّ is from ردّ, to return. So what it reports is that he became seeing *again*, back to a state he had held before. صار on its own does not carry that.

> **Rule Box**
>
> لَعَلَّ الفَائِدَةَ فِي كَلِمَةِ ارْتَدَّ الدَّلَالَةُ عَلَى الصَّيْرُورَةِ إِلَى حَالٍ سَابِقٍ

### عاد

> **Core Text**
>
> ﴿لَنُخْرِجَنَّكَ يَا شُعَيْبُ وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مَعَكَ مِنْ قَرْيَتِنَا أَوْ لَتَعُودُنَّ فِي مِلَّتِنَا﴾
>
> We will drive you out of our town, Shuʿayb, and those who believe with you, unless you return into our ملة.

Some hold that لَتَعُودُنَّ is بمعنى صار here, giving the sense *you come into our ملة*. Their reason is that شعيب was never in that ملة for him to return to it. He was never a kafir. No نبي was ever upon كفر at any point.

The easier answer keeps عاد on its own meaning of going back, and reads the sentence as the words of the قوم, spoken according to their own زعم. Before نبوة, شعيب was not upon their كفر and شرك and idol worship, but he did not speak against it either, so it appeared to them that he was content with it and nothing was noticed. Once نبوة came and he spoke against it, it looked to them as though he had changed his religion. Their demand is that he come back to what they had taken him to be. He never belonged to their ملة; the wording expresses their understanding of the matter, not the fact.

> **Rule Box**
>
> العَوْدُ إِلَى مِلَّةِ الكُفْرِ بِاعْتِبَارِ زَعْمِ قَوْمِهِ المُتَكَلِّمُونَ، وَإِلَّا فَلَمْ يَكُنْ نَبِيٌّ عَلَى الكُفْرِ قَطُّ

### استحال

استحال likewise reports a change of state.

> **Core Text**
>
> إِنَّ العَدَاوَةَ تَسْتَحِيلُ مَوَدَّةً ... بِتَدَارُكِ الهَفَوَاتِ بِالحَسَنَاتِ
>
> Enmity turns into affection, by making good the slips with acts of kindness.

| تَسْتَحِيلُ | (هِيَ) | مَوَدَّةً |
|---|---|---|
| فعل ناقص بمعنى صار | اسم تستحيل، ضمير مستتر | خبر منصوب |

عداوة between two people is not fixed in place. بِتَدَارُكِ الهَفَوَاتِ بِالحَسَنَاتِ names how it moves: someone treats you badly and you repay it with good, and that, إن شاء الله, is what turns العداوة into مودة.
