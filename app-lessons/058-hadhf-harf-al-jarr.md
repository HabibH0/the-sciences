# Lesson 58: حذف حرف الجر — القياسي، السماعي، والمنصوب بنزع الخافض

Having covered every particle that can function as حرف جر — أصلي، شبيه بالزائد، زائد، and even the handful some scholars add — the discussion now turns to the opposite question: when can a حرف جر be dropped from a sentence entirely, while its meaning and its عمل remain understood?

## مواضع حذف حرف الجر قياسا

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجُوزُ حَذْفُ حَرْفِ الجَرِّ قِيَاسًا قَبْلَ أَنْ وَقَبْلَ أَنَّ

حرف الجر is itself a حرف — and when a حرف immediately precedes another حرف, such as أَنْ or أَنَّ, the resulting sequence of two حروف back to back feels ثَقِيل, "heavy," to pronounce and hear. To relieve that heaviness, Arabic regularly drops the حرف الجر immediately before أَنْ or أَنَّ, while its عمل — the جر it would have imposed — is understood to remain in force underneath.

| Context | Example | With the حرف جر restored |
|---|---|---|
| قبل أَنْ | ﴿إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَنْ تُؤَدُّوا الْأَمَانَاتِ إِلَى أَهْلِهَا﴾ | ﴿وَيَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ﴾ |
| قبل أَنَّ | ﴿إِنِّي أُشْهِدُ اللَّهَ وَاشْهَدُوا أَنِّي بَرِيءٌ مِمَّا تُشْرِكُونَ﴾ | ﴿وَاشْهَدْ بِأَنَّا مُسْلِمُونَ﴾ |

The verb أَمَرَ ordinarily takes its مفعول به through الباء, exactly as seen in ﴿وَيَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ﴾, "and they enjoin what is right." ﴿يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَنْ تُؤَدُّوا﴾ has simply dropped that باء before أَنْ, though the underlying meaning is unchanged. The same happens before أَنَّ: the أصل of ﴿وَاشْهَدُوا أَنِّي بَرِيءٌ﴾ is وَاشْهَدُوا بِأَنِّي, with الباء present, as in ﴿وَاشْهَدْ بِأَنَّا مُسْلِمُونَ﴾, "and bear witness that we are Muslims."

> **Careful Box**
>
> Why the باء is kept in some verses and dropped in others is not something نحو alone settles — it falls to بلاغة, rhetorical analysis, to weigh whether one phrasing carries more emphasis than the other in its particular context. نحو only establishes that the drop is جَائِز, permissible, wherever a حرف جر directly precedes أَنْ or أَنَّ; it does not mandate the drop, and both phrasings remain fully correct Arabic.

## مواضع حذف حرف الجر سماعا

> **Rule Box**
>
> يَجُوزُ حَذْفُ حَرْفِ الجَرِّ سَمَاعًا

Beyond the regular قِيَاسًا rule tied to أَنْ/أَنَّ, حرف الجر can also be dropped in scattered positions known only from actual attested usage, سَمَاعًا, without a general rule producing them:

> **Example Box**
>
> ﴿إِنَّ عَادًا كَفَرُوا رَبَّهُمْ﴾
>
> Indeed, ʿĀd disbelieved in their Lord.

كَفَرَ ordinarily takes its object through الباء — ﴿وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِرَبِّهِمْ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ﴾, "and for those who disbelieve in their Lord is the punishment of Hell," shows the expected بِرَبِّهِمْ. In ﴿إِنَّ عَادًا كَفَرُوا رَبَّهُمْ﴾, the باء has simply been dropped, with no أَنْ or أَنَّ nearby to explain it by the قياسي rule — this drop is only known because it is attested in actual usage, not because a general principle predicts it.

## منصوب بنزع الخافض

> **Rule Box**
>
> حَذْفُ حَرْفِ الجَرِّ يُسَمَّى حَذْفًا وَإِيصَالًا، وَيُسَمَّى اتِّسَاعًا. وَيُسَمَّى المَجْرُورُ بَعْدَ حَذْفِ الجَارِّ مَنْصُوبًا بِنَزْعِ الخَافِضِ

This whole phenomenon — dropping the حرف الجر while its عمل survives — carries two established names.

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| حَذْفٌ وَإِيصَالٌ | "Dropping and connecting" — the sentence connects directly to the noun the حرف الجر would otherwise have introduced |
| اتِّسَاع | "Expansion" — Arabic tolerating a looser structure than the strict حرف-جر-plus-noun pattern would require |

Once the حرف الجر is dropped, the noun it used to govern does not simply lose its case marking — it shifts from مجرور to منصوب, taking on the same مَنْصُوب marking a direct مفعول به would carry. This specific kind of منصوب has its own name: مَنْصُوب بِنَزْعِ الخَافِض, "منصوب by the stripping-away of the خافض" — خَافِض being another term for حرف الجر itself, since it "lowers" its noun into جر. In ﴿إِنَّ عَادًا كَفَرُوا رَبَّهُمْ﴾, رَبَّهُمْ is exactly this: منصوب بنزع الخافض, standing in the place the مجرور بِرَبِّهِمْ would otherwise have occupied.
